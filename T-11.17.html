Split design · T-11.17

Heavy day, pump day and undulating weeks grow nothing extra

A heavy day and pump day split is a way of distributing the week. So is a week that changes load and reps from session to session (daily undulating periodisation). None of them out-grows plain straight sets matched for volume and effort. Run one for the variety, the adherence or the recovery spacing it buys a given client, never because she will grow faster on it.

What structuring the week does buy. Arranging load across a week helps maximal strength, through better coordination and fatigue management. It does not help growth. Growth comes from the weekly total of hard sets (hard set) and the tension inside them (mechanical tension), not from how the week is arranged. So a strength-path client can gain from a structured week. A muscle-building client gains nothing extra from it.

So heavy day/pump day is neither folklore nor a superior method. It is "no split is superior" in a more specific costume.

Watch for

Reaching for a heavy/pump split or an undulating model because a client has stalled. Nothing about the shape of the week caused the stall. Volume has the order to work through.

Terms on this page

daily undulating periodisation: Changing the load and the rep target from one session to the next inside the same week, heavy one day and lighter the next.

hard set: A working set taken within three reps of failure.

mechanical tension: The pulling force through a muscle as it works against a load. It is the main signal that tells a muscle to grow.
