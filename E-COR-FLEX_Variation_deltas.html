Variations

The GHD sit up is the stretched-range member of this family, not a harder crunch. Every other member loads the muscle short.

The four versions

# Crunch and decline crunch

sub: Body weight, short-range loading

What changes: The trunk curls from lying to fully flexed. The decline adds range and load on the way up.

# Machine ab crunch

sub: A crunch-family member on a machine

What changes: It loads the muscle from mid-range to shortened, like the rest of the family. The continuous-tension advantage belongs to the cable version specifically. Do not read it across to the machine.

# GHD sit up

sub: The stretched-range member

What changes: The trunk extends backwards under control before it flexes (lengthened position). It is the one member of the family that loads the muscle long. You will also hear it called the GHR sit-up. Same movement.

Do weighted crunches thicken the waist? No. The rectus abdominis runs vertically down the front of the body, and it works by bringing the ribs toward the pelvis. Its growth adds depth to the muscle, not width across the waist. Lifters who avoid ab training for this reason often find their abs look flat and lack pop once their body fat comes down. The muscle was never built.

What actually widens a waist

# Rapid off-season weight gain

sub: The abdominal wall stretched over time

Why: The wall is pushed out from inside and stays there.

# A deconditioned deep abdominal wall

sub: The transverse abdominis cannot pull the wall in

Why: The corset muscle under the six-pack is what holds the waist in. Untrained, it does not.

# Fat inside the abdomen, and organ enlargement

sub: Visceral fat; organ growth in lifters using performance-enhancing drugs

Why: Neither is a muscle and neither is fixed by avoiding crunches.

# Bloating

sub: Specific foods

Why: A gut problem, not a training one.

Waist trainers are contraindicated outright. They do not train the muscle. They encourage the wall to relax instead of engaging, and they risk compressing the gut.

When she is scared to load her abs

No, weighted ab work will not thicken your waist. The muscle grows deeper, not wider. If your waist has changed, the reasons are on a different list, and the crunch is not on it.

Terms on this page

lengthened position: The point in the rep where the working muscle is at its longest while it carries the load. It describes the muscle's length, not whether she feels a stretch.
