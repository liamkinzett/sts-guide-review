Setup

Set it up in this order

Pick the knee angle against the job before anything else. Straight or only slightly bent keeps the gastrocnemius in the movement. Bent is the seated variation. What it's for has the reason.

Set the load against the range being worked. Lighter for the fully shortened end, heavier for the stretched end. Loading explains why one load will not do.

Choose the STS equipment. The machine standing calf raise and the machine seated calf raise are the two STS calf machines. Barefoot on a block is the setup for the pre-squat version only.

Full range, both ends, before the first rep. With the load you chose, check she reaches the full stretch at the bottom. Then check she reaches the full squeeze at the top.

You will hear two other machines preferred. One is a hip-loaded sled raise, with the pads on the hips rather than the shoulders. The other is a leg-press calf raise, balls of the feet at the bottom of the footplate. STS programs neither. The two machines above are the STS calf raises.
