Screen readiness before any deficit, and be willing to lose her

Screen whether she is ready before any deficit starts. Where she is not, the diet does not start, and you accept that she may leave over it.

A client who is under-eating is not in a positive metabolic situation. Her calories are not high enough to warrant putting her in a deficit (deficit). Say so as a plain statement of the situation. It is not a negotiation and it is not softened.

The refusal, delivered plainly

You're not ready to diet. We are not in a positive metabolic situation. Your cals are not high enough for me to warrant putting you in a deficit.

The cost is named and accepted. If she does not like that, she leaves, and the coach who says it is perfectly okay with that. Be willing to lose her over it.

Why the screen is every time, not occasional. The base rate is the reason. Every single client that came to that coach had been under-eating. Assume yours has too until her intake says otherwise. The female body covers the signs of under-fuelling and what to refer.

What you do not do. You do not set her calories. Anything eaten belongs to the Nutrition Manual. What you own here is the decision that a deficit does not start, and the conversation that tells her why.

Watch for

Starting the deficit because she asked for it. She is paying for objectivity. A friend would say yes.

Terms on this page

deficit: Eating fewer calories than she burns, so that she loses body fat.
