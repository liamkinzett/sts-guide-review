Fuelling a breastfeeding client

A breastfeeding client is fuelled and trained strong. She is not a client to lean out.

This is the under-fuelling rule applied in the situation where it is most likely and least suspected. Under-fuelling (low energy availability) means she is not eating enough for what she trains and the rest of what her body needs. A breastfeeding body needs a great deal.

The demand is higher than pregnancy. She needs more calories breastfeeding than she did in the third trimester. Use the direction with her. Do not quote it to her as a number.

The ask you will get is the wrong one. Leaning out while breastfeeding is close to a universal request. The answer is the same every time. Just focus on the strength. Literally just fuel yourself. Make the focal point the strength and the aesthetics will follow, and they'll be more solid too.

Why it works. Under-fuelling is a coach's problem to notice. A breastfeeding client running a deficit is the clearest case of it you will meet. Do not under-fuel her. It will absolutely bite. Recovery varies between women, and the whole period is a marathon, not a sprint.

Say it like this

Just focus on the strength. Literally just fuel yourself. Make the strength your focal point and the look will follow, and it'll be more solid too.

Verdict

The six-week postpartum medical check is non-negotiable.

Attending it is not yours to waive.

Watch for

Body image. It is the thing clients struggle with most postpartum, alongside the plain logistics of eating enough to both train and feed. The request to cut is usually about that and not about hunger.

What this page does not cover. The return timeline, the week-by-week progression, load and caesarean recovery live on the postpartum return pages. This page is the fuelling half.

Terms on this page
