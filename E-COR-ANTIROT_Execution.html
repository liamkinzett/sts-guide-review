How to do it

Pallof press: she presses the cable straight forward and resists its pull. The trunk does not rotate. Then she returns the handle to the chest under control and goes again.

Bird dog. She drives the heel back and produces real hip extension, then holds the contraction for the time on How long she holds. The limbs do not simply point. The position is held under deliberate tension.

The named failure of the drill is coaching it without that hold. A coach who has her point the leg and the arm, with no timed contraction and no cueing, is the reason. That is why people say remedial holds do not work. The exercise is not what failed.
