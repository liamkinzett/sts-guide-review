Re-tether a scale-fixated client to different data

For a client fixated on the scale, re-tether her to different data. Do not remove the data. Substitution, never confiscation.

A client who wants numbers is given better numbers. Labs, blood pressure, strength on the bar. Give her metrics she can see improve, and while she is focused on those, body weight comes off and fitness goes up in the background.

Closing the loop

If you focus on the training and the diet I give you, the weight will take care of itself.

The other move is to keep the scale and re-teach it. The scale gets demonised, but it can give her a lot of good feedback if she knows what she is looking for. It is not for dictating weight loss or gain. It is for day-to-day trends.

Compared

columns: Re-tether | Re-teach

The scale: Stays in her hands, stops being the headline | Stays in her hands, read as a trend

What she watches: Performance, labs, blood pressure | The weekly average, not the daily number

Use it when: She has no other source of good news | She can read a number without distress

Verdict

Neither move is a scale break. If she cannot look at the number without distress, refer.

That is past the referral line, not past this page.

Motivation and adherence makes the same move for a different client, one who simply has no other source of good news. This page is for the client whose relationship with the number is going wrong. The female body adds the case after menopause, where the scale can hold steady while composition changes underneath it.

Watch for

Taking the scale away. That is confiscation. Change what she is measuring and leave the scale where it is.
