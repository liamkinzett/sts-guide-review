Mobility and stretching · T-16b.4

Under twenty minutes a week changes nothing structural

Stretching totalling under 20 minutes a week, kept up for less than 8 weeks, is nowhere near enough to change the tissue. Almost every client routine sits below that floor.

Key figure

value: 20 minutes

unit: of stretching a week, the structural floor

note: Below it nothing in the tissue changes. Real remodelling needs one to two hours a day for six weeks.

What the dose that changes tissue actually looks like. Genuine structural remodelling takes one to two hours of daily stretching over six weeks, usually with a board or a brace holding the position. Those gains reverse quickly once the stimulus stops. Ten minutes a few evenings a week is a typical genuine effort from a client. It is less than half the floor.

When you may deviate. The dose that does change tissue exists. If a client genuinely needs a structural change, it is available. She does one to two hours daily with a board or brace for six weeks. In practice that is rehabilitation, or a joint stiffened short after injury, rather than physique work. That is why the default treats the figure as a reason to stop selling small doses, not as a target to program toward.

Watch for

Prescribing a routine below that floor and expecting a structural result from it. The dose is not a small version of the effective dose. It is below the threshold entirely.
