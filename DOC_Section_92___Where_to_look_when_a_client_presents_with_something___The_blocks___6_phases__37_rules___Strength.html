Strength: the standards

About twelve weeks, and only the last four are the peak. It ends in a test, then a deload.

Rule means always. Default means the usual choice, changed only for a reason you can name.

The standardHow firm

Test toward a 5RM, 3RM or 1RM.Default

Judge a specialisation block on the target muscle's own logbook, not on a week count.Default
Accumulation and intensification are a real structure for a client peaking toward a date. Borrowed onto a physique-only block, they are cosplay.Default
A client who skips or PRs through her deloads is not withholding buy-in. She is being asked to agree with a self-assessment she cannot make. Prescribe the week rather than negotiating it.Default
A strength phase has no separate readiness gate. It inherits priming's exit criteria, read on the specific patterns the block will load.Default
