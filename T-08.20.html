Why her numbers dip on a diet

Her numbers hold or dip a little in a cut because three things were turned down on purpose. She has not got weaker. Her top set felt easy last month. This month the same weight is a grind, or has come down. That is the plan working.

  • Effort tightened. Her reps in reserve (RIR) floor sits higher when she is eating less than she burns (deficit), from wherever the block started.
  • Volume dropped. Weekly sets fall in a deficit regardless of training age, because the job is retention rather than growth.
  • Fatigue-buying techniques came out entirely. Drop sets, rest-pause, myo-reps and stretch-shortening work are removed. A deficit is exactly the context where the fatigue they buy is hardest to recover from.

    • Stack those three and the training stimulus has been reduced on purpose. That is before counting any cost of the deficit itself. A held or slightly falling number here is not a sign of a failing program. Look the current figures up in the volume table and in The phases. Do not assume a client's felt sense of "easy" from a higher-volume, higher-effort month still applies to this one.

    Why it works. "Felt easy" measured last month's effort, not this month's capacity. Last month her sets ran closer to failure, so her top numbers were higher. This month she stops a rep or two earlier by design. Take away the extra sets and the intensity techniques as well, and the same load is bound to feel and perform differently. Her capacity has not changed. The way she is being asked to use it has.

    Say it like this

    Your number's not dropping because you're getting weaker. Your program's running less volume and slightly less effort on purpose because right now the job is holding what you've built, not adding to it. A held number in a cut is the plan working.

    Terms on this page

    RIR: Reps in reserve. How many more reps she could have done when the set stopped. Zero RIR is failure.

    deficit: Eating fewer calories than she burns, so that she loses body fat. Recovery is reduced and the training job becomes holding muscle.
