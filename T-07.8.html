Effort runs from 3 RIR to 0 across the block

Effort climbs across a hypertrophy block, from 3 reps in reserve in week one to 0 in the final week. Reps in reserve (RIR) is how many reps she has left when the set stops. Only one week sits at 0.

Effort across a six-week hypertrophy block

3 RIR | 1 | Week 1. Three reps left in every set

2 RIR | 1 | Week 2

1 RIR | 3 | Weeks 3 to 5. Compounds stay here for the rest of the block

0 RIR | 1 | Week 6. Isolation work goes to failure. Compounds hold at 1

The final week is the only one at true failure, and true failure means isolation work. Single-joint exercises (isolation) go to 0. Multi-joint lifts (compound) hold at 1 RIR through the zero week and never go below it. A squat taken to failure (true failure) buys nothing the last clean rep did not already buy. It costs recovery, and it costs technique on the lift that matters most. Effort and intensity has the reasoning.

The table assumes a client who can rate her own effort. A beginner cannot. So the zero week is not written for her literally. She is prescribed below 3 RIR on supported work (externally stabilised) and held at 2-3 RIR on work she stabilises herself (internally stabilised). She lands where the table intends without ever being told to reach failure. Her first program has the detail.

A second week at 0 is possible. It is not something she earns by being advanced. Run it only where all three of these hold.

Check

Is her recovery in good order?

Is she still adding load at 0 RIR, rather than grinding through it?

Is the deload straight after already locked in?

yes: A second week at 0 can make sense.

no: One week at 0 is the answer.

Terms on this page

RIR: Reps in reserve. How many more reps she could have done when the set stopped. 2 RIR means she stopped two reps short of failure.

isolation: An exercise that works one muscle across one joint, like a leg extension or a cable curl. Nothing else can take the set over.

compound: An exercise that moves several joints and loads several muscles at once, like a squat, a deadlift, a press or a row.

true failure: The point where she tries to complete another rep and cannot move it. 0 RIR.

externally stabilised: The machine, bench or support holds her position so she does not have to. Leg press, hack squat, chest-supported row, cable work.

internally stabilised: Her own trunk and bracing hold the position under load. Back squat, deadlift, barbell bench, standing overhead press.
