Watch for

  • The shoulder over a long range is the named vulnerability. The caution is about load, not about range. The range is the point of the exercise.
  • Thoracic spine. The movement is deliberately used to open the upper back (thoracic spine) and free up shoulder mobility. That makes the stretched pause a mobility instruction as well as a size one. Tempo and pauses owns the duration.
  • It qualifies her for other work. Visible lat control on a cable pullover sits before barbell hinging. This movement gates other lifts rather than needing a gate itself. See Glutes for the deadlift.
  • Where it sits in a shoulder-mobility ladder. A loaded cable-pullover stretch first. Then a dead bar hang or a single-arm cable pullover as the drill. Then a single-arm pulldown, then a landmine or Viking press. The pullover occupies the first two rungs. STS doesn't program these rungs as exercises; use the ladder to place her.
  • Who to modify for. Restricted overhead range scales by how far the arms travel behind the head. Lower-back sensitivity under an overhead load takes the glute-braced bridge and a lighter dumbbell. Pain itself is Pain rules and when to refer. Anything about her being a woman is The female body.
    • Terms on this page

    thoracic spine: The upper and middle back, the part of the spine the ribs attach to. It is the part of the spine that has to extend for the arms to get overhead cleanly.
