Practising a lift · T-48.5

Her movement is different every day, and that is normal

Her coordination is in a different place every single day she walks into the gym. It will never be the same twice. That is how the system works, not a fault in her.

Some days the first squat feels awful. The answer to "I forgot how to squat" is that she did not. Nothing has been lost. Her output that day is simply different, and the fix is to warm up into it rather than to diagnose it.

Warming up into it beats diagnosing it. Experienced lifters learn what they can ignore. A first rep that feels terrible does not need 20 minutes of unrelated drills. It needs more of the lift itself, working up a little slower than usual. Warming up covers the warm-up.

Where the line is. A bad day sits inside one session. A pattern that has genuinely gone backwards shows across weeks. If it persists from week to week, that is no longer this rule. Progression covers a stall.

Watch for

Changing the program because of one bad session. A single session changes nothing on its own. A week is not a trend.

A first rep that feels wrong being treated as a technique fault. Let her work up to it slower before you call anything.

Say when she says she has forgotten how to lift

You haven't lost it. Some days the first few reps feel off. Work up a bit slower than usual and it will come back.
