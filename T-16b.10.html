Mobility and stretching · T-16b.10

Stretching is not training and never enters the volume count

Stretching is not a set. It does not count toward the weekly volume in Volume, and it does not substitute for a session.

Why it works. Static stretching builds no muscle. That holds at up to 4.5 minutes a session and 36 minutes a week, kept up for 24 weeks. A stretching session written in place of a training session is not a small dose of training. It is a different activity, and the week is one session short.

When you may deviate. This rule is about unloaded stretching done as a routine. A muscle held under load at length inside a working set is a training method rather than a stretch. Volume owns how any set is counted. This topic does not rule on that.

Watch for

A light week where a stretching session is written in place of a training session and the week is still counted as complete. It is not a small dose of training. It is a different activity.
