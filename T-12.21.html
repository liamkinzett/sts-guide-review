Building a session · T-12.21

"A bench session" means the strength path

When a client or a coach names a competition lift (competition lift) as the session, she is on the strength path. "A bench session", "a squat session", "a deadlift day" or "SBD", squat, bench and deadlift, all name the powerlifting-strength emphasis path. Build the session to serve the lift. The strength system's own topic owns that context.

Two consequences.

  • The direct arm work rule does not gate the accessories here. That rule governs direct arm work as muscle-building volume for a female client. On the strength path, accessory selection serves the press, and triceps work is normal push-day accessory work.
  • "A bench session" is not "a chest session". The target is the lift, not the muscle.

    • A muscle-building request is the other path, unchanged. "Build my chest", "an upper day" or "more glutes" route to Split design and Volume. Assessing a new client explains the three paths.

    Terms on this page

    competition lift: One of the three lifts tested at a powerlifting meet: the squat, the bench press and the deadlift.
