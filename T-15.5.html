Eccentric two to three seconds, longer while she learns

Two to three seconds on the eccentric is the standard for everything unless another page says otherwise. Three to four seconds applies while she is learning a movement pattern, or where there is a reason for caution about a joint.

Key figure

value: 2–3

unit: seconds on the lowering phase

note: 3 to 4 while she is learning the pattern or a joint needs care. Never past 4.

Why it works. Two to three seconds is long enough to remove momentum and short enough that the set is not dominated by the descent. A 2 second descent on a leg press is controlled. A 3 second descent on a Romanian deadlift keeps the hamstrings loaded the whole way down. The learning range is longer for a different reason. A slower descent gives her time to feel the position, which is about how she learns the movement, not about growth.

What it looks like. A client in her second week on the hack squat gets a descent of up to 4 seconds. She is still finding the depth and the knee track. Once she owns the pattern, it comes back to two or three. The longer descent was teaching, not training.

Terms on this page

eccentric: The lowering half of a rep, where the muscle lengthens under load. The lowering phase is the first digit of the tempo code.
