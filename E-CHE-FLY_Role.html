Chest · E-CHE-FLY#Role

What it's for

The pec fly is the single-joint chest movement. It exists to cover the range pressing cannot reach. It trains the chest at a fully stretched position (lengthened position). Because it is cable-loaded or machine-loaded, it also challenges the fully squeezed position (shortened position) safely. Presses give nothing at the squeezed end. This is the movement that fills that gap.

The resistance is continuous across the whole range. Unlike a press, there is no point where the load falls away. On a cable the peak can be placed at the squeezed end, which is the argument for cables and pec decks over dumbbells. The peak is machine-dependent. On a pec deck the cam puts the hardest point at the stretched end instead. Check the machine in front of her rather than assume it.

It is the only chest movement STS programs that loads both ends, stretched and squeezed.

Its slot. After the presses, for the range they miss. It is also the named warm-up ahead of a provocative press: fly first, then the press runs at a lower absolute load. Load stays moderate by design. A long lever with extended arms makes every kilo far harder than on a bent-arm press. That is a property of the movement, not a weakness in the client.

Terms on this page

lengthened position: The point in the rep where the working muscle is at its longest while it carries the load. It describes the muscle's length, not whether she feels a stretch.

shortened position: The muscle is working while it is bunched short. On a fly that is hands together in front of the chest.
