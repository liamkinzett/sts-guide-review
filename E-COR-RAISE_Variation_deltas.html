Variations

Choose the captain's chair when the grip or the shoulder is the reason, not because it is easier. The two versions do the same trunk work.

Compared

columns: Hanging knee raise | Captain's chair knee raise

Adds: An overhead shoulder demand and a grip demand on top of the trunk work | Nothing. The same trunk work with the grip demand removed

Choose against it when: The shoulder sits in an unstable environment under load, and hers does not tolerate that | You want the shoulder work that hanging gives her

Progression: Knees to straight legs | Knees to straight legs

Load is not on the ladder for either. A dumbbell held between the ankles shifts tension to the hip flexors. The tell is a pinch in the hip crease rather than the lower abs.

If the ab wheel rollout is her rung and she cannot hold the brace even at a short range, regress inside the movement. Shorten the range rather than change the exercise. Roll out only as far as the brace holds and stop the rep the instant it does not.

If she cannot hold that either, drop her back to a front plank hold at whatever duration she can brace. Shorten the lever and end the set on position loss rather than on the clock. Then return to the rollout at a shortened range.
