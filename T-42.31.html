The coaching relationship · T-42.31

"Am I wasting your time" is a question about the goal

"Am I wasting your time" is a question about the goal, wearing the clothes of a question about herself. Answer the one underneath. Reassurance answers a question she did not ask.

The tool is the open-ended question about the goal. Turned back onto her question, that means something close to the standard opener. Are you happy with where you're at. Not a verdict about her worth as a client.

Answering the question underneath

Let's leave my time out of it for a second. Are you happy with where you're at?

Why reassurance fails. "Of course not" ends the conversation with nothing learned. The question she asked was about herself. The question she meant was about whether this is working, and only she can answer that.

Where it goes if the answer is no. She may supply the answer herself: the fit is not working. Then ending it is a legitimate outcome rather than a failure on either side. The question deserves a straight run at the goal before it goes anywhere near the relationship's ending.

Watch for

Hearing it as a fishing for compliments. Sometimes it is. Answer the goal question anyway. It costs nothing if she is fine and it is the only route in if she is not.
