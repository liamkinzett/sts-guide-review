Knee travelling inside the foot at the deadlift setup

Start with a narrower stance, or turn the hips out, so the knees stay in line with the toes. The A-frame is a setup fault and it is fixed at the setup.

Knee travelling inside the foot on the deadlift

# The A-frame at the setup

sub: Setup

What you see: Her knees point in toward each other before the bar moves, like the letter A.

Do this: Narrow the stance, or rotate the hips outward (external rotation), until the knees track the line of the toes. One or the other; you do not need both.

Exercises: Conventional deadlift

Terms on this page

external rotation: Turning a limb outward in its socket: the upper arm at the shoulder, so the palm turns to face forward or up, or the thigh at the hip, so the knee points more to the side.
