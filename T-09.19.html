Effort and intensity · T-09.19

The rep range does not change with her goal

Cutting and bulking do not get different rep bands. The implement sets the range, and nothing about that reads her goal. A dumbbell movement is 6 to 15 in a deficit for the same reason it is 6 to 15 in a surplus. The implement did not change.

What actually changes in a deficit is elsewhere. Weekly volume comes down, and the reps-in-reserve floor tightens to one. Volume and The phases own those. Both protect the tissue against a lower food intake. Neither one touches the rep band.

Why it works. "High reps to cut, low reps to bulk" describes a real difference in programming and misplaces it. The difference is volume and effort, not the number of reps in a set. Moving the rep band to chase a goal loses the loadability the implement was chosen for. It fixes nothing the volume and effort adjustments were not already fixing.

Say it like this

The reps you're doing on a movement don't change because you're cutting. What changes is how much of it we do and how close to failure we take it.
