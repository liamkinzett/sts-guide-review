When to use it

The problem you notice. Her session budget is tight and something in it has to pay at two sets. Or her arms have no thickness, no line between biceps and triceps. Or she is new enough that a free curl breaks down before she gets near a real effort. The standing curl is the default curl for all three. It is the mid-range mass builder for the muscles that bend the elbow (elbow flexors).

Reach for it when

# The session budget is tight and something has to pay at two sets

sub: Read it off the session plan

Confirm it: Count the slots. If the biceps slot is getting two sets, it needs a movement that pays at two.

What it gets her: Real work out of a small slot. Lower-skill, single-joint work held by a fixed position pays at two sets. Bicep curls, calf raises and the leg press are that kind of work. An RDL or a split squat is much higher skill, asks much more of her own stability, and needs more sets.

# She has no thickness or separation between biceps and triceps

sub: Or she is running a reverse curl as a slot of its own

Confirm it: Look at the arm from the side. The line between biceps and triceps is missing.

What it gets her: The brachialis and the brachioradialis trained in one movement (brachialis) (brachioradialis). The hammer and neutral grips bias both. That is why a reverse curl is redundant rather than a third lever. A more neutral grip on a standing or preacher curl is the same lever.

# She is new enough that a free curl breaks down before she reaches a real effort

sub: Her form goes before her biceps do

Confirm it: Watch her last two reps. If the body swings and the elbows drift before the reps slow, the curl is a skill exercise for her.

What it gets her: A slot she can actually take close to failure once the technique holds. Whether a movement is a skill exercise is decided per client, not per exercise. Somebody new to the gym may not be able to train a bicep curl to a relative failure point without technique breaking down first. For her, even a machine curl is a skill exercise. Coach the rep first.

What this page will not give you

Expect no load off the bottom. Gravity passes straight through the elbow there, so the turning force at the joint is zero (torque). The stretched range is unloaded by definition. If the long head at length is what is missing (long head), this is the wrong page. See Lengthened-position curl.

Screen before you prescribe any two-arm dumbbell curl

Arms straight forward, then turned outward as far as she can (external rotation). If the elbow does not stay in line with the wrist and shoulder, curling with both arms at once is contraindicated. She works single-arm.

Forcing it puts the hand on a diagonal while the load falls straight down. That mismatch is the named cause of medial elbow pain in curls. The taller and wider she is, the likelier single-arm is required.

Single-arm is the standard. The alternating curl is not the single-arm version. Setup explains.

Setup. Stand tall, feet hip width, blades set down and back, elbow level with the torso line. Setup has the full list.

Terms on this page

elbow flexors: The muscles that bend the elbow, mainly the biceps and the brachialis underneath it. Curls train them directly and pulling trains them indirectly.

brachialis: The muscle under the biceps that bends the elbow. It carries arm thickness and the separation between biceps and triceps.

brachioradialis: The muscle on the thumb side of the upper forearm that bends the elbow. A hammer or neutral grip puts more work on it.

torque: The turning force on a joint. The further the load sits from the joint, the more of it there is. It is the force times its moment arm, so the same force can produce very different torque at different joint angles.

long head: The head of a muscle that crosses the shoulder joint as well as the elbow: on the triceps it only stretches with the arm raised, on the biceps only with the arm behind the body.

external rotation: Turning a limb outward in its socket: the upper arm at the shoulder, so the palm turns to face forward or up, or the thigh at the hip, so the knee points more to the side. On the screen it is what she does with the arms held straight out in front.
