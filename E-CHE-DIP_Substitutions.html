Swaps

Two ladders. The first is for a client who cannot yet hit the standard. The second is for a provocative shoulder.

She cannot yet hit the standard, or the bars are taken

# An assisted dip machine

sub: Bodyweight turned down below gravity

Why: It lets a beginner express the joint position without guarding, instead of cutting range to cope. Among the best pieces of equipment made. Where the gym has one, use it.

# A band-assisted dip

sub: The same idea with a band

Why: A band under the knees or feet takes weight off at the bottom. Where the gym has no assisted machine.

# A dip machine

sub: Seated, pressing handles down

Why: Good for teaching the basic mechanics because it can be underloaded. It fits tall and long-armed lifters badly.

A provocative shoulder: stay in the joint position, take the load off it

# A decline press or bridge press

sub: Similar shoulder extension, less load on the joint

Why: Both still reach similar positions of shoulder extension.

# A horizontal dumbbell press

sub: In the plane where the upper arm clears the top of the shoulder

Swap to: Flat DB bench press

Why: Pressed in the shoulder-blade plane Region by region governs, where the upper arm clears the bony roof of the shoulder (acromion).

# A cross-body cable triceps extension

sub: Exposure to the joint position, not a triceps exercise

Why: Used to expose her to the position. That exposure is what carries over to the dip.

# Passive-loaded shoulder extension

sub: Scaled by how far she steps away from the cable

Swap to: Incline DB curl

Why: The arm hangs behind the body under load. The incline DB curl is the STS form of it.

Which ladder, for which problem

# Equipment missing, or she is not yet strong enough

sub: The first ladder

Why: Alter the body angle or use the assisted machine to keep the movement elbow-friendly and shoulder-friendly.

# A provocative shoulder

sub: The second ladder

Why: Then Region by region, shoulder.

# Home

sub: No gym access

Why: STS doesn't coach home training, so there is no home swap to give.

Terms on this page

acromion: The bony roof at the top of the shoulder blade that sits over the shoulder joint. Pressing where the upper arm clears it avoids pinching.
