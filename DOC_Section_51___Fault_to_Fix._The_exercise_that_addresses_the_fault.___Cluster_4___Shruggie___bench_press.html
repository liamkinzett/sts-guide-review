Shruggie on the bench: load a dip

A loaded dip variation. The lower trap's job is to stop the shoulder blades rising, and the dip loads that job through a range nobody lacks. If she cannot hold the dip position, grade it down the ladder.

Her shoulders climb toward her ears on pressing, flyes or dips (shruggie).

Shruggie on the bench

# Traps riding up

sub: Accessory: a loaded dip

Why it works: The lower trap does not pull the blade down for its own sake. It stops the shoulder blades from elevating. A dip is where that job is loaded.

Do this: Program a loaded dip variation. The dip page carries the standard and its own faults.

# She cannot hold the position the dip demands

sub: Regression: the graded ladder

Do this: Bodyweight dip. Below that, a bench dip with hips and knees at 90 degrees. Below that, an assisted dip machine with the pin at the bottom. At the bottom of the ladder, simply holding the arms at the side with a stacked rib cage. Grade the exposure to the arm-behind-the-body position to her tolerance. She is weak exactly where she should be strong, and the pin at the bottom is the point.
