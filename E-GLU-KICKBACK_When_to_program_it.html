Glutes · E-GLU-KICKBACK#When to program it

When to use it

The problem you notice. She is on three glute sessions a week and the third has to be recoverable. Or you want to isolate the glute and were about to distort a compound lift to do it. Or she is already doing kickbacks as a rushed pump movement. The kickback is the side dish in all three, never the meal.

Reach for it when

# She is on three glute sessions a week

sub: The third one has to be recoverable

Do this: Two loaded glute days, then a third day of shortened-range work. Maybe a 45 degree hip extension and a kickback, and that's the glutes for the day. Easy to recover from, but enough to actually stimulate something.

Why: This is one way a third day can look. It is not a rule that a third day must look like this.

# You want to isolate the glute

sub: And you were about to distort a compound lift to do it

Do this: Kickbacks. If we want to isolate a muscle we get into a machine or a cable setup. Kickbacks are fine; they're great. Keep the compound lift honest and put the bias here.

Why: The main thing needs to be the main thing.

# She is already doing them as a pump movement

sub: Rushed, half range, back arching

Do this: Keep the kickback and organise it. Rib cage stacked over pelvis, reaching true hip extension (hip extension). STS doesn't remove kickbacks; it organises them.

Check

Does her program already hold a hip thrust and a 45 degree hip extension?

yes: A kickback makes it short position, short position, short position. Don't add it.

no: It can go in as the shortened-range touch point.

Keep it in its place

It is the seasoning, not the meal. A side dish, or the seasoning on top.

A program that is exclusively kickbacks and five different cable lateral raise variations is a failure. Fix it in two ways. Challenge her in the lengthened range, and take her closer to failure.

Not a sport-transfer tool. A cable-resisted kickback for a kicker is mimicry, like shooting free throws with a medicine ball.

Same low-back diagnostic as the 45 degree hip extension. If she is feeling her low back, she is probably moving too much through there.

Terms on this page

hip extension: Opening the hip so the thigh travels behind the body line. It is the glute's main job, and a kickback reaches the end of it one leg at a time.
