Warming up · T-16.6

A feeder set never tires her

The ramp exists to make the working sets better. The moment it starts costing reps from the first working set it has stopped doing its job and started competing with it.

Why it works. This is the failure mode of every long warm-up. A coach adds sets to be thorough. The client arrives at her first working set already three sets deep. The session's actual stimulus is then delivered by a tired lifter. The volume total in Volume never sees it, because feeders are not working sets and nobody logs them as volume. The fatigue is real and invisible on paper.

What it looks like. Last week she squatted 100kg for five clean reps. This week her ramp added two extra sets at 85kg and 90kg, and the 100kg set stalls at three. Nothing about her changed. The ramp took the reps.

Watch for

Feeders creeping toward the working weight. A feeder at 90% of the working load is a working set that has been mislabelled. If the ramp tired her, it was not a ramp.

Terms on this page

feeder set: A light set done before the working sets to rehearse the movement and find the day's load. It is never counted as volume.

ramp: The run of feeder sets that climbs toward the first working set.
