Assessing a new client: what to say

Words for her on assessing a new client, by situation.

A script is only right inside its rule's conditions. Read the rule in Assessing a new client before you use the words.

Her goal needs both a build phase and a cut phase.

Say it like this

What you're after needs both a build phase and a cut phase. I'd recommend we cut first. It keeps your body fat easier to manage through the build which usually means we can build for longer. You also tend to feel better about how you look the whole way through. So we prime, then cut, then build from there.

If she has told you she wants to build, build.

Say it like this

Perfect. We're building. We'll prime first so I can see how you move and how you recover, then we get into it properly.

She feels a second cut is going backwards.

Say it like this

I know it feels like we're going backwards into a cut. We're not. You're coming into this one stronger than you started the last one, with better technique and more muscle underneath. Every lap around this thing puts you further ahead than the last.

You suspect the goal she gave you belongs to someone else.

Say it like this

Can I ask, is this something you want, or something you're doing for someone else? I ask because it changes what I build, and I'd rather build for the person actually in the room.

Her timeline is unrealistic. Work it back from where she is now before you answer.

Say it like this

I've worked the timeline back from where you actually are right now. That date doesn't get you there safely. I'd rather tell you that today than build toward it and have both of us find out later. Here's what the real number looks like, and here's what we can do with the time you do have.
