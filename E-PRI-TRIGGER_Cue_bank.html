Cues

This page gives a technique standard rather than a cue set. Two cues exist. Do not manufacture more to fill the space.

Cues

When: She is travelling along the muscle

Say: rolling like a pizza

Why: External cue, used as the negative. Naming the wrong thing is the whole cue. Active pin and stretch, not rolling like a pizza.

When: Pinned on a tender point

Say: heel to the butt

Why: External cue. The active half of pin and stretch at each tender point (pin and stretch). Follow it with a lock-out and emphasis on the inner-knee quad (VMO).

Terms on this page

pin and stretch: Hold pressure still on one tender point while she moves the joint next to it through its range.

VMO: The teardrop quad muscle on the inside of the knee. It works hardest in the last few degrees of straightening the knee.
