When to use it

The problem you notice. Her knee is achy and the session still has to load the quad. Or one leg is visibly behind the other and you want a number, not an impression. Or she has been told extensions will wreck her knees. The leg extension straightens the knee against resistance with the hip out of the movement, and no other exercise on these pages does that.

Reach for it when

# Her knee is achy and the session still has to load the quad

sub: The standard knee-pain block: hamstring curl, step-down, then the extension

Confirm it: She reports the ache, and a full pain-free rep may or may not be available. Pain itself is Pain rules and when to refer.

What it gets her: The quad loaded at an angle she can tolerate.

Do this: If a full pain-free rep is not available, the answer is a hold (isometric), not a shortened pain-free arc. Hold it halfway up and load it for sets of 30 seconds. The ladder is in Region by region.

# She is in rehab, or she is an athlete who has been told extensions wreck knees

sub: The objection answered and the quad loaded anyway

Confirm it: She raises the worry, or someone has raised it for her.

What it gets her: Quad extensions are not bad for performance for an athlete. In a rehab setting they are one of the best exercises available, and there is nothing wrong with a quad extension. They are preferred over terminal knee extensions because they are more loadable.

# One leg is visibly behind the other

sub: You want a number instead of an impression

Confirm it: Test each leg alone on the machine and write the weights down.

What it gets her: A side-to-side strength gap you can write down. A worked case reads 45 lb on the right against 85 on the left, alongside a step-up rep count on a 12-inch box. That is the objective half of an assessment, not a prescription. The plan that follows it is holds below the painful joint angle, working through limited range and building capacity over months.

# She is late in a prep and the instinct is to write a whole new program

sub: The same stimulus for less fatigue and less mental effort

Confirm it: She is deep in a deficit and tired, and the program is about to change for no reason.

What it gets her: The stimulus that built the tissue is the stimulus that keeps it. The quad extension is a named late-prep swap. Similar patterns that feel good, not forty new exercises.

One flavour, chosen for a reason

Leg extensions three ways is redundant. Banded terminal knee extensions, banded leg extensions, leg-extension holds and leg extensions in one program is waste specific to this machine.

Squat, then leg press, then leg extension is doubling down on the same position. Pick the extension for one of the reasons above, not to fill a template.

Terms on this page

isometric: A hold. The muscle works against the load but the joint does not move.
