Hamstring: run both curl variations

Program the seated curl one day and the lying curl another, deliberately. They load the hamstring at different lengths and are not interchangeable.

Why. The seated curl keeps the hip bent and works the hamstring near its longest (lengthened position). The lying curl keeps the hip straight and works it bunched short (shortened position). Hamstrings owns selection and the detail.

A tool for a knee progression. The single-leg kneeling hamstring curl is one of the best tools available when a knee is being rebuilt. It limits load and forces her to control her lower back and pelvis to produce hamstring force.

Terms on this page

lengthened position: The point in the rep where the working muscle is at its longest while it carries the load. It describes the muscle's length, not whether she feels a stretch.

shortened position: The muscle is working while it is bunched short.
