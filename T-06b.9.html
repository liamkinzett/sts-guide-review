Her first program · T-06b.9

When she asks to see the whole twelve weeks, explain why program one is provisional

The answer to "can I see the whole plan" is that block two has not been written, because it cannot be written yet. Program one exists to find out who she is. Growth is a by-product. Her classification for each movement pattern is still provisional. What block two contains depends on what block one's footage and logbook show.

Why a full plan would be false confidence. You have not yet earned the right to write twelve weeks. Showing her a plan anyway is worse than declining to. It commits you to decisions before the information exists to make them properly. A plan that gets rewritten in week five reads as the coach not knowing what she was doing. In fact it is the system working as designed.

Say it like this

I don't write the whole twelve weeks up front. Here's why. Your first block is how I find out how you actually move, recover and progress. It's not a formality. Once I've seen that, block two gets built on real information instead of a guess. I could hand you a plan today. It would be a guess dressed up as a schedule.

Watch for

Reading her request as impatience to manage rather than a fair question to answer. She is asking a reasonable thing. The real answer is that the plan is being built responsibly, not withheld.
