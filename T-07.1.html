The phases · T-07.1

Priming ends when she is ready, not at four weeks

Priming is a readiness gate, not a training phase. It ends when she is ready, not when four weeks have passed.

Key figure

value: 4 weeks

unit: minimum, with no upper limit

note: Run it longer for as long as the exit criteria are unmet

There is no version of this where she moves on because the calendar says so. If the exit criteria are still unmet at week six, she is still in priming.

What sits inside priming

Priming | Four weeks minimum. It ends when all five exit criteria are met

The split | The training days she will keep, covering every pattern you need to see

The exercises | Machines and supported movements carry the early load

The footage | Every clip she sends comes back with feedback, and feeds the movement screen

Why it works. Everything after priming assumes things about her. That she can execute the patterns. That she recovers on a predictable timeline. That she will complete what you write. Priming is where you find out whether any of that is true, while the cost of being wrong is still low.

Watch for

Treating a technically proficient client's four weeks as time to fill. The gate is about readiness rather than attendance. If she clears it in four, move on.
