Cues

Cues for the 45 degree hip extension go in the order printed. Try the first, and only move on when it fails.

Cues

When: At the top, before anything else

Say: When I get to this neutral position, that's when I'm done.

Why: The stopping rule said as a cue. External. Try this one first, because the most common way this movement stops training the glutes is by not ending.

When: She reaches the top and the squeeze is soft

Say: Squeeze your glutes hard in the fully shortened position.

Why: Internal cue. It puts her attention on the glute at the point where it should be doing the most.

When: The lower back is arching

Say: Keep the lumbar spine neutral and tuck your rib cage down.

Why: Internal cue. The lower back (lumbar spine) stays in its natural curve, and ribs to hips takes the arch out of the finish.

Terms on this page

lumbar spine: The lower back, the part of the spine between the ribs and the pelvis. It is the part that arches when the hip runs out of range.
