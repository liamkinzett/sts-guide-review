Rest the part, not the person

Rest the hamstring. Do not rest the person. She keeps doing everything she can do while she avoids working that one muscle.

Verdict

A suspected tear is referred the day it happens.

The ladder below is what a clinician does with a client they have assessed. Read it to understand what recovery looks like. Do not start it.

Replace the R in RICE with range of motion. Rest, as most people mean it, is not the answer. Movement the tissue tolerates is.

The failure is a missing word. Coaches mean the specific sense and say "rest". The client hears the general one and stops training. Six weeks disappear.

The worked ladder, on a diagnosed tear

Passive. Sat on a couch, heel on a wheeled chair. Lift the leg, put it down. Knee and hip move, the hamstring does not work.

Active-assisted. Same setup. Pull the chair in with the heel.

Active. Bodyweight squat.

Active-resisted. Load.

The criterion to move up is a range, not a date. Full range, pain free, at one rung before the next one starts.

Say it like this

We're resting that hamstring. That doesn't mean you rest. You keep doing everything you can do, and we leave that one muscle alone until your physio says otherwise.
