The lookup table: four faults on the barbell pulls

Four more faults, named so that a coach can write them down and file a fix under them. Three belong to the deadlift.

#Canonical nameAlso calledWhere it is not a fault
41Lateral hip shifthip shift; shifting to one side; hips move sideways out of the hole; weight shift; she loads one leg more than the otherA visible shift under a maximal single is not automatically this fault. Region by region owns the judgement of when a shift matters. This row exists so the fault has a name
42Sumo toe flare past the baseexcessive sumo toe flare; feet turned out too far; too much flare, she goes overConventional stance. This is a sumo fault. It is foot angle, not stance width, so it is not "base too wide" and narrowing the stance does not fix it
43Bar and body set on the wrong lineshoulders too far over the bar; shoulders behind the bar; bar not over the midfoot; centre of mass off the midfootHolds on both pulls. It happens before the pull starts, which separates it from "bar off the midline", the bar leaving the line during the pull
44Sitting too deep into the pullsquatting too deep; squatting the deadlift; trying to make it a pure push; bum down, read as a depth instructionDeadlift only. On a squat, depth is the range. On a deadlift, depth past leg drive loses the muscles down the back of her body and adds bar travel
