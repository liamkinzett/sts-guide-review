Body image and the referral line · T-43.3

The line is fear and rigidity, never the food itself

The line is fear and rigidity. It is never the food itself. A client eating carefully is not the finding. A client frightened of eating otherwise is.

There is a spectrum. At the extreme end sits the eating disorder, which is clinical. Before it sits eating driven by fear and rigid rules (disordered eating). That is short of a clinical diagnosis, and it is common in the fitness world. Both are past your line to treat. The referral page covers what you do.

Where the line falls. It comes down to habits, and to fear. The level of fear attached to certain foods, or to changing a routine, is where you draw it. A client who cannot eat out without getting anxious is over it.

Check

Can her routine flex when the day does not go to plan?

Can she eat out without getting anxious?

Does a food she did not plan pass without panic?

yes: She is on the safe side. Coach the rest of this topic.

no: Fear and rigidity are present. Read the three referral behaviours and refer where one is met.

Why the test is about flex, not food. Two clients can eat the same tidy week. One does it because it works and drops it on her sister's birthday. The other does it because she is afraid of what happens if she does not. The food is identical. The fear is the finding.

Terms on this page

disordered eating: Eating driven by fear and rigid rules rather than by hunger and choice. It is the non-clinical end of the spectrum that runs up to an eating disorder, and it is common in the fitness world.
