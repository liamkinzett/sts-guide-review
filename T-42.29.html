When a client asks for daily check-ins

A request for daily check-ins is a request to change the cadence. The channel was never the thing that was closed. Two different things are being asked for, and only one of them should move.

The two systems. The check-in stays on its own weekly schedule. It is kept separate from ordinary communication precisely so it does not become the only channel that exists. The ordinary channel is open every day, with a reply inside the window she was promised.

Compared

columns: The check-in | The platform

Cadence: Weekly, on her day | Open any day

What it is for: The structured review of her week | Anything that comes up between reviews

What she can expect: Your reply inside the window, with a summary and action points | A reply inside the same window. Not instantly.

The answer is telling her these are two systems. The check-in stays weekly because that is what the structure is for. The platform stays open every day at the window you already promised. Framed that way, the request for more contact is already answered. A daily check-in was never the actual need.

What this does not settle. Whether daily contact is itself a dependence worth naming to her is your judgement. This page gives you the structure to offer. It does not tell you whether to raise the dependence question too.

Watch for

Agreeing to daily check-ins to feel responsive. You have doubled your work and taught her the structure bends on request.
