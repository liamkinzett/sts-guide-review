Which movement carries each deadlift fix

Every deadlift prescription in this topic lands on a movement STS programs, or on a page that tells you how to run it. This is the map.

Prescription to movement

# Hamstring accessory work

sub: Hips shoot up

Exercises: Lying hamstring curl; Seated hamstring curl; Barbell RDL

Note: These are the STS picks for the job.

# A back extension for the erectors

sub: Losing back tightness

Exercises: 45 degree hip extension

Note: Glutes carries the page, and it isolates the erectors when set up for them.

# Rigid-torso core work

sub: Loss of trunk rigidity off the floor

Note: The RKC plank, the GHD sit up and the ab wheel rollout are on the Core pages. The RKC plank is a plank with the emphasis on maximal tension rather than time.

# The paused deadlift

sub: Cannot hold tension off the floor

Note: A tempo change on the conventional pull. Tempo and pauses already carries the settled pause as the 11X1 tempo: one second settled on the floor and one at lockout.

# The good morning

sub: Losing back tightness, the middle option

Exercises: Good morning

Note: A rigid torso through hip flexion. More specific than a back extension, less than a deficit pull.

# The deficit deadlift

sub: Losing back tightness, and time in the start position

Note: Deadlift variations carries who gets one, the 1 to 2 inch height, and the four conditions. Never for a pull that hurts off the floor.

# The block pull

sub: Two rows, pointing opposite ways on purpose

Note: Not for a lagging lockout caused by position off the floor. The right lever for a pull that hurts off the floor. Deadlift variations keeps the jobs apart.

# Adductor work

sub: Sore inner thighs after sumo. The inner thigh pulls the leg in (adduction)

Exercises: Machine hip adduction

Note: Calves and adductors has the page.

Terms on this page

adduction: A limb travelling in toward the midline of the body: the leg pulled in by the inner-thigh muscles, or the arm brought in to the side. The adductors also help extend the hip at the top of a pull.
