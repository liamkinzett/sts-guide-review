How to do it

Hold the spine still and breathe under the brace. The hips do not drop. The body stays a straight line from the shoulders through the knees to the ankles.

She does not hold her breath. Long, deep breaths. On the way in she lets the side abdominal muscles facing the ceiling (obliques) expand. On the way out she breathes out through the obliques closest to the ground.

Breathing into the belly in every direction under a brace is the same skill as bracing a heavy lift. That is one reason the hold is trained at all. Breathing under load covers the skill itself.

Progression is by complexity and by taking support away. A longer clock on its own is not the progression. The ladder is on Swaps.

Terms on this page

obliques: The abdominal muscles down each side of the trunk. They twist and side-bend the trunk, and they resist both.
