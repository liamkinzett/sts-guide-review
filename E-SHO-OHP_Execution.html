How to do it

The rep, in order

Run the first rep slowly. She should not meet the frame or her own chin.

Press straight up, and stop just short of lockout. Stopping short keeps continuous tension on the front delt.

Depth is set by how deep the upper arm can tuck. It is not set by a facial landmark. The front delt attaches to the upper arm, not to the face.

On a rack press, control the bar back to a dead stop on the pins every rep. No bounce.

Separate the shoulder from the rib cage. This is STS's central standard for vertical pressing. The shoulder blade moves around a fixed rib cage. She does not lean or rotate the torso to reach lockout.

No extra movement anywhere. The head stays still. No rounding or arching of the upper back to buy range.
