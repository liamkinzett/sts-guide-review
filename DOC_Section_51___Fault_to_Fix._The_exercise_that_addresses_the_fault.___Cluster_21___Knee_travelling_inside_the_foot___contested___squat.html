Knee travelling inside the foot on the squat: regress

If "knees out" does not fix it, the cue failing is the diagnostic. Regress the exercise and program lateral hip stability. A band round her knees is not the answer.

Her knees fall inside the line of her toes (knee valgus). Faults and what you can actually see says when it counts. Above 90 degrees it is a genuine fault. Below parallel it often is not, and a woman's neutral knee sits further in than a man's. Decide it is a fault first. Then this is what you do.

Knee travelling inside the foot

# The cue did not hold

sub: Regression

What you see: You cue "knees out". She cannot fix it.

Do this: Regress the exercise and work on lateral hip stability. The cue failing told you the exercise was wrong for her today.

# Build force where she is held, and stability where she is not

sub: Accessory

Do this: Leg press to build force production in a position of external stability, where she can push hard and get stronger in an easy environment. Alongside it, split squats, step-downs and rear-foot-elevated split squats, where the hip has to hold her steady.

Exercises: Leg press; DB Bulgarian split squat; Step-down

# A warm-up gatekeeper that trains the hip's real job

sub: Accessory, instead of band lateral walks

Why: Band lateral walks challenge and activate the glutes. They do not carry over to what the glutes do in a squat, which is stabilising the hip during gross movement. A single-leg hinge or a hip airplane trains that function. STS programs the B-stance RDL as its single-leg hinge. Warming up carries the hip airplane.

Exercises: DB B-stance RDL

# A lifter who is already strong

sub: Accessory, timed to the squat session

Do this: Load a rear-foot-elevated split squat two days out and a front-foot-elevated split squat five days out. She then gets under the barbell with the work already done, and the squat expresses it. For a lifter squatting 700 or 800 pounds, unloaded correctives do nothing.

Exercises: DB Bulgarian split squat; DB FFE split squat

Not a band round the knees

True hip stability is not built through little banded exercises. The pairing is refuted, not merely absent. Lifters taught to shove their knees out ended up squatting on the outside edges of their feet, which is its own named fault.

Terms on this page

knee valgus: Her knees fall inwards, inside the line of her toes, as she squats or stands up.
