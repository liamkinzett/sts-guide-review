Drill performed passively

The shape is right and no tension is produced.

Slow it down and add the hold.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Anti-rotation

sub: Pointing rather than holding. The arms move, the trunk does not resist

Do this: Slow it. Add the hold

# Anti-rotation

sub: Loading it instead of progressing it

Do this: Progress the position, not the stack

# Activation drill

sub: Doing the drill without producing tension. The shape is right and nothing is working

Do this: Slow it. Add the hold

# Soft tissue work

sub: *"Rolling like a pizza"*, with no pin and no joint movement

Do this: Pin, hold, move the joint

# Soft tissue work

sub: Lying passively on the thoracic (thoracic) roller

Do this: As above

Cues written for this fault.

Cues

When: Soft tissue work · external cue

Say: rolling like a pizza

Why: Used as the negative. Naming the wrong thing is the whole cue. Active rolling, not rolling like a pizza.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Anti-rotation

Say: Pushing the earth away.

Why: Start here. It produces the tension the drill exists for.

When: Anti-rotation

Say: Drive the heel back.

Why: The hip-extension half, which is what the pointing version misses.

When: Anti-rotation

Say: Make a fist.

Why: Note that this phrase doubles as our standard for what a cue has to be. A cue must be as easy to execute as making a fist. No verbatim cue for the Pallof press exists in STS.

When: Activation drill

Say: Tall as you can. Tall as you can.

Why: The in-session landmine press cue.

When: Activation drill

Say: Slight forward reach.

Why: The serratus cue, used on every delt variation.

When: Activation drill

Say: Keep those scaps down. Cue more depression than retraction.

Why: End range of retraction becomes elevation. On paper that reads as a detail.

Terms on this page
