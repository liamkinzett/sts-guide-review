Elbow presentations that go straight to referral

Some elbow presentations have no working read and no first lever. They go straight out. Knowing which ones stops you sorting something that should have been referred.

Red flags

title: Refer, and do not sort first

Numbness, tingling, pins and needles, a hand that gives way, or dropping things. Nerve symptoms at the elbow are referred immediately

A visibly swollen elbow

Locking, or a mechanical block to bending or straightening

Any elbow after surgery. Her surgeon and physio set the plan

emergency: Do not read the wrist's nerve material across to the elbow. It is a different nerve and a different tunnel. Refer.

Two more things you name rather than fix.

The wrist-up presentation. A lifter who catches bars overhead can strain the wrist flexors on the inside of the elbow. You can name the mechanism: loaded wrist extension on the catch. You cannot load the flexors back up yourself. Reduce the catching exposure and refer for the loading plan.

A client who cannot rack a bar. The snatch, the clean and jerk and the front rack are the movements most often named as provoking this region. They tell you what her elbow has been exposed to. They are not movements STS programs, so say plainly that you have no progression for them, rather than inventing one.

Do not invent a protocol

Elbow sleeves and wraps. We give no guidance on tightness, duration or when to remove them, and nothing from the wrist transfers.

Elbow nerve symptoms. There is no read to give. Refer.
