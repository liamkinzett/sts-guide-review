Setup

Set her up in this order

Upper back and shoulder blades planted firmly against the back support. The contact point does not move once the set starts. If it slides, the movement has stopped being a hip movement.

The load sits across the crease of the hips, padded. Check under load that it cannot slip onto the bony points of the pelvis. If it does, she will not load the exercise.

Feet flat. Set the width and the distance so that the shin is perpendicular to the ground at lockout.

Stance width is individual. It follows her pelvis structure and glute size, both of which change the levers inside the hip (moment arm). There is no single correct width. Let her find where the glute takes the load.

Ribs pulled down to the pelvis before rep one. A flared rib cage becomes a lower-back finish at the top.

Terms on this page

moment arm: The perpendicular distance between the joint axis and the line of force.
