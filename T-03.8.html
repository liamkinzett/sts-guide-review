Does sauna affect training the way cold water does?

Sauna and cold plunge are routinely paired. Their effects on the muscle are not a pair. Cold after lifting costs some muscle. Sauna after lifting costs nothing shown, and builds nothing shown either.

What is known. Female team-sport athletes did 10 minutes of far-infrared sauna at 50 °C after every lifting session for 6 weeks. Their muscle size and lean mass were not harmed. At the signalling level, heat after training may even nudge the growth pathway (mTOR) the other way from cold.

Why that is not a recommendation. Signalling is not an outcome. No comparison in lifters shows that sauna produces more muscle. Do not promote it on that basis.

Where you may let it be. Some clients simply enjoy it. If it is not close to training, there is nothing here to stop her. This rule refuses a claim, not a sauna.

Say it like this

The sauna isn't going to build you muscle, and it isn't going to cost you any. If you enjoy it, enjoy it.

Terms on this page

mtor: The signalling pathway inside the muscle that switches on protein building after training.
