What she will ask you · T-47.9

"Can I target my belly fat?"

On spot reduction, say only what both sides accept: fat loss is systemic and muscle growth is local. Then program the exercise she asked for anyway. This is a judgement call, because the claim has two sides and you do not state it flat either way.

The two sides, plainly. One side says targeted fat loss is real, through blood flow to the area, and even that side calls it a last-few-percent tool. The other side says nobody who has got in genuinely good shape has ever used the word belly fat. Serious fat loss is a global process. Neither side gives you a mechanism you can stand behind to a client.

What to actually do. Educate the belief away, and still program the exercise she asked for. She gets the answer and she also gets her set. Nothing is lost by running the exercise. The muscle underneath grows, locally, which is the part that is real.

Say it like this

Fat comes off everywhere, not from the spot you train. What training a spot does do is build the muscle underneath it, and that changes how it looks. So we'll keep the exercise. Just not for the reason you thought.

Why this is the exception in the topic. Almost nothing here changes a program. This page does not change one either, in the sense that you still run what she asked for. What changes is what she thinks it is for.

Watch for

Stating it flat either way. "Spot reduction is a myth" and "we can target that" both overreach. Say what both sides accept.
