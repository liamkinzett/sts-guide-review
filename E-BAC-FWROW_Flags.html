Watch for

  • The low back is the designed-in limiter. Whether that is a feature or a defect depends entirely on the goal. Either way expect it. Do not read it as a coaching failure.
  • Grip can end the set before the back does. Grip must not become the roadblock to muscular endurance. Straps are allowed on this family. They are refused only on pull-ups. Grip-burning work is placed so it does not pre-exhaust the hands; see Building a session.
  • Soreness between the ribs and down the sides after heavy dumbbell rows is expected, not a warning sign. One of the spinal muscles runs on a slant from the pelvis to the ribs (iliocostalis). It works when the trunk bends and when it bends with a twist, which is where the twisting demand of a single-arm row lands.
  • Opposite side against same side is a real fork, not a preference. Opposite-foot-forward loading (contralateral) taxes the trunk. Same-foot-forward loading (ipsilateral) taxes hip stability. If either is what fails first, switch setups rather than cueing harder.
  • Who to modify for. Anyone with a low back that will not hold a still hinge under load. Anyone whose hinge is not yet owned, since STS gates barbell hinging behind demonstrated lat control on a cable pullover; see Straight-arm pulldown. Anything about her being a woman is The female body. Pain is Pain rules and when to refer.
  • Prerequisite: an owned hinge. The barbell row is deliberately less loadable out of the gate, and that is a feature. It is the same high-shear position under far less load, which makes it the safer teaching vehicle for a lifter hesitant about technique.
    • Terms on this page

    iliocostalis: The outermost of the long spinal muscles. It runs on a slant from the pelvis up to the ribs. So it works when the trunk bends, and when it bends with a twist.

    contralateral: Opposite side. On a single-arm row it means the forward foot is on the opposite side to the rowing arm.

    ipsilateral: Same side. On a single-arm row it means the rowing arm and the forward foot are on the same side.
