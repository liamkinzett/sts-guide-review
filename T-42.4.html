The coaching relationship · T-42.4

What a client is actually paying a coach for

She is paying for objectivity. A friend cannot give her that. She is paying you to be honest with her, in a way people who are friends with her will not be.

The worked example is a performance conversation. It connects the behaviour to the goal rather than judging the behaviour. Performance is dropping. The conclusion is plain: there is no way she reaches her goals if this continues.

How to open it. With an open-ended question, never a verdict.

Opening the conversation

Hey, I know your goal was to lose some weight. Are you happy with where you're at?

Why it works. An open-ended question makes her supply the observation. A verdict makes her defend herself, which is the conversation you did not want. Once she has said the thing herself, you are on the same side of it.

This is the tool most of the topic runs on. Nearly every hard moment later, a regain, a boundary pushed, a goal that looks borrowed, is opened with this same open-ended question about the goal.

Watch for

Softening the objectivity to keep the friendship. The friendship is not what she hired. A coach who bends to keep her comfortable is the friend she cannot afford.
