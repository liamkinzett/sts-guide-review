The phases · T-07.36

Staged blocks are for a test date, not a physique block

Accumulation and intensification are a real structure for a client peaking toward a date. Borrowed onto a physique-only block, they are costume.

The strength phase's staged structure exists to solve a specific problem: converting volume into a peak strength expression on a fixed test date (accumulation) (realisation). A physique client training for muscle rather than a number on a specific day has no realisation phase to build toward. There is no single day the block is aimed at.

What her hypertrophy block already does instead. A hypertrophy block does not stage volume into rising-then-falling on a schedule. It runs at the dose she needs until overload stops happening, then ends. Volume does not ramp on a schedule; Volume has that rule. Nothing distinguishes an accumulation phase from an intensification phase inside that structure. Nothing is being staged toward a peak. The block is one continuous phase by design, not two undeclared phases.

Where the borrowed language does real damage. A coach importing "accumulation" and "intensification" onto a hypertrophy block usually means start light and build to heavy.

Building a ramp into the program before the block starts turns a response to her progress into a schedule. The schedule then runs regardless of her progress.

The peaking version earns its staging because it solves for a test day. A physique block has no test day to solve for. Staging one anyway reintroduces exactly the scheduled-progression error Volume exists to prevent.

When the language is legitimate anyway. A powerbuilding client genuinely approaching a strength phase is, at that point, using the real staged structure for a real reason (powerbuilding). She has a test date. The distinction is not her goal in general. It is whether there is a fixed day the current block is building toward.

Compared

columns: Physique-only, no test date | Approaching a real test

Structure: One continuous hypertrophy block | Accumulation, transmutation, realisation

Volume: Runs at the dose she needs until overload stops | Staged toward the test day

Ends when: Overload stops happening | Test day

Terms on this page

accumulation: The first four weeks of a strength phase, where volume is high and the load is moderate. The job is to build the raw material.

realisation: The last four weeks of a strength phase. Fatigue is shed so that the strength she has built can show up on test day.

powerbuilding: A pathway that wants both a stronger squat, bench and deadlift and a more muscular physique, and alternates between the two.
