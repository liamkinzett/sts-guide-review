Pain rules and when to refer · T-35.27

A workplace injury runs on the same method

The bucket method does not care where the injury came from. Sort what she does into palliative, no effect and provocative. Train the first two. Rank and queue the third. None of those steps asks how the injury began.

What changes is the history you take, not the method you run. The intake asks about onset and mechanism regardless of source. A workplace injury with a clear, recent, mechanical cause is exactly the case the intake's shortcut describes. A client who tripped on a kerb does not need the full interrogation, only the first block.

Deviate where the injury removes an entire training modality. A hand injury that takes gripping off the table. Six weeks with a limb in a cast. The buckets still sort whatever remains. The method needs only whatever she can currently offer, not a full body's worth of movements.

Watch for

Treating "not a training injury" as a reason to wait rather than to sort. The delay costs her a smaller and smaller box, while everyone waits for permission a diagnosis was never going to give.
