The phases · T-07.13

A holding phase runs three to six weeks, extended to twelve

A holding phase is set at 3 to 6 weeks when you plan it, and extended to 10 or 12 weeks when she needs it. It is reactive rather than planned. You extend it on how she is actually recovering.

How long a holding phase runs

Set at planning | 3-6 | Three weeks is the floor

Extended when she needs it | 10-12 | Twelve is the ceiling

She leaves when her weight is stable and she feels rested, physically and mentally. Both, not either.

Three weeks is the floor. If she only needs a week, what she needs is a deload rather than a shortened holding phase (deload).

Ten to twelve is the ceiling, and it is real. Past 12 weeks it has stopped being a holding phase. It is a maintenance block. She should be in a phase with a goal attached.

When you use one. Assessing a new client governs it. A holding phase sits between hypertrophy and fat loss in either direction, plus the one exception it names for sectioning a very large cut.

Terms on this page

deload: A planned easier week that lets fatigue clear while the muscle and the skill stay. At STS only the number of sets comes down.
