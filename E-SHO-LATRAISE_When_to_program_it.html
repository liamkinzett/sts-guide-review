When to use it

The problem you notice. An overhead press is already in her session and you want the side delt worked from the other end of its range. Or a press provokes her shoulder and the trigger is load. Or she goes shruggy on every raise and reports a tight neck. The lateral raise answers all three, and the equipment you pick decides which one.

Reach for it when

# An overhead press is already in the same session

sub: The other arc of the arm's travel, not a second helping of the same one

Confirm it: Read the session. A press is in it.

What it gets her: The stretched, horizontal part of the movement loaded, on a cable set at knee height. "if I'm doing an overhead press and a lateral raise on the same day, I'm probably going to program a more horizontal cable". The press has trained the vertical top portion. The cable trains the stretched bottom.

# A press provokes her shoulder and the trigger is load

sub: The same perceived effort, at a lower absolute load

Confirm it: The press hurts when it is heavy and not when it is light.

What it gets her: Run the raises first, so the press is done tired (pre-exhaust). "when I do dumbbell lateral raises first, it feels even better, because I'm going to load that movement less". And the conclusion: "if it is an issue that is provoked by load, I have solved that problem".

# She reports neck or upper-trap symptoms

sub: She goes shruggy when the load pulls straight down

Confirm it: Watch her on a dumbbell. The shoulders creep toward the ears (shruggie).

What it gets her: A raise the traps do not take. Pulley at knee height, driving away. "a lot of people, when they get that more vertical vector of resistance, they'll get really shruggy". Fix the arm path first: trap dominance is an arm-path problem before it is a cueing problem.

# You count four lateral variations in her program

sub: Pick one or pick two

Confirm it: Count them on the sheet.

What it gets her: One or two versions, with the freed slot spent on something she is not getting. The verdict on exactly that program was "pick one or pick two", then use the slot to get the arm behind her.

# Her hips and knees pump to start the rep

sub: The fault removed by position, not by a cue

Confirm it: Film from the side. The knees dip before the arms move.

What it gets her: Tall kneeling. It gives immediate tactile feedback the moment she drives from the hips.

One cable height per session is the working limit

Do not stack a dumbbell raise, a mid-range cable and a stretched-position cable in one session. "I wouldn't go through and do a dumbbell lateral raise and then a mid-range cable lateral and then a lengthened cable lateral. It isn't that complicated."

Choosing the height is not a range decision. Where the rep stops is set on How to do it, and where the pause goes is Tempo and pauses.

Keep the nuance in proportion

The fine cable heights and rotation angles are for advanced physique athletes, where small changes in proportion count. A general fitness client needs only to pull back and work the muscle.

Setup. Rib cage stacked, arm path slightly forward of straight out, elbow above the hand. Setup has the full list.

Terms on this page

pre-exhaust: Tiring one muscle with an isolation exercise first, so that it is the limiter in the compound lift that follows.

shruggie: Her shoulders climb toward her ears mid-rep, so the upper traps take over work they were not given.
