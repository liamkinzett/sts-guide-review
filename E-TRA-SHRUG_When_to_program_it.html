Traps · E-TRA-SHRUG#When to program it

When to use it

The problem you notice. Her traps are one of the groups that keeps losing its slot. Or you want mid-trap and rhomboid work and are reaching for a shrug to get it. The machine shrug is the only shrug STS programs, and both situations need reading before you write it in.

Reach for it when

# You want mid-trap and rhomboid development specifically

sub: The target is between the shoulder blades, not on top of the shoulders

Confirm it: Ask what she wants to grow. Mid-trap and rhomboid work is a pull straight back (scapular retraction), not a lift.

What it gets her: Nothing from a standing or machine shrug, which lifts the shoulders. The movement for that target is the prone incline shrug (the Kelso shrug), which pulls straight back against an incline bench. STS doesn't program it. Get that work from her rows instead.

# The traps are one of her stubborn or neglected groups

sub: And they keep losing their slot

Confirm it: The program sheet. Weeks go by with no trap work written down, and she wants them.

What it gets her: The traps trained without spending a headline slot on them. Traps sit with the odds and ends: forearms, calves, rear delts, traps, abs. Those lagging groups are woven into a session, not given one. That is a note about the muscle, not an indication for the shrug in particular, and it is not done during maximal-strength work.

Count her trap exposure before you add one

Her traps work in a lateral raise no matter what, assisting the shoulder blade as it rotates upward. Her weekly total is not confined to what you wrote down as trap work.

Heavy trap loading has a consequence for what follows it. After a bunch of heavy farmer's carries, do not program a military press. Program a lengthened cable lateral raise instead.

Terms on this page

scapular retraction: Pulling the shoulder blades together and back towards the spine.
