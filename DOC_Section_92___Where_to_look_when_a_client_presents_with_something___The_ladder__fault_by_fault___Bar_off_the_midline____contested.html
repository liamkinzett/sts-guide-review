Bar off the midline

The bar leaves her body or drifts off its line.

Reset over the midfoot and re-tighten the lats before you reach for any cue.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Deadlift

sub: The bar leaves the body

Named there as: Bar drifting forward off the midline

Do this: Lats are off. There is no exception to this one: "the bar drifts away from the body, you're out of position".

# Romanian deadlift

sub: Bar drifts forward, sometimes scraping the shins

Do this: Track the midfoot. Brace the lats

# Barbell squat

sub: Bar drifts forward, shoulders reaching forward over it

Do this: Reset the midfoot position and re-tighten the lat brace before touching the cue list. The same fix as the Romanian deadlift's identical fault.

# Lateral raise

sub: Reaching to the side instead of forward

Do this: Correct the arm path

# Overhead cable extension

sub: Lowering behind the head badly. Forcing extreme shoulder flexion on a vertical cable path

Do this: Correct the path

# Skull crusher and JM press

sub: JM press: bar path drifting low, tracking to the mid-sternum

Do this: Correct the path

There is no set cue written for this fault. Say the fix in plain words, one thing this set.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Forward shin angle.

Why: The master cue for anyone who lives in their heels.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Fall forward before you push back.

Why: If the centre of mass is already back, there is nowhere left to travel.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Keep the main thing the main thing. It is a front leg exercise.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Control the eccentric and move origin to insertion.

When: Barbell squat

Say: Spread the floor.

  • Barbell squat. Front-loaded variation as a constraint rather than a cue. You cannot sit back and shear under a counterbalance.
