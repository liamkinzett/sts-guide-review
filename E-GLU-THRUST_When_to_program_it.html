Glutes · E-GLU-THRUST#When to program it

When to use it

The problem you notice. She has shown you she can hold her pelvis still under load. She knows where her pelvis is in space and keeps it there through a set. You still want the glute loaded at its shortest (shortened position). Or her hamstrings finish every hip extension she does, and you want the glute carrying it.

Reach for it when

# She can hold pelvic position under load and you still want the shortened position loaded

sub: Demonstrated control is the gate, not time served

Confirm it: Watch her 45 degree hip extension. The rep ends at neutral every time, with no lower-back finish, set after set.

What it gets her: Short-range glute work she can take close to failure. On the machine hip thrust the external stability makes it easier to reach true muscular failure before her technique fails. Note machine. That is the version this reasoning is about.

# Her hamstrings finish every hip extension she does

sub: You want the glute carrying it

Confirm it: She reports hamstring cramp or fatigue at the top of hip extensions, and her glutes feel little.

What it gets her: Hip extension with the hamstrings mechanically out of the way. The bent knee shortens the hamstrings until they can produce almost no force (active insufficiency). That leaves the glutes. It is the reason to pick this over a hinge when the goal is glute size.

The strongest reason not to reach for it

If she is stalled, it is almost certainly already in her week. Most people are already doing hip thrusts. Most people are already good at shortening their glutes and poor at loading them long. So it cannot be what unlocks her. Before adding it, check what it displaces.

Where it sits in the session: either, by purpose. Decide which job the hip thrust is doing before you decide where it goes.

Light activation sets may open a lower session. A few easy sets to wake the glutes up before the main lift is a fair use. Heavy working sets go later, in the session's normal order. A working-weight thrust at the front of the session is the version we rule out. It spends her freshest effort on an accessory.

Setup. Upper back planted, load padded across the hip crease, feet set so the shins are vertical at the top, ribs down. Setup has the list.

Terms on this page

shortened position: The muscle is working while it is bunched short. For the glute that is full hip extension, locked out at the top of a thrust.

active insufficiency: A muscle that crosses two joints and is shortened at both is too bunched up to produce much force. Bending the knee does this to the hamstrings in a hip thrust.
