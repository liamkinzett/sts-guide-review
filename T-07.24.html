The phases · T-07.24

Test toward a 5RM, 3RM or 1RM

Test toward a 5RM, 3RM or 1RM. Each is a rep max (rep max), the heaviest weight she can lift for that many reps. The shakier her technique or the newer the lifter, the higher the rep max you test.

Which rep max

# 1RM

sub: Main barbell lifts where a true single is a skill she can express safely

Use it when: A true single is a trainable skill for her and safe to express on that lift.

# 3RM

sub: The middle ground

Use it when: You want a genuine strength test with less risk, and less peaking needed to reach it.

# 5RM

sub: Where a single is unsafe or tells you nothing

Use it when: A true single is either unsafe or uninformative for that lift. Also for a lifter whose technique is not yet stable enough to trust under one.

Terms on this page

rep max: The heaviest weight she can lift for a set number of reps. A 5RM is the most she can lift five times, a 1RM once.
