Building a session · T-12.16

Regional growth justifies coverage, not session bloat

Different exercises can bias different parts of the same muscle (regional hypertrophy). That is why the coverage rule exists: compounds chosen for different portions rather than one movement repeated. The same rule caps how far the idea is allowed to run. One or two isolations, not five.

How to choose between two and four movements for one muscle. Ask one question of each added movement. Does it bias a portion the others miss? If yes, it is coverage. If no, it is session bloat, whether or not the angle in question is real. There is no count of angles that makes coverage complete. The coverage question is the whole test.

Watch for

A long exercise list built to chase one more angle. That is the session bloat the coverage rule already warns against.

Terms on this page

regional hypertrophy: Growth that is greater in one part of a muscle than another, depending on which exercise loaded it.
