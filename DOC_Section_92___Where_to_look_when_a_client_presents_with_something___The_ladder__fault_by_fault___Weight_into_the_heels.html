Weight into the heels

Her toes lift and her weight sits back.

Forward shin angle; toes gripping the ground.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Free-weight row

sub: Rocking back into the heels, negative shin angle: "in a position to fall backward".

Do this: Shin angle forward

# Deadlift

Named there as: Toes lifting

Do this: She lifts her toes: "toes off the ground, the devil".

# Romanian deadlift

sub: Toes lift, weight sits back

Do this: Forward shin, head slightly forward as counterbalance

# Step-up and step-down

sub: Step-down made too glute-biased. She reaches back far and the weight goes into the heel

Do this: Shorten the reach

# Machine squat

sub: Feet set too high on the plate, driving the shin angle negative

Do this: Lower the feet

Cues written for this fault.

Cues

When: Free-weight row · external cue

Say: toes on the ground like grip in the ground

Why: The direct fix for the heel fault. It produces whole-foot pressure rather than a conscious weight shift.

When: Romanian deadlift · external cue

Say: Forward shin angle.

Why: The master cue for anyone who lives in their heels.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Free-weight row

Say: chest over your knees

Why: Sets the parallel torso the whole exercise depends on. It is checkable from the side in a single frame.

When: Free-weight row

Say: separate the rib cage and the pelvis

Why: For the contralateral single-arm row specifically. It names the actual task, which is the trunk resisting rotation while the arm moves.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Fall forward before you push back.

Why: If the centre of mass is already back, there is nowhere left to travel.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Keep the main thing the main thing. It is a front leg exercise.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Control the eccentric and move origin to insertion.

When: Step-up and step-down

Say: Drop that belly button straight down over the heel.

Why: The step-down's primary cue. Use it after the position check of big toe, knee and sternum in line with the hip outside the knee.
