Core · E-COR-RAISE#Flags

Watch for

  • The prerequisites, which are also the reasons it gets swapped. Complex coordination and timing. Substantial overhead shoulder mobility and shoulder-blade control. Whole-body core strength and pelvic stability through every plane. A sturdy overhead bar. Grip capacity, since high-volume hanging tires the hands.
  • The grip rule. Do not let the grip become the roadblock to her muscular endurance. Grip burners go at the end of the session. Building a session has the order.
  • Progression runs knees to straight legs. Load is not on that ladder. See Swaps.
  • Keep the hanging version where the shoulder tolerates it. Hanging under load trains the shoulder blade's full upward turn and the shoulder's inward and outward rotation. That is a reason to keep it, separate from the trunk work.
  • The top of the rep. One standard coaches the top as spinal flexion, which is this page. Another, coaching the hanging raise as a pull-up accessory, coaches extension at the shoulder so the feet travel higher. They cannot both be the standard for one rep. Coach the standard printed here, and log which one you used.
  • Core exercise or quad exercise? The straight-leg hanging version also appears as a leg-extension alternative, with core and quads as its target. STS has no page for it yet.
