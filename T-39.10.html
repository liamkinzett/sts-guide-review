Reading her training data · T-39.10

Recomposition or stall: two readings, reported separately

A question that asks for one word, recomp or fail, is not answered with one word. You run two independent readings and report both.

(Recomposition) is the word for losing fat and gaining muscle at the same time. The scale can sit still while it happens, which is why the question gets asked.

Reading one. Are the lifts actually progressing? Progression judges that by the rep band, not by the number on the plate. Flat measurements in a single week carry no verdict either way.

Reading two. How is her composition moving? Assessing a new client reads it in bands, from her check-in photos, never as a trigger on its own.

Run both and report both. Manufacturing a single combined label for the two is exactly the invented synthesis this topic does not do. The two readings exist so that a coach is never left guessing at one from the other.

Reporting both readings

Two separate things. Your lifts are moving inside their rep ranges, so the training stimulus is working. Your shape is changing slowly in the photos. Those are two answers, not one.
