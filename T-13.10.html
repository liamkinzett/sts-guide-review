Choosing exercises · T-13.10

Tag every exercise KEPT, ROTATED IN or EVOLVED, and record why

At every phase change, each exercise in the new program carries one of three tags. The reason is recorded with it.

The three tags

# KEPT

sub: Carried forward

What it means: Load data continues and progressive overload runs unbroken.

# ROTATED IN

sub: New, replacing something

What it means: Fresh tissue exposure. A new logbook entry starting from zero.

# EVOLVED

sub: A progression of what was there

What it means: A dumbbell split squat becoming a barbell split squat. The pattern continues while the demand rises.

Why it works. Without tags, how much of the program changed is a feeling. With them, you can count. Tagging also forces the reason to be stated at the moment the decision is made, rather than reconstructed later.

Record the machine setup as well. For a logbook to mean anything, the exercise's mechanical profile has to stay identical. Write the configuration down: "incline bench hole 7, pulley setting 19". Changing a seat height or a cable angle shifts the joint torques and fabricates progress on paper.

Watch for

Tagging everything KEPT because the program looks similar. An exercise performed on a different machine is ROTATED IN, whatever it is called.
