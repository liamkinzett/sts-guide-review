The phases · T-07.12

In a deficit, effort never goes past 1 RIR

In a deficit, effort floors at 1 rep in reserve. Her reps in reserve (RIR) never go below 1. Zero is the exception, never the end of the progression.

Effort across a four-week cut

3 RIR | 1 | Week 1

2 RIR | 1 | Week 2

1 RIR | 2 | Weeks 3 and 4. The floor. She does not go past it

Week1234
RIR3211

Her recovery is already compromised in a deficit (deficit). A set to failure costs her more than it returns.

The exceptions. There are only two. She explicitly pushes for it and understands the trade. Or the cut is running alongside a strength peak, where the demands are different.

Terms on this page

RIR: Reps in reserve. How many more reps she could have done when the set stopped. 2 RIR means she stopped two reps short of failure.

deficit: Eating fewer calories than she burns, so that she loses body fat. A cut is run in a deficit.
