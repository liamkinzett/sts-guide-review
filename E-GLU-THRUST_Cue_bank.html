Cues

Cues

When: At the top of the rep

Say: Blow all your air out, pull your obliques in, and suck your belly button to your spine.

Why: An internal cue. It is a stomach vacuum (stomach vacuum) that trains control of the deep abdominal wall (transverse abdominis). It stops the lower back taking the lockout.

When: She stops short or finishes with an arch

Say: Drive up until your shins are vertical and there's a straight line from your kneecap to your head. Chin tucked, eyes to your knees.

Why: An external cue that gives her the finish position as a shape she can check.

When: At peak contraction

Say: Focus on the bone-to-bone pull of the glutes.

Why: An internal cue for the lifter who owns the pattern. It puts her attention on the glute doing the squeezing.

Terms on this page

stomach vacuum: Breathing all the way out and drawing the belly in hard toward the spine. It trains the deep abdominal muscles and flattens the lower back.

transverse abdominis: The deepest layer of the abdominal wall, wrapping around the trunk like a corset. It braces the spine and pelvis from the inside.
