Trunk angle wrong for the pattern: move the load forward

To make a squat more vertical, move the load forward. Low bar, then high bar, then front, then goblet. The further in front of her the centre of mass sits, the easier it is to stay upright.

Trunk angle wrong for the pattern

# Too much forward lean for the pattern

sub: Setup: bring the load forward

What you see: Her torso folds forward. The squat looks like a hinge.

Why it happens: With the load behind her, her centre of mass is back, so she leans forward to balance it.

Do this: Move the load from back to front until she can stay upright. Low bar to high bar to front squat to goblet squat.

Exercises: High bar barbell back squat; Barbell front squat; Goblet squat

# Forward lean straining the lower back

sub: Setup: widen the stance

What you see: She leans forward and reports her lower back working hard.

Why it happens: The hips sit behind the bar, so the trunk has a long lever to hold (moment arm).

Do this: Widen the stance. It lets the hips travel forward, so she can sit up. That shortens the lever on the lower back and cuts the strain.

# She cannot hold her upper back up

sub: Accessory: front squat

What you see: Her upper back rounds under the bar.

Why it happens: With her hands on a back-loaded bar, upper-back extension is passive. Nothing asks the muscles to hold it.

Do this: Front squat. In a front squat that upper-back extension becomes totally active.

Exercises: Barbell front squat

Terms on this page

moment arm: The perpendicular distance between the joint axis and the line of force.
