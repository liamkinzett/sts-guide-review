A session that is bad at the same hour every week is a fuelling question

A session that is bad at the same hour every week is a fuelling question. Route it, do not solve it.

Guide

name: Nutrition Manual

reason: Anything she eats belongs there. This page tells you how to recognise a fuelling problem and where to send it, not what she should eat.

The pattern that identifies it. The same slot, week after week, worse than her others. Not a bad session. A bad hour. Where the pattern runs across her whole week rather than one slot, this is the wrong page and Fatigue and recovery owns it.

Refuse the obvious fix. The instinct is to make a 5am client eat at 4:45. The intervention moves earlier instead. For someone who trains in the morning, the pre-workout meal is the last meal before bed the night before. At a certain point, all nutrition is pre-workout nutrition.

Two things are yours. Two are not.

Compared

columns: Yours | The Nutrition Manual's

The pattern: Naming it from the logbook and telling her it is a pattern | Deciding what she eats or when

The fix: Moving the session where the schedule allows, which is a programming change | Prescribing a pre-training meal

Whether it is even a fuelling problem depends on her body composition. Whether carbohydrate before training makes a meaningful difference to her performance depends on where she sits. That threshold, and the numbers around it, belong to the Nutrition Manual. Do not carry them here.

One low-friction move is training-side. Have her eat the same thing every day before she trains. The mechanism is conditioning rather than fuelling: when she eats that thing, her body knows that in 45 minutes it is going to train. Consistency, not composition, which keeps it on your side of the line.

Where she insists on training fasted, or you would rather she ate, the food half of that question is the Nutrition Manual's. Yours is the pattern and the slot.

Say it like this

Your Tuesday 5am session has been your worst one for a month now. That's a pattern, not a bad day. I'll raise the food side with your nutrition coach, and we can look at moving the slot.
