When to use it

The problem you notice. Her free squat ends with a cooked lower back and legs that had more in them. Or she has knee complaints and has been chasing load on the hack squat. Or she is simply not skilled at the back squat yet and the block still needs a loaded squat. The machine squat holds her in place so the quads become the only limit.

Three machines, none of them the "real" one. The hack squat, the pendulum squat and the Smith machine squat all have different resistance profiles (resistance profile). None is superior to another, and the Smith machine is not inferior. Each one loads her legs through the rep in its own way. Each machine's path and pivot changes where in the rep the weight feels heaviest. Pick the one that suits her and her gym, then keep it long enough to progress on it.

Session order. If her session has a barbell back squat, it comes first and the machine work follows. The higher-skill movement goes first, while she is fresher, to get more out of it. A barbell squat needs her to balance, brace and steer the bar herself, so it suffers most when she is tired. A machine holds her in place, so it can still be pushed hard later in the session.

Reach for it when

# Her lower back and erectors give out before her quads on free squats

sub: The set ends with a cooked back and legs that had more in them

Confirm it: She reports the back tiring first, set after set, and video shows the trunk folding before the legs slow.

What it gets her: The whole quad stimulus kept, with the limiter taken out. She can move to the hack, pendulum or Smith machine squat entirely without losing quad stimulus. This is the main reason the page exists.

# She has knee complaints and has been chasing load on the hack squat

sub: The same training effect without the joint pain

Confirm it: A chronic knee complaint, not an acute flare. Chasing load on a hack squat has produced acute knee injury.

What it gets her: The same training effect without the joint pain.

Do this: Swap to the pendulum squat, run with aggressive knee bend, pauses and a controlled tempo. The swap is inside the family, not out of it.

# She is not skilled at the back squat and the block still needs a loaded squat pattern

sub: Load without waiting for the skill

Confirm it: Her back squat is not yet a position she owns under load.

What it gets her: Load now, from a pendulum or hack squat. Keep teaching the barbell skill alongside it: squat coordination and body awareness transfer back into pushing harder on the machine.

# She is late in a prep and the instinct is to write a whole new program

sub: The same stimulus for less fatigue and less mental effort

Confirm it: She is deep in a deficit, tired, and the program is about to change for no reason.

What it gets her: The stimulus that built the tissue is the stimulus that keeps it in a deficit. The hack squat is the named late-prep swap.

Read before she lifts

A client reporting intense knee pain is not doing a hack squat today. That is a screening answer from the intake form, not a judgement call in the session. A chronic complaint gets the pendulum. An acute flare gets neither.

It is not the third quad slot. Squat, then leg press, then leg extension is doubling down on the same position. The hack squat by itself does not bring any function back. Program it to answer one of the four situations above, not to fill a gap in a template.

These situations were built on the hack and the pendulum. Read them as indications for those two. The Smith machine squat has no back pad, so its faults and its indications are its own.

Terms on this page

resistance profile: Where in the range an exercise is hardest and where it is easiest. It is set by the line of the load against the joint, so a dumbbell and a cable can load the same muscle very differently. On a machine the cam sets it.
