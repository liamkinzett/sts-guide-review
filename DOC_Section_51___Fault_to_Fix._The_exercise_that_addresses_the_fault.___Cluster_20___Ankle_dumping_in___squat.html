Ankle dumping in: negative shin angle or a strap

Stuck flat and unable to roll onto the outside of the foot: put her in a negative shin angle. Or run a strap under the arch to push it up. Both fixes were written for the split squat.

Her arch collapses and she rolls onto the inside of the foot. Two fixes exist, and both are split-squat prescriptions. Nothing prescribes an exercise or setup for it on a two-footed squat. Use them on the split squat, and read a two-footed squat from what the split squat shows you.

Ankle dumping in

# Stuck flat, cannot supinate

sub: Setup

What you see: The foot lies flat and she cannot roll it onto its outside edge (supinate).

Do this: A negative shin angle: a front-foot-elevated split squat. Or run a strap under the arch to push it up.

Exercises: DB FFE split squat

# Stuck on the outside edge of the foot

sub: Accessory sequence

What you see: She lives on the outside edge and the arch never loads.

Do this: A three-part sequence. A bent-knee calf raise first, a split-squat hold (isometric) second, a landmine B-stance RDL third.

Exercises: Machine seated calf raise; DB B-stance RDL

Terms on this page

negative shin angle: Her front shin leans back, so the knee sits behind the ankle rather than out over the toes.

supinate: Turn the forearm so the palm faces up. Of the foot, roll it onto its outside edge so the arch lifts.

isometric: A hold. The muscle works against the load but the joint does not move.
