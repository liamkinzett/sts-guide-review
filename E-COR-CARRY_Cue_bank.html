Core · E-COR-CARRY#Cue bank

Cues

The carry has one cue, and it is for the suitcase carry. Use it as the example it is: one small cue reorganising a whole pattern.

Cues

When: Suitcase carry, as the trailing foot leaves the ground

Say: Keep your big toe on the ground as your foot goes behind you.

Why: Internal, and body-focused on purpose. Awareness has to be conscious before it can be automatic. One client's response: "I've never felt that in my life. I didn't understand that I could walk this way."

It sits against the cue ladder

Cueing ranks cues about the outside world above cues about her own body. This cue is a body cue. Use it because it is the only carry cue there is, and know that it is the exception.

One cue is not a bank. Do not make up others to fill the gap. Coach the position standard on Dose instead.

Remember where the carry stands. STS does not yet write a carry into a client's program. This page is for coaching one when it appears in the strength system's lower session or in a client's existing training.
