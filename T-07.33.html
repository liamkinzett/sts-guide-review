How a year of blocks adds up on each pathway

The year's block mix is arithmetic, not a new quota. Run this topic's own rules forward across a year and the mix falls out on its own.

This is what the phase rules produce once you run them across a year for an intermediate woman on each of the three pathways. Every block below is at its default length. In practice a block that is still working gets extended, so a real year holds a little less than these.

A year on each pathway

# Hypertrophy path

sub: Several hypertrophy blocks, at most one strength block

How it adds up: A year holds several hypertrophy blocks at the six-week default, each ending in a deload. A holding phase sits wherever the pathway calls for one, and a cut where her goal calls for one. At most one strength block. It does not need to spend a realisation phase unless she specifically wants the number (realisation).

# Powerbuilding path

sub: Two strength blocks, two to three hypertrophy blocks

How it adds up: At twelve weeks per strength block and six per hypertrophy block, a year holds two strength blocks and two to three hypertrophy blocks. That lands exactly on the cap of two realisation phases a year without a coach having to watch for it.

# Powerlifting path

sub: Two full realisation phases, hypertrophy blocks between them

How it adds up: Two blocks carry a full realisation phase. Hypertrophy blocks fill the weeks between them to build what the next strength block expresses. A third strength block is legitimate on this path, but only two of however many get to end in a test. The rest finish at the end of transmutation and skip the bill (transmutation).

Deficit blocks are not a quota. A cut is not scheduled per year. It is scheduled where her goal and pathway call for one, and projected (projection) before it starts. It replaces a hypertrophy block's slot on the calendar rather than adding to the year's total.

What this is not. A ceiling on any individual number. Every block length still ends on its own condition, not on this arithmetic. Running the existing rules forward for a full year already lands powerbuilding and powerlifting inside the cap. The hypertrophy path never approaches it.

Terms on this page

realisation: The last four weeks of a strength phase. Fatigue is shed so that the strength she has built can show up on test day.

transmutation: The middle four weeks of a strength phase. Volume comes down, intensity goes up, and the tested lifts become the main movements.

projection: The whole cut worked out on paper before it starts. How much needs to come off, at what rate, and roughly how many weeks that takes.
