Swaps

This is where the rest of the chest injury ladder arrives. Every other press walks down to the machine. The ladder below is for when the machine itself is the provocative thing.

When the machine is provocative

Seated cable chest press against a back pad. STS treats this and the machine as the same job.

Dumbbell incline press. More freedom of motion.

More freedom of motion, not less range. When the machine itself is provocative, the answer is a freer path, never a shorter one.

Two further levers. Slow the tempo (Tempo and pauses). Or tire the pec first with a pec fly (pre-exhaustion) so the press runs at a lower absolute load.

Which rungs, for which problem

# Equipment missing

sub: The machine is taken

Swap to: Cable chest press; 30 degree incline DB bench press

Why: Rungs 1 and 2. Same job, different equipment.

# A provocative shoulder

sub: The machine hurts and she is not injured

Swap to: Cable chest press; 30 degree incline DB bench press; Pec fly

Why: Rungs 1 to 4, then Region by region, shoulder.

# Home

sub: No gym access

Why: STS doesn't coach home training, so there is no home swap to give.

Terms on this page

pre-exhaustion: Tiring one muscle with an isolation exercise first, so that it is the limiter in the compound lift that follows.
