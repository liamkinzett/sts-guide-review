Assessing a new client · T-06.19

The intake screens for programming inputs, not for symptoms

The seven intake items ask about programming inputs, not symptoms. None of them asks about continence or the menstrual cycle by name, and that is deliberate. The intake was never built to be a symptom screen.

What matters is what you do once you know, whenever that turns out to be.

Verdict

A pelvic-floor symptom, whenever she tells you

Route it the moment it surfaces, at any point in the relationship. Pain rules and when to refer names the destination.

What already covers the question

# Anything cycle-related

sub: Ongoing symptom tracking, not a one-off form answer

Where it lives: The female body. Tracking her symptoms across the cycle is the mechanism, so a single intake answer was never going to do that job.

# A pelvic-floor symptom

sub: A named referral destination, whenever it surfaces

Where it lives: Pain rules and when to refer. It names the destination the moment a pelvic-floor symptom surfaces, not only at intake.

# When she does disclose

sub: What you never say

Where it lives: The female body. "Leaking is normal after a baby" is barred.

# Pregnancy or postpartum on her form

sub: Coached at STS, with fixed referral lines

Where it lives: The female body owns how she is coached. Pain rules and when to refer has the lines. Any bleeding, cramping, fluid loss or reduced movement in pregnancy goes to her GP or midwife the same day. Postpartum, loaded training resumes after clearance at the six-week check, then a pelvic health physiotherapist.

# Cycle, contraception, perimenopause or menopause questions beyond training

sub: Her GP, then a women's health specialist where it persists

Where it lives: Pain rules and when to refer. The female body covers what changes in her training.

You may worry that you started a client thin because you asked nothing about her cycle or continence on day one. You did not. The intake was not built for that job.

Watch for

Treating a late disclosure as proof the intake failed. The routing exists for whenever she tells you. It does not depend on having asked first.
