Common faults

Six common faults

# Shruggie

sub: Traps taking over. Read it as a range signal

Why it happens: She has run out of range where tension is still being added, so the traps finish the rep. On a dumbbell the load pulls straight down, which invites it.

Do this: End the rep there. Then fix the arm path from Setup step 2 before you cue anything.

# Rib flare

sub: "Tits to the ceiling." "Flappy bird wing dog"

Why it happens: The rib cage was never stacked, or she lifts the chest to make the weight feel lighter.

Do this: Ribs to hips.

# Body English

sub: "Michael Jackson lean." Body swing and hip drive

Why it happens: The load is more than the delt can move on its own.

Do this: Reduce load. Tall kneeling removes it by position.

# Elbow working on a fixed-arm movement

sub: Bending the elbows mid-rep

Why it happens: Bending the elbow shortens the lever and makes the weight easier.

Do this: Fix the elbow angle.

# Load past the position she owns

sub: Too heavy at the expense of tension

Why it happens: She is chasing a number, and the delt has stopped being the thing that moves it.

Do this: Reduce load.

# Bar off the midline

sub: Reaching to the side instead of forward

Why it happens: The arm has drifted out of the plane of the shoulder blade to straight out to the side.

Do this: Correct the arm path. Keep the dumbbells in your peripheral vision.

Do not cue 'avoid elevating your shoulders toward your ears'

Fix the arm path first. Trap dominance is an arm-path problem before it is a cueing problem. Reach for a shoulder-blade-down cue (depression) only as a second lever if the path fix does not hold.

It collides with the shrug, where lifting the shoulders is the trained action.

Terms on this page

depression: The shoulder blade pulled down, away from the ear. The opposite of a shrug.
