When to use it

The problem you notice. She competes, or intends to, and the bench is a lift the platform will judge. Or her tightness is visibly gone before the bar reaches her chest. Or she is long-limbed and the triceps is what runs out at lockout. The barbell bench press, and its variations, answer each of these.

Reach for it when

# She competes, or intends to

sub: The bench is one of the three lifts she will be scored on

Confirm it: Her goal is a powerlifting meet, not a physique. The phases tells you which block she is in.

What it gets her: The lift she will be scored on, trained as the thing it is. For a powerlifting-goal client the barbell bench is non-negotiable. For a physique-goal client it is optional. That is a goal difference, not a technique one.

# Her position degrades on the way down

sub: The tightness is visibly gone before the bar reaches her chest

Confirm it: Film from the side. She leaves the top tight and arrives at the chest loose.

What it gets her: She arrives at the chest in the position she left the top in. That is the Spoto press. The bar hovers an inch short of the chest on a feather touch. Program it strictly for a lifter who struggles to control the descent and loses tightness before touching her chest.

# She is long-limbed, or benching in a shirt, and the triceps is what runs out

sub: The lockout is where the rep dies

Confirm it: The bar stalls in the top half, set after set, with the chest position holding.

What it gets her: Triceps strength overloaded where the lockout actually needs it. That is the block press, pressing from a board or block.

# The heavy work is done and the next press slot is meant to refine technique

sub: A supplemental slot, not more specificity

Confirm it: Her main bench is already in the session. The phases places the slot.

What it gets her: The same motor pattern at a lower technical demand. The close grip barbell bench press is the STS row for this slot. You will hear chains, boards and the floor press grouped with it for the same reason.

The fault the Spoto press does not fix

Relaxing on the chest is a technical fault, not a bottom-range strength gap. A lifter who goes loose once the bar touches needs the touch coached, not removed. Board and Spoto work reinforce the relaxation instead of removing it.

Two contraindications

A history of pec tear, anterior shoulder pain, long limbs or triceps dominance. The flat barbell bench is orthopaedically unfriendly for a large population. The fixed bar path often triggers pinching at the front of the shoulder (impingement). Dumbbell bench press is the same pattern without the fixed path.

The board press has its own. Just be wary that you don't turn into a good board presser and a shitty bench presser.

Setup. Edge of the bench, head off the end, feet back onto the balls, blades set before the unrack. Setup has the full list.

Terms on this page

impingement: Soft tissue at the front or top of the shoulder getting pinched between the bones as the arm moves. A fixed bar path can force the arm into the position that pinches.
