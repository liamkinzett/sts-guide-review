Cues

Cues go in the order printed. Try the first, and only move on when it fails. One cue, at most two, is the working limit.

Cues

When: She shrugs under the press

Say: Keep those scaps down. Cue more depression than retraction.

Why: Internal cue, and the first to reach for. Shrugging under a braced press is the fault this movement produces most.

When: Her sternum is stuck down and she cannot find a tall, stacked position against the pad

Say: Top of the head to the ceiling, like you're pulling yourself up with a string.

Why: External cue. It lifts the sternum without asking her to arch.

When: Teaching the position before the set

Say: Spine tall, don't overly flare, don't overly arch, keep those scaps more pinned down.

Why: Internal cue. The full summary string. Use it as a teaching sentence, not a mid-set correction.

When: She does not believe the shoulder-blade fuss is worth it

Say: On paper, it sounds like whatever; in practice, you feel it right away.

Why: External cue. The framing line. Then let the set prove it.
