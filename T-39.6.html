Reading her training data · T-39.6

"I'm doing everything right and nothing is happening" is answered with data, or pre-empted

The reply to claimed compliance is data she cannot argue with, pulled while she watches. Better still, the stall is announced before she can complain about it.

The audit. It is run live, on her own device, before any reassurance. She has her phone or her watch on her. Ask to see her weekly average steps, then and there. Then the follow-up in the same turn: we do not need seven sessions a week, we need the steps to happen.

The audit, in her words

Show me your weekly average steps. You've got your phone on you, let's see it.

The mechanism is the point. The reply to "I'm doing everything right" is a number she produced herself, in front of you. That is the first question of the diagnosis, whether she is training hard enough, made visible. The effort half of the same audit is the repeat-set test and the video in Effort and intensity. Steps as a prescription stay with Steps and cardio. The wording bends to the client; the message does not move.

The pre-emption is the stronger play, and you start it. You can see the stall two weeks before she can. The scale about to sit still, the mirror about to look the same, the tiredness, the Saturday night that went sideways. So you say it first: we are going to pull you out of this for a few weeks. Nobody panics at a move that was announced.

Motivation and adherence owns the day-one version of the same pre-frame. Muscle and growth carries the week-one script that sets the stall up alongside the timeline answer.

Pre-empting it

Here's what's coming. In a couple of weeks the scale will probably sit still and you'll feel flat. That's expected, and when it happens we're going to pull you out of it for a few weeks. All according to plan.

Watch for

Running the audit as a gotcha. You already know the answer before you ask. The point of pulling the data in front of her is that the conversation that follows is about the data rather than about her honesty.

What the data uncovers is a hidden constraint rather than a lie. Then Motivation and adherence governs what you do with it. Instructing her a fourth time is the one move guaranteed not to work.
