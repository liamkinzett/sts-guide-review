Hack squat, pendulum squat, leg press and belt squat as the second exposure

In the strength system the second lower day is a machine squat, not a second barbell squat. Competition squat on squat day, then a hack squat, a leg press or a belt squat on the second lower day. The category is what repeats, not the exercise. The pattern is trained twice a week; the competition lift once.

Count the pattern, not the exercise. A squat pattern includes the hack squat, the leg press and the safety bar squat. Hack squat on Monday and leg press on Thursday is squatting twice. And a second session (exposure) inside a day and a half is read for whether it was fresh. If the second session's early sets already look like the first session's late ones, it was not a fresh exposure, whatever the schedule says.

Order. A barbell back squat comes first, then the machine work. A machine holds her in place (externally stabilised), so it can still be pushed hard later in the session.

Reach for the machine squat family when

# Her lower back, the muscles beside her spine (erectors), gives out before her quads on free squats

sub: The limiter taken out, the quad stimulus kept

What it gets her: The whole quad stimulus, with the limiter removed. She can move to hack, pendulum and Smith squats entirely without losing quad stimulus.

# She has knee complaints and has been chasing load on the hack squat

sub: The swap is inside the family

What it gets her: The same training effect without the joint pain: a pendulum squat run with aggressive knee flexion, pauses and a controlled tempo.

# She is not skilled at the back squat and the block still needs a loaded squat pattern

sub: Load without waiting for the skill

What it gets her: Substitute a pendulum or hack squat, and teach the barbell skill anyway. Squat coordination transfers back into pushing harder on the machine.

# She is late in a prep

sub: Less fatigue, less mental effort

What it gets her: The same stimulus for less fatigue and less mental effort.

# She cannot squat to depth

sub: Depth built where the machine holds her

What it gets her: Depth built in externally stabilised patterns: leg press, hack squat, belt squat, machine squat, and the rear-foot-elevated split squat (Bulgarian split squat). Film every set to confirm depth without rounding. Going lighter and squatting deeper is only part of the solution.

When not to

She reports intense knee pain today. She is not doing a hack squat. A chronic complaint gets the pendulum squat. An acute flare gets neither.

As the third quad slot. Squat, then leg press, then leg extension is doubling down on the same position. Program a machine squat to answer a row above, not to fill a template.

The belt squat. It is one of the strength system's second-exposure options, and one of the externally stabilised patterns for building depth. Few gyms have one. Whenever you program it, write a substitute into her session notes.

Terms on this page

erectors: The long muscles either side of the spine that hold the back straight and extend it. They hold her back from rounding under a load.

exposure: One session in the week where a lift, a pattern or a muscle is trained.

externally stabilised: The machine, bench or support holds her position so she does not have to.

Bulgarian split squat: A split squat with the back foot raised on a bench behind her. It has its own page in Glutes.
