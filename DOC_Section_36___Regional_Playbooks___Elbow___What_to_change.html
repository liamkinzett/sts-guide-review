Elbow: what to change first, by presentation

Where the elbow presentation is one we already know, the first lever is written down. Change the position before you change the load.

First lever by presentation

# Hurts on one triceps movement and not another

sub: The shoulder position, not the elbow

Do this: Keep loading the version that does not hurt. Build toward the other position unloaded.

# Pain on the inside of the elbow

sub: Wrist flexors and the palm-down muscles (pronators) first, then the shoulder

Do this: Then ask about her desk. The twenty-three hours are the likelier cause.

# Pain on the outside of the elbow

sub: Suspect the brachialis

Do this: Look for a static elbow angle under a palm-down grip. Change the grip, not the load.

# Pain appearing overhead first

sub: The early warning

Do this: Treat it as the alarm it is, not as a reason to drop the movement.

Terms on this page

pronators: The muscles on the inside of the forearm that turn the palm down.
