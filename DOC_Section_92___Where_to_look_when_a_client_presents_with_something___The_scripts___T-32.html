Faults and what you can actually see: what to say

Words for her on faults and what you can actually see, by situation.

A script is only right inside its rule's conditions. Read the rule in Faults and what you can actually see before you use the words.

A fault has one name across the team. Faults and what you can actually see is where that name lives.

Nothing. This rule is for what you write, not what you say.

If you did not see it, you did not see it. Some faults cannot be screened from video at all.

Nothing to the client. To yourself: "would I bet on that if the video were two seconds shorter?"

The film has to show the angle before you can call a fault absent.

Same lift next week. Film it from behind for me though. There is something I can only check from that side.

The film has to cover the whole set before you can call a fault absent.

Say it like this

Leave it running to the end of the set. The last two reps are the ones I actually need.

Alignment passing is not the standard passing.

Say it like this

Your knee is tracking fine. I want to look at what your hips are doing to make that happen.

Momentum is not range.

Say it like this

Do that again at half speed for me.

What survives at half speed is hers.

Coning or doming down the midline under load changes the set. It is not a referral on its own.

I want to change how that one looks. Same exercise, slower, with the range shortened a little.

A fault that is not costing her anything is not a fault.
