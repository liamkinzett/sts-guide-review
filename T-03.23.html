Programming through a week of broken sleep

When her sleep is broken across a week, do not program to her worst night or to her average night.

Read the session she is actually in.

Each session, in this order

Ask how she feels before she gives you a number. A tracker's average is an input, not the verdict. Reading it first produces a client who feels what the number said.

Ask which exhausted she means. Broken sleep from night sweats, or anything else, does not hit every session the same way. Some days she is not sharp (neurological fatigue). Some days the muscle is not the limiter at all. A plan built for her worst night answers a question she may not be asking on a given Tuesday.

Apply what sleep does. One bad night measurably suppresses the adaptation. That argues for treating sleep as a live input to the day's session. It does not argue for building the week around either extreme.

Adjust the session to what she reports that day. That is what it means to autoregulate (autoregulate). Use the split to find which tired she is describing. Her report comes before any number.

When the pattern itself is the finding. Consistently broken sleep is a reason to hold volume rather than add it. Volume is fourth on the list when she stalls, behind asking whether she trains hard enough. It is not a reason to fix a target to her best or her worst night.

Where the cause is clinical. Night sweats from perimenopause are one example. The female body owns the cause and what changes in the program around it. This page owns the session-by-session read. It does not own the diagnosis.

Say it like this

We're not going to plan your week around your worst night or your average one. Each session, tell me how you slept and how you feel, and we'll set the day from that.

Terms on this page

neurological fatigue: The nerve side of being tired. The brain and nerves are not telling the muscles to do the right thing, and the person is not cognitively clear.

autoregulate: Adjust the session on the day to what the trainee brings to it, rather than to what was written in advance.
