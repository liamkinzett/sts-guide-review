Squat, bench and deadlift are not run on one day

The strength system never puts squat, bench and deadlift in one session. There are three reasons, and two exceptions.

Three reasons

# Systemic fatigue

sub: Three big compounds in one session

Why: Whatever comes later has to be dumbed down in intensity.

# Order bias

sub: Whatever goes last is shafted

Why: The last lift returns less than it should.

# The psychology

sub: One bad night's sleep ruins the whole week in her head

Why: Because the whole week was in one session.

The exceptions. A practice competition or competition testing (mock meet). And temporarily, to build trust with a client who is attached to the idea.

Terms on this page

mock meet: A practice competition run in the gym. Attempts, commands and the order of lifts all copy the real day.
