Motivation and adherence · T-44.7

If she will not do the extra thing, stop prescribing extra things

If she will not do the extra thing, stop prescribing extra things. Build the quality into the lifts themselves. Hide the vegetables in the spaghetti sauce.

The case is mobility homework. Around one client in ten actually does it. The response is not better homework. It is building the quality into the training itself, so that compliance is structural rather than requested.

The question this rule turns on. Is this something she has to remember? Or is it something the program does to her? Anything in the first column is a candidate for the second.

Compared

columns: She has to remember | The program does it to her

Mobility work: A routine she is asked to do on her rest day | A paused, deep split squat as her first accessory

Bracing practice: Breathing drills she is asked to do at home | A cue on the first set of every lift, every session

Walking: A step target she checks at night | A walk built into how she gets to the gym

Why it works. A behaviour that depends on her remembering competes with everything else in her day, and loses most days. A behaviour that lives inside the session happens every time she trains, because training is the thing she already does.

Watch for

Better homework. A nicer PDF is still homework. Move it into the session or drop it.
