Where each Tier 3 report goes

Seven of the ten Tier 3 faults are a movement felt in the wrong place, and the next page handles them. Two are pain and one is effort. Route those first.

Verdict

Shoulder pain on a front raise, or elbow pain mid-set: Pain rules and when to refer first.

It is pain. It is never a felt-in-the-wrong-place problem. The exercise page's escalation ladder and Region by region come after.
