Hamstrings · E-HAM-CURL#Execution

How to do it

Find the pad before she curls. Before any knee bend, she presses the thighs up into the pad, then contracts. The lifting half of the rep (concentric) starts with the hamstring pulling, not with a heave.

Hold a still joint position. The hips must not lift or shift to steal leverage.

Slow the lowering half deliberately (eccentric). She controls the descent into a deep stretch, then stops just short of a fully straight knee. Stopping short keeps tension on the muscle and stays out of the bunched-up position where the hamstrings cannot produce force (active insufficiency). Nothing about the rep is explosive.

Lying curl only. She drives the thighs into the pad rather than the hips. The hips sit in slight extension. Do not let her chase a glute squeeze; it reduces total output.

Terms on this page

concentric: The lifting half of a rep, where the muscle shortens under load.

eccentric: The lowering half of a rep, where the muscle lengthens under load.

active insufficiency: A muscle that crosses two joints and is shortened at both is too bunched up to produce much force. Bending the knee does this to the hamstrings in a hip thrust.
