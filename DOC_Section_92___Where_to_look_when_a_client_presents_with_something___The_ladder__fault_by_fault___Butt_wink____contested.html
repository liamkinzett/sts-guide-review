Butt wink

At the bottom her pelvis tucks under and her lower back rounds.

On a machine, stop the range above it. On a barbell squat, judge it by what it costs her.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Machine squat

sub: Pelvis rounds off the back pad. Hack and pendulum only.

Do this: Stop above it. On a back-supported machine this is always a fault. The pelvis leaving the pad means the lumbar (lumbar) spine loses support while the machine keeps driving load through it.

# Leg press and hip press

sub: The sled lowered past the pelvis. The tailbone peels off the pad

Do this: Stop the sled above it

# Barbell squat

sub: Pelvic flexion at depth

Do this: Judge it by what it costs her. Cue it and watch whether it changes. A fault that is not costing her anything is not a fault.

There is no set cue written for this fault. Say the fix in plain words, one thing this set.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Machine squat

Say: Get your hamstring on your calves and pause there.

Why: It names the depth standard and the pause in one instruction.

When: Machine squat

Say: Get in a position where we can drive our knees as far forward as possible while keeping full foot contact.

When: Machine squat

Say: Control that bottom.

Why: Used against the fatigue-driven dive-bomb rather than as a general tempo instruction. Over-cueing is endorsed on this movement.

When: Leg press and hip press

Say: Bracing allows for unlimited force output.

Why: Paired with the physical instruction to squeeze the handles and push the back into the pad.

When: Leg press and hip press

Say: Keep the torso static; drive the hips through the pad.

Why: Hip press only. It is what holds the movement at the hip rather than the knee.

When: Leg press and hip press

Say: Focus on deep, non-reactive breathing.

Why: Given for novices, alongside holding a stable pelvis. A setup instruction that works as a cue: "I'd like to go more hip width.

Terms on this page

lumbar: The lower back: the part of the spine between the ribs and the pelvis.
