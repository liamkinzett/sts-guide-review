Setup

Set her up in this order

Pulley at chest or waist height with a dual rope. She stands facing away from the column, hinging slightly forward. Angle the line of pull slightly backward rather than straight up and down.

Upper arms vertical and close to the head. They stay there for the whole set.

Squeeze the glutes and core before the first rep. The low back then cannot arch to buy overhead range.

Establish her comfortable limit of overhead reach before you load it (shoulder flexion). That limit sets the bottom of the rep. A textbook depth does not. The test is the overhead reach, read in order. Rib cage over pelvis, then the shoulder blade turning upward while staying down, then the joint finishing the reach. Overhead press, Watch for, has it in full.

Single arm overhead cable version. Pulley at the bottom, standing side-on to the stack with the working arm furthest from the machine. The rope is raised overhead with the shoulder and elbow locked out before the first rep.

A French press is a different setup. Its close double-overhand grip and flat-bench position belong to that exercise, and STS doesn't program the French press. Do not carry them over to the cable version.

Terms on this page

shoulder flexion: The arm travelling forward and up in front of the body, all the way to overhead.
