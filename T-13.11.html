Choosing exercises · T-13.11

Glute range is the one coaches misjudge most

Three positions, three movements. The middle row is the one coaches get wrong.

RangeMovement
LengthenedSplit-stance Romanian deadlift (B-stance). The split stance lets her hips shift sideways (lateral hip shift), which puts the glute in its lengthened position
MidTwo-footed Romanian deadlift (bilateral). Commonly taken for the lengthened slot. It does not fill it
ShortenedHip thrust variations

Read the middle row as a gradient, not a verdict. A two-footed RDL does load the glute at length. It is a position of real length through the glutes, and one she can load heavily. What it cannot do is reach the lateral hip shift. It leaves side-to-side movement (frontal plane) on the table, so it does not fill the lengthened slot. The three-position map holds.

Why the mistake matters. A coach who fills the lengthened slot with two-footed RDLs builds a program that never reaches that hip position, while believing the opposite. Glutes are the priority for most of our clients. That gap is expensive.

Terms on this page

B-stance: A split stance where the back foot sits behind on its toe as a kickstand and the front leg does the work.

lateral hip shift: The hips shifting sideways over the working leg as she lowers. A split stance allows it and a two-footed stance does not, and it puts the glute in its longest position.

bilateral: Both limbs working together, as in a two-footed squat or a standard Romanian deadlift.

frontal plane: Side-to-side movement, like a lateral lunge or a side raise.
