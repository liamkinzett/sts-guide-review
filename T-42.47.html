The coaching relationship · T-42.47

A check-in landing at 2am every week is data

A check-in landing at 2am every week is itself data. Name the pattern before you decide whether it is her business.

Why the timestamp matters. Sleep is a training variable, not a lifestyle one. A check-in that lands at 2am every week, without exception, is one of two things. A schedule fact, or a message about the sleep her training depends on. She works nights, she simply writes late, or she cannot sleep. The timestamp alone does not say which.

Noticing is not surveillance. A pattern she did not narrate herself is still yours to name. Her intake agreement licensed it. Naming it costs one line.

Naming the hour

I've noticed your check-in usually lands around 2am. Is that just how the week falls, or is something keeping you up?

Then the usual mechanism. Say what you noticed. Ask what is happening. Do not diagnose the hour yourself. Fatigue and recovery owns what to do with the answer if it is sleep. Training around life owns shift work.

Watch for

Deciding it is none of your business. The sleep is your business, because the training runs on it. Whether the reason is your business is what the question finds out.
