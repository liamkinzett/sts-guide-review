What she will ask you · T-47.5

"Won't lifting give me arthritis?"

Lifting is not what causes degenerative arthritis. Load the muscle, not the structure. This page answers a myth. It does not manage a diagnosis.

The mechanism, which is the part she needs. Done appropriately, you load musculature and not structure. Muscle that meets greater load over time adapts to deal with greater load over time. That is the whole point.

The failure case, named. When adaptation happens through bones and joints instead, through inflammation and calcium deposit, that is degenerative arthritis (degenerative arthritis). It is what happens when structure carries what muscle should have.

The one-liner for the uncle who blames squats. Saying deadlifting is bad for your back is the same as saying eating protein is bad for your kidneys.

On a scan that says wear and tear. The most helpful thing to know about disc or spinal degeneration is that the best thing for it is movement.

Say it like this

Lifting done well loads your muscles, and muscles get stronger under load. Arthritis is what happens when joints carry what muscle should have. Training is the thing that stops that, not the thing that causes it.

Where this page stops. Explaining why lifting is not the cause is inside your scope. Telling her what her joint can take is not. Where she actually has diagnosed arthritis, or a scan she is frightened of, Pain rules and when to refer governs. It forbids you to interpret the scan. Region by region carries the regional playbooks.

What you cannot give her. No comparison of arthritis rates in lifters against runners against people who do neither. The mechanism is what you hold.

Watch for

Reading her scan for her. The moment she shows you the report, you are past this page.

Terms on this page

degenerative arthritis: Joint disease where the joint surface wears and the body responds with inflammation and calcium deposits. It is a joint adapting to load that muscle should have carried.
