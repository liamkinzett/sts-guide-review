Building a session · T-12.15

A wall at a fixed time is read like an overrun

A session that reliably runs out of her at the same clock mark every time is not automatically a session-length problem. Check her execution first, exactly as for an overrun. Is she holding her rest periods? Or is the sixty-minute wall actually a ninety-minute session's worth of drift compressed into her own perception of an hour?

Once that is ruled out, it is a design constraint like any other. Sixty minutes may truly be all she has before her output drops. Then build to that number, the way any session is built to the time available. Place what matters most at the front. That way it is trained before the wall rather than after it.
