Rest and intensity techniques · T-14.12

Pre-fatigue is a technique here and an ordering decision elsewhere

The same arrangement appears twice, and the two rules answer different questions (pre-fatigue).

Building a session answers when. A client strong enough that her joints rather than her muscles are the limiting factor can be given an isolation before her main compound. The compound is then performed at a lower absolute load and still reaches failure. That is a judgement about the session's order.

This topic answers how. The pre-fatigue superset structure: isolation into compound, a fifteen-second transition, two to three minutes before looping.

They are compatible and they are not the same permission. Building a session does not license running pre-fatigue on every client because the structure exists here. This topic does not license reordering a session because the technique is available. Check both.

Terms on this page

pre-fatigue: Tiring one muscle with an isolation exercise first, so that it is the limiter in the compound lift that follows.
