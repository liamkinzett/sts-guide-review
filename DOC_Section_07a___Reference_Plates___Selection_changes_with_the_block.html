Selection changes with the block

In a block peak, which exercises she does changes with the block. That is the part a linear peak does not do. Variations early, the tested lifts in the middle, the tested lifts only at the end.

What she lifts in each block

Weeks 1 to 4 | 4 | Mostly variations: pause squat, tempo bench, deficit deadlift

Weeks 5 to 8 | 4 | The tested lifts become the main movement. Variations drop to second exercise

Weeks 9 to 12 | 4 | Tested lifts only, exactly as she will perform them on test day

Weeks 1 to 4. Mostly variations. Pause squat, tempo bench, deficit deadlift. Higher reps, plenty of tissue built, low joint stress. Deadlift variations covers the deficit pull.

Weeks 5 to 8. The tested lifts become the main movement. The variations drop back to the second exercise of the session.

Weeks 9 to 12. Tested lifts only, performed exactly as she will perform them on test day (competition variation). Commands, pauses, her stance, her grip.

The tested lifts carry their commands in the tempo, from the first week of the phase on the strength path. Squat 20X1: one second stood up. Bench 21X1: one second on the chest and one second locked out, because bench has three commands, start, press, rack. Deadlift 11X1: one second settled on the floor, enforcing the dead stop, and one second at lockout. Tempo and pauses has the rule.

Terms on this page

competition variation: The exact lift as it is performed on the platform, with her competition stance, grip and bar position.
