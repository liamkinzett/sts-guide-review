Muscle and growth · T-01.10

Can a client build muscle in a calorie deficit?

Whether muscle can be built while dieting depends on training status. Say that. Never promise it to a trained client.

Yes, muscle can be built while body fat is being lost. The more useful answer is: it depends who is doing the dieting. Building muscle and losing fat at the same time is called recomposition (recomposition), and it is real. It is common in beginners and in people coming back after time off. It is not something every body does.

Beginners and returning lifters. A beginner has a large amount of unused adaptive room. Someone returning after time off is also rebuilding adaptations she has held before. Both groups have more "room" to gain muscle even when she is eating below maintenance.

Trained lifters. The more advanced she becomes, the harder additional muscle is to build. For a well-trained client eating below her needs (calorie deficit), the primary goal is usually different. Lose fat while holding on to as much existing muscle and performance as possible. If extra muscle comes too, that is a bonus. It should not be promised.

Two sentences you will hear both hold. "You can gain muscle in a deficit." And: "a deficit is there to drop fat and hold muscle, not to build it."

The first is about what is possible for a new lifter. The second is about what a deficit is for. They do not have to disagree, and you can hold both.

What this means for coaching. It depends on where she is starting. Far more likely if she is new or returning than if she is trained. For a trained client, the deficit's job is fat loss while holding muscle. Growth is a bonus, never the promise. The next page explains why.

Say it like this

Whether you can build while we're cutting depends on where you're starting from. If you're new or coming back, quite likely. If you've been training hard for years, the aim of this phase is to drop fat and keep every bit of muscle you've got.

Terms on this page

recomposition: Building muscle and losing fat at the same time, so her body changes shape while her weight barely moves.

calorie deficit: Eating fewer calories than the body burns, so body weight falls.
