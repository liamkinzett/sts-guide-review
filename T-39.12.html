Reading her training data · T-39.12

A request for a named program is a request for variety

A named program pulled from social media is a request for variety with a source attached. It does not enter her program by being asked for.

The signal-and-noise table already puts a client's own request for variety on the side that changes nothing on its own. A named program is the same request, not a different category. What may be prescribed is governed by the STS exercise list, whatever the idea's origin. Nothing about her bringing the request herself opens a route around that.

The conversation itself runs on The coaching relationship's open-question method, whether you explain the standard or simply decline. Ask what she is hoping the program will give her before you answer. Where the real answer is boredom, Motivation and adherence says to engineer the novelty inside the plan before she goes looking for it outside.

Answering the request

What is it about that program that you're after? If it's a change, I can build that into what you're already doing.
