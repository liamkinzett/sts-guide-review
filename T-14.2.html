Rest and intensity techniques · T-14.2

How long she rests, by movement

Rest is set by the movement. Read the table by demand rather than by category.

MovementRest
Big compounds: squat, deadlift, bench, row3 to 5 minutes
Loadable movements her own trunk holds (internally stabilised)3 minutes
Machines, cables, isolation2 to 3 minutes
Working above 85% of her one rep max (1RM)As needed. Full recovery, which is often 5 or more

Read the table by demand rather than by category. A movement that is heavy, loaded through a long lever and held by her own trunk needs longer than a supported machine. That holds whatever either is called.

It is a default. If the rests look too long against the standard advice, the advice is what is wrong. The next page explains why.

Terms on this page

internally stabilised: Her own trunk and bracing hold the position under load.

1RM: The heaviest weight she can lift once with good form.
