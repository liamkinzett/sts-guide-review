When a client asks you one of these

When she asks one of these, separate the position from the mechanism before you answer. Give her both, and scope any claim that has another side. This page is the topic's rules in the order you use them.

Run it in this order

Test. Is it on this list? Twenty questions are, and six of them need scoping rather than a flat answer. Check which kind you are holding before you open your mouth.

Read. Separate the position from the mechanism from what has actually been shown. "Lifting stunts growth" is the worked example: the compression story is wrong. That is different from saying a measurement proved children who lift end up the same height. Only the first is yours to say.

Do. Give her the position. Give her the mechanism where one exists. Where the claim has another side, scope it: say for whom and at what dose the answer holds, and give her both sides.

Change. Almost nothing here changes a program. Spot reduction is the exception, in that you still run the exercise she asked for.

Escalate. Her food is the Nutrition Manual. Anything medical is Pain rules and when to refer. How she feels in her body, rather than what is true about bodies, is Body image and the referral line.

Watch for

Stating a scoped thing flat. "Lifting will never thicken your waist" and "heavy lifting is completely safe" both have a counter-example she can find. Scope them.

Answering the feeling as if it were the fact. "Will I get bulky" is sometimes a question about bodies and sometimes a question about hers. The second is Body image and the referral line.
