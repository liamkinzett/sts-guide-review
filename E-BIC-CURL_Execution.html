Biceps · E-BIC-CURL#Execution

How to do it

The rep, in order

Flex at the elbow only. The shoulder stays still. A slight lift of the upper arm appears at the very top and nowhere else (shoulder flexion).

Palm-up dumbbell curl: the palm faces straight up for the whole rep (supinated grip).

Hammer curl: a strict neutral grip held throughout (neutral grip), with the elbow locked so the shoulder cannot add momentum.

Past halfway, think of moving the forearm horizontally toward the biceps rather than lifting the handle upward. This is what stops her cutting the last 10 to 15 degrees of elbow flexion.

The lowering is not a return trip (eccentric). It is the phase where the muscle lengthens while resisting the load. Control it. Tempo and pauses sets the speed.

Terms on this page

shoulder flexion: The arm travelling forward and up in front of the body, all the way to overhead.

supinated grip: Palms facing up or toward her. On a curl it is the standard grip, and it puts the work on the biceps.

neutral grip: Palms facing each other, or facing in toward her body. On a curl it is the hammer grip, and it shifts the work to the brachialis and brachioradialis.

eccentric: The lowering half of a rep, where the muscle lengthens under load.
