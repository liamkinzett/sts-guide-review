Pain rules and when to refer · T-35.28

An aggravator outside the gym she will not remove

When the aggravator is her job or her running club and she will not remove it, the bucket method runs around it, not against it. Ask what she is doing between the aggravator and the gym.

The question is Region by region's, asked about her life instead of her desk. It was written for a desk job and a wrist. Nothing changes when the aggravator is a standing job or a running club she has already said she will not leave.

Compared

columns: Heel pain, on her feet all day at work | Shin pain, a running club she will not stop

Ask: What is she doing between the standing and the gym? | The same question, about the running

The finding: Usually nothing bridges the gap. That is the finding | The same finding

In the gym: The bucket sort still runs on the session | Whatever the session does not have to touch stays palliative or no effect. Gym volume that loads the same tissue the running already loads moves towards the provocative queue

What you do not do. Try to talk her out of the job or the running club. That decision is hers.

What you do. Treat the unaddressed activity as a fixed input, the way the intake already treats a high-stress period or a changed split. Program the sessions she does control around it rather than pretending it is not happening.

Why it works. A coach who waits for the causal activity to stop before doing anything ends up doing nothing. The bucket method exists so training does not have to wait for the whole picture to resolve.

Watch for

Naming what you think is happening to the tissue outside the gym. That is description for your own planning, never something you tell her is happening inside her foot or her shin.
