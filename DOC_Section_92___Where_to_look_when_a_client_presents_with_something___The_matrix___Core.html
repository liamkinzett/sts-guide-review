Pair: the peaks are in different places, so this is the same-muscle superset Building a session describes. Partial: the peaks overlap and also differ; usable, with less of the range advantage, once Choosing exercises' alignment and bracing screen passes. Redundant: both peak in the same place, the pairing Building a session forbids.
