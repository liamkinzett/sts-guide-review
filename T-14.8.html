Rest and intensity techniques · T-14.8

Myoreps go on low-fatigue isolation only

Complete the final full set, then move immediately into short, high-effort mini sets performed back to back with minimal rest (myoreps).

Restricted by movement. Calf raises, rear delts, side delts, pec deck. Movements where the local fatigue is high and the whole-body cost is near zero. Not on anything loadable or complex. There, the same protocol produces a fatigue bill that outlasts the session.

Record total reps per set, not reps per mini set. Range of motion degrades across myoreps, which makes any single mini set an unreliable number. The total is the metric. It is the only one comparable week to week, and Progression depends on it.

Watch for

Myoreps on a movement chosen because it was next in the program. The restriction is the rule, not a suggestion about what works best.

Terms on this page

myoreps: After the final full set, short high-effort mini sets performed back to back with minimal rest, on a low-fatigue isolation movement.
