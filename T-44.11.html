Motivation and adherence · T-44.11

Put the check-in on the day that polices her weak window

Put the check-in on the day that polices her weak window, and give it a deadline. The deadline is the part that carries the effect.

Less disciplined clients pick a Monday check-in, because it keeps them accountable over the weekend. The weekend is where their week falls apart, and a Monday form means the weekend is on the record.

The deadline does the work. Once there is a deadline and a set process around the check-in, compliance goes through the roof and results follow. Structure is where the benefit comes from.

Where you deviate. The day is chosen against the client's own weak window. Where her weak window is midweek, Monday is the wrong day. The rule is the principle, not the weekday.

Match the day to the window

# Her weekends fall apart

sub: The common case

Do this: Monday check-in. The weekend is on the record before it has faded.

# Her midweek falls apart

sub: Shift work, a Wednesday she dreads

Do this: Put the check-in on the far side of it. Thursday or Friday.

# She goes quiet on a fixed day

sub: The night she never trains

Do this: Check in the morning after. Ask about that night by name.

The coaching relationship owns the channel the check-in travels on and the response window she can expect. This rule owns the timing.

Watch for

Monday because everyone does Monday. Ask where her week breaks first.
