Rest and intensity techniques: what to say

Words for her on rest and intensity techniques, by situation.

A script is only right inside its rule's conditions. Read the rule in Rest and intensity techniques before you use the words.

Techniques come out in a cut.

Say it like this

We're actually pulling the intensity techniques out for this phase. While you're eating less, your recovery is the thing in short supply. The job right now is keeping what you've built rather than chasing more. Training harder here works against that, not for it.

Default to standard loading. Reach for blood flow restriction only where heavy loading is off the table, not as an upgrade on it.

Say it like this

That's a tool for when we can't load the joint properly. You can load it properly, so we're not reaching for it here.

Whether core work belongs in a rest period depends on the training goal. Do not answer it as a blanket yes or no.

Say it like this

We're not pairing your core work into the rest here. I need your brace fresh for the next set. In a fat-loss block it's a different call and I'll tell you.

She wants a heavy lift paired with an explosive one to prime it. STS does not build a session around that.

Say it like this

There's a theory that a heavy lift primes the next explosive one. I don't have a sourced answer on whether that actually holds up, so I'm not building your session around it.
