Chest · E-CHE-FLY#When to program it

When to use it

The problem you notice. She cannot feel her chest working and says so. Or a press bugs her shoulder and you would rather not drop it. Or her whole chest week is presses, so nothing she does loads the squeezed end. The pec fly answers each of these, and it sits after the presses.

Reach for it when

# She cannot feel her chest working

sub: She says so, and her presses look like arm exercises

Confirm it: Ask her where she feels a press. If the answer is shoulders and arms, the pec is not the limiter.

What it gets her: A pec she can find before you ask her to press with it. If someone is having trouble feeling her pecs, start with something like the cable fly. Pause in the contraction and get some blood pumped in.

# A press bugs her shoulder and you would rather not drop it

sub: The press is fine once the pec is already working

Confirm it: The press hurts cold. Try a fly first and re-test the press.

What it gets her: The same press at a lower absolute load, tolerable again. Start with a pec fly that feels okay, then go into the press that was bugging her. Now it feels okay.

# Her whole chest week is presses

sub: Nothing she does loads the squeezed end

Confirm it: List her chest exercises. If every one is a press, the squeezed end is untrained.

What it gets her: The range pressing cannot reach, covered. Presses give nothing at the squeezed end. On a cable or a pec deck this is the only chest movement STS programs that loads both ends.

# A decline press provokes her shoulder but you still want the lower-chest bias

sub: The arm path without the joint position

Confirm it: The decline press hurts. A high-to-low cable path does not.

What it gets her: The lower-chest arm path without the decline press's joint position. A decline arm path on cables is comfortable and safer at the shoulder than a decline press.

Which end a fly loads is a property of the machine

The pec deck loads the stretched end. The cable fly loads the squeezed end. They are not swaps for each other. Pick the one whose end you actually wanted. Where it's hardest has the detail.

Dumbbell flyes are ruled out, not tiered. No tension at the top, a poor profile through the arc, and extreme shear at the shoulder. There is no sanctioned home form of this movement.

Setup. Cables or a pec deck, handles at armpit height, back on the pad, ribs stacked. Setup has the list.
