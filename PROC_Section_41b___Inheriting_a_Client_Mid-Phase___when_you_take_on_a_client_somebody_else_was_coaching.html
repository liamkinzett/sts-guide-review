Taking on a client somebody else was coaching, start to finish

Nothing here is new doctrine. It is this topic's rules in the order you use them.

Start from the assumption that the program is defensible. Most of what looks wrong is a decision you cannot see. Reading it before judging it is the rule, not a courtesy. Look backwards before you look forwards, and read the audit page before the rules that follow it.

The five moves, in order

Test. Two passes over what she arrives with: backwards, then forwards. Then run the gate.

Read. Sort every finding into three piles. The pile decides the timing.

Do. Change the immediate pile now. Everything else one at a time, with the reason said out loud.

Change. After the immediate pile, the order is the one the audit produced, not your preferences.

Escalate. Medical, nutrition and conduct questions each have their owner.

1 · Test. Two passes over what she arrives with, in this order.

PassWhat you are asking
BackwardsWhat has she actually been doing. What did it produce. Which positions and ranges has she been living in
ForwardsDoes the written program break a rule with no stated deviation

Run the completeness gate on the inherited program exactly as you would on your own. Write down what is missing rather than filling it silently.

2 · Read. Sort every finding into one of three piles.

FindingPile
Breaks a rule and carries no deviation conditionImmediate
Not how you would have written it, breaks nothingLeave it
Missing rather than wrongRecord it, then close it in order

A default the previous coach deviated from is not a fault. The middle pile exists for exactly that reason.

3 · Do. Change the immediate pile now. Everything else moves one thing at a time, with the reason said out loud to her. The reason is not optional. A silent rewrite in week one reads to her as the last coach having been wrong. That is a claim you cannot support from a program you have read once.

4 · Change. After the immediate pile, the order is the one the audit produced rather than the one your preferences produce. Reading her training data governs which signals justify a change from here. Training around life owns a week that changed around her. Where the program is complete and you simply prefer another approach, nothing changes yet.

How long the first month runs, and what would justify a faster rewrite, is your call. A client who is actively deteriorating is the likely exception. Make the call deliberately and record it.

She has passed none of priming's gates: the movement screen, the exit criteria, the per-pattern classification. She is not re-primed. What stands in for those gates is not a call you make alone. Escalate it before you assume she has passed them.

5 · Escalate. Where this stops being a programming decision.

It stops being this topic whenWhere it goes
Anything medical, or any reason she should not be training at allPain rules and when to refer. Neither is a programming decision
She arrives already working with a physioShe keeps that plan. Support it rather than compete with it. Assessing a new client has the rule
What you inherited is a nutrition or a conduct question, not a training oneThe coaching relationship. You escalate, you do not improvise
The handover itself is the problem, not the programThe delivery guide and STS, not this topic
