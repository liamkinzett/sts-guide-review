Knee: what to change first, by presentation

Where the knee presentation is one we already know, the first lever is written down.

First lever by presentation

# Pain on any loaded knee bend

sub: The sort is the plan

Do this: Sort every movement into helps, no effect, hurts. Train the first two.

# Knee pain on leg extensions

sub: Ask about it. You will not see it

Do this: It is a fault you find by asking after the set. Then run the pain rules. Quads carries the leg extension's one injury-grade fault.

# Pain at depth in a squat

sub: Depth is what she owns under load

Do this: Quads covers how depth is set.

# Pain that changed after another injury

sub: The ankle or the hip is the likelier origin

Do this: Take the whole injury history, not just this one.

# A visible left-right difference

sub: Reduce the difference. You do not need the cause

Do this: Start her sets on the worse side and let it set the dose.

# The knee travelling inside the foot

sub: Depth decides whether it is a fault

Do this: See the page on knees falling inward (knee valgus) in this region. Knees caving above 90 degrees is a fault. Knees travelling in below parallel is not.

Terms on this page

knee valgus: Her knees fall inwards, inside the line of her toes, as she squats or stands up.
