Swaps

Swaps by situation

# Equipment missing

sub: No crossover

Swap to: Single arm cable rear delt fly; Dual cable rear delt fly; Reverse pec deck

Why: The three versions substitute for each other. Prefer a cable fly over the reverse pec deck. A cable lets you match the line of pull to her fibre direction. The machine fixes one angle for everyone. With no crossover available, the reverse pec deck is the machine option.

# Injury

sub: The standing version does not suit her shoulder

Do this: Change the pulley height rather than the exercise. The correct height is individual anyway. Do not force the hands further back. The range limit is where active shortening stops, which protects the joint as well as loading the muscle.

# Home

sub: No gym access

Why: STS doesn't coach home training, so there is no home swap to give.

This movement is a keep rather than a cut. In the worked case of shoulder discomfort the overhead press came out of the session while the single arm cable rear delt fly stayed unchanged. See Overhead press.
