How to do it

The pull, in order

The wedge. Take the play out of the bar (slack) with the lats. At the same time, push into the floor. Both happen before the bar leaves the floor, and together they are the wedge (wedge). It is a push and a pull together.

Hips and knees extend together. If the knees straighten first she gets shot forward, and the hips have to catch up on their own. The two are not synced.

Push the floor away rather than pulling the bar up. The deadlift is both a push and a pull.

Lockout: knees locked, hips push through. She stands straight, not leaned back.

Terms on this page

slack: The small amount of play between the bar and the plates before the bar is under tension. Taking it out means pulling until the bar is tight against the plates.

wedge: The braced position she takes before the bar leaves the floor: slack pulled out of the bar, lats on, feet pushing into the ground, so the whole body is tensioned before the pull.
