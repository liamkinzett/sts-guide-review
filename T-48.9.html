Practising a lift · T-48.9

Visualisation is a competitor's tool, and the day before is the wrong time

Visualising a lift is endorsed for competitors. Rehearsing it the day before the meet is not. For a general client, it is not something you prescribe.

Why it works. When she visualises the lift, she builds the association with the movement without accumulating any fatigue that would degrade it. It is rehearsal at no fatigue cost.

Why the timing matters. Lifters read about visualisation and rehearse exactly what they want to do the day before the meet. Visualising is fine. Gradually building the controlled aggression as the day arrives is fine. Doing it the day before accumulates arousal rather than skill, and she spends her nervous system before the platform.

Scope. This is a competitor's tool, for a lifter with a meet to rehearse. It is not prescribed for a general client, whose practice is the sets themselves.

Watch for

The competitor lying awake the night before running her openers in her head. That is arousal, not rehearsal. Move the visualising earlier and keep the day before quiet.
