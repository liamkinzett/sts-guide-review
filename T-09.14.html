Strength blocks run on percentages. Priming finds the weights

Inside a strength phase the prescription moves from reps in reserve to a percentage of a one rep max. That is the one place in the system where percentages govern.

The order matters. Reps in reserve is used through priming to establish what she can actually do on the main lifts. Those numbers become the maximum the strength block is calculated from. You do not open a strength block with a percentage of a figure nobody has verified.

Why it works. A strength block is planned in advance across several weeks. Planning needs a fixed number to plan against. Reps in reserve cannot do that, because it only exists once the set has happened. The trade is real. Percentages let you build the wave. They also stop tracking her on the day she sleeps badly. That is why they are confined to the phase that needs them.

A worked example. Priming ends with her heaviest clean squat triple at 90 kg. Her estimated max is worked out from that triple, the way the next page describes. The strength block then writes 85% for three, and she takes the weight off the estimate. If those sets move like 90%, the estimate is what you correct.

Watch for

Percentages leaking into hypertrophy work because a coach found them convenient. Outside a strength block they are a prediction where a description would do.
