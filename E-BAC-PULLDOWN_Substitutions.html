Swaps

When the pulldown is not available

# Equipment missing

sub: No pulldown machine

Why: A cable column and a handle is the floor. Purpose-built pulldown machines are a preference, not a requirement. A single-arm cable pulldown does the job on any column. STS doesn't program that version as its own exercise. Once she can hold the position on both arms, write the programmed version below.

Swap to: Neutral grip lat pulldown

# A difficulty step, which is not an injury swap

sub: She cannot organise the position on both arms

Why: Single arm before both. Both arms at once doubles the load the trunk has to organise, and most machines have no shoulder-width grip. A single arm lets her move herself to match her own arm path.

# Injury

sub: Her shoulder will not tolerate overhead loading

Swap to: Single arm cable high row

Why: First a single-arm cable pulldown at a comfortable range, off hand braced on a bench. Then the single arm cable high row. Then biceps and triceps isolation.

# Home

sub: No gym access

Why: STS doesn't coach home training, so there is no home swap to give.
