Adherence as a signal · T-39b.1

When a client does not adhere, the plan is the first suspect

A program she does not do is a program that does not work, whatever it would have achieved if she had. When she does not adhere, suspect the plan before you suspect her.

The failure mode is common enough to name. A coach writes a rigid plan. The client fails to follow it. The coach concludes the client is soft or undisciplined. STS rejects that outright. Real coaching means adjusting the plan, and yourself, to get a better response.

Why it works. It is the same instinct Cueing names about cues: suspect the cue before the athlete. A cue that does not land is usually the wrong cue. A plan that does not get done is usually the wrong plan for this client, in this life, this month.

The trade is real, even if nobody prints a percentage. A large share of a good program executed willingly beats a perfect program executed reluctantly. The first one gets done. It also gets done harder.

Compared

columns: The perfect program | The one she will do

What is on paper: Every variable exactly where you would put it | Most variables where you would put them

What happens: Sessions skipped, sets cut short, effort held back | Sessions done, and done hard

What she gets: What the paper promised, minus everything she did not do | What the program actually delivers, which is more

Extras she will not do are a different rule. Mobility homework, activation drills, anything she has to remember: if she will not do the extra thing, stop prescribing extra things. Build the quality into the lifts so adherence is structural. Motivation and adherence owns that rule.

Watch for

The word "compliance" doing the work of a diagnosis. "Low compliance" describes the logbook. It explains nothing. The pattern of what she skips is the diagnosis.
