A busy or timed station is not a missing machine

A rack under a time limit, or a bench nobody will give up, is present but rationed. Sequence around the queue and share the station, rather than waiting it out.

The substitution page was written for equipment that is absent or genuinely obscure. A rationed station is a different shape of the same problem.

Where a swap exists that STS programs, treat the rationed station exactly like a missing machine: a substitution problem the exercise page owns.

Where she wants to keep the lift itself, two moves come before any redesign.

Before you redesign anything

Sequence it first. Put the lift where she is freshest and the room is likeliest to be quiet, at the start of the session. Building a session orders by priority anyway.

Work in. Where sharing is realistic, she alternates sets with whoever is on the station (working in). She helps load and unload, remembers her own seat height and weight, and the handoff costs nothing.

Build supersets around one station. Supersets (superset) built around one station survive a busy floor. Supersets built around two do not. Rest and intensity techniques tells you which pairings share a station. Write for the gym you know is busy, not the gym the program would prefer.

Neither frees enough of the window. Treat it as a substitution problem: the lift's own ladder owns the swap. And bank the priority work first, in writing, so a session that runs out of clock still delivers most of its intent.

Working in covers the cooperative case. Where the other lifter will not share, do not stand and wait through her rest period. Swap the lift for the next rung on its ladder and move on.

Say it like this

If someone's on the rack, ask to work in. You take turns, help them change the plates, and keep your own numbers in your head.

Terms on this page

working in: Sharing a station with another lifter by alternating sets, each resting while the other works.

superset: Two exercises done back to back, with the rest taken after the pair rather than between them.
