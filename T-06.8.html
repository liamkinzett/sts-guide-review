Assessing a new client · T-06.8

No body fat number reopens the cut conversation

You never decide on her behalf that she has gained enough fat and it is time to revisit the cut. There is no threshold. She never agreed to one, and setting one privately is the same recommendation with a delay attached.

Builds end. They end when she wants something different, or when she has what she came for. Or when the training data says the block has run its course. All of that is normal phase management, and The phases covers it.

The conversation reopens in two situations. She raises it herself. Or the training data gives you an independent reason. Her performance is going backwards, or her recovery has broken down in a way the build is causing. Never because she crossed a number.

Why it works. Putting a date on it does not make it a different conversation. She will recognise it as the same one, and the trust you spent months building goes with it.
