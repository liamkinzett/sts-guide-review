Tendon pain at the elbow is positional, not load-dependent

Most people think tendon pain depends on load. At the elbow it usually depends on position. So scaling the position does not mean scaling the load.

The worked template. A skull crusher hurts. A rope pushdown does not. Same elbow extension, same load. The difference is the shoulder position, which loads the long head of the triceps in a less stable place.

So do two things at once. Keep training elbow extension in the shoulder position that does not hurt. At the same time, do mobility and stability work in the plane you cannot yet load. Weeks later, move the shoulder angle under load.

Pass where the player is going to be, not where he is. Build mobility and stability toward where you intend to load her, not toward where she is now.

Why it works. Changing the position is the first lever in the bucket method, and this is where that lever comes from. Reducing her weights is not the first move. Triceps carries the same mechanism on its exercise pages.

Say it like this

Same muscle, same weight, different shoulder position, and one hurts and one doesn't. So we keep the one that doesn't and build toward the other one. You don't have to go lighter.
