Assessing a new client · T-06.17

Until she proves her effort ratings, do not build on them

Less experienced lifters believe they are closer to failure than they are. Until she has shown you an accurate rating, anchor her prescriptions to load and reps. Not to reps in reserve (RIR) alone.

Our whole system speaks in reps in reserve, so this matters more than it looks.

How far out she usually is. Lifters asked to train at a set percentage of their max have averaged three to four reps in reserve on sets they called hard. Women, as a group, have rated their effort less accurately than men at some points on the leg press. Both point the same way for our clients. Effort and intensity carries the detail.

Then teach the calibration. The simplest test, once she is training, is the repeat test.

The repeat test

She stops a set she calls one rep short.

She rests sixty seconds.

She goes again at the same weight.

If she comfortably repeats it, she was not where she thought she was. Keep anchoring to load and reps, and keep teaching.

Terms on this page

RIR: Reps in reserve. How many more reps she could have done when the set stopped. Zero RIR is failure.
