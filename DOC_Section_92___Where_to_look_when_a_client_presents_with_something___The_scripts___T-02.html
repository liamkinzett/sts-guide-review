Strength and skill: what to say

Words for her on strength and skill, by situation.

A script is only right inside its rule's conditions. Read the rule in Strength and skill before you use the words.

Tissues adapt in a fixed order. The order explains most beginner coaching.

Say it like this

The first stretch moves quickly because your nervous system learns fast. Your tendons and bones take much longer, so we build the habit now and the load later.

Specificity is about the adaptation, not the appearance.

Say it like this

This doesn't look like what you're training for. It builds the thing that's actually holding you back, which is why it's the more specific choice.

Rate of force development is a separate trainable quality from maximal strength. It rarely earns its place against a look-better goal.

Say it like this

Jumps build a different quality to the one you're actually after. They're not bad for you. They're just not the best use of your time if the goal is size.

Hypertrophy and maximal strength are dissociable adaptations. Neither one causes the other.

Say it like this

Getting stronger and getting bigger are related. They're not the same dial. You can move one without much of the other.

A low-volume block of heavy doubles builds neuromuscular efficiency, not muscle. There is nothing left in reserve for hypertrophy to run on.

Say it like this

Your lifts went up because your nervous system got better at using the strength you already had under that heavy load. It's real progress. It's just not new muscle.

She asks about motor imagery, picturing the lift in her head. Answer with where she puts her attention while lifting instead.

Say it like this

That's a good question and I don't have a sourced answer on it yet. What I can tell you about is where you put your attention while you're actually lifting, which is related but not the same thing.
