Where the fit is wrong early, refunding is the named option

Where the fit is wrong early, refunding is the named option. Two worked examples, for two different reasons. This is the usual practice, and the reason it is not policy is below.

Two reasons to refund early

# It is simply not what she wanted

sub: Found in week one

What is done: A full refund in week one. If this isn't for you, it's not for you. No hard feelings.

Why: We do not want to take your money if it is not what you are looking for.

# She wants an approach you are not willing to coach

sub: A method you will not stand behind

What is done: Here's your refund. Best of luck. Please be safe.

Why: Continuing would mean enabling a behaviour.

This is a business decision, and STS's own refund position is not recorded in the Library. Do not read these two examples as an STS policy. They are here because the reasoning is worth having, not the terms. Before you promise a refund, take it to the head coach.

Why the reasoning is worth having. Both examples release her without a fight and without a verdict on her. The first protects her money. The second protects her safety and your integrity. Neither pretends the fit was her fault.

Watch for

Promising a refund to end an awkward call. You may be right. You still do not own the decision.
