Practising a lift · T-48.10

Pick the exercise that survives nobody watching

When you will not have eyes on her, pick the exercise she will execute well without you. Do not fight unsupervised form with cueing.

Remote coaching means most of her sets happen with nobody watching. Three things go wrong out of sight, and each has its own fix.

Three reasons form and compliance slip when you are not there

# The exercise needs supervision she does not have

sub: Selection is the fix

What happens: Some movements are easy to butcher unwatched. The good morning is one. Without eyes on it, you are going for the best bang for buck.

Do this: Give her the RDL instead of the good morning when you will not see it. Question every exercise from that angle: will she do this well alone?

# A drill she thinks is low-value

sub: She skips it

What happens: The second you are not watching, a drill she sees no point in is skipped. That is compliance, and it is predictable rather than a character flaw.

Do this: Design around it. Either make its purpose clear to her or replace it with something she will do.

# Fatigue takes away her reset

sub: The last reps, not the first

What happens: As she tires she loses the ability to reset and hold position. Form degrades across the set, not because nobody is looking, but because she is tired.

Do this: Watch the last reps rather than the first. The remote fix is filmed sets, whole, to the end.

The honesty check on remote effort is speed. The speed of the last couple of reps should be significantly slower than the speed of the first couple. If every rep moves at the same pace, the set was not as hard as it was logged.

When you do have eyes on it, in person or on every filmed set, the constraint relaxes. The higher-skill exercise is back on the table. That decision belongs to Choosing exercises. Reading the filmed set is Diagnosing from video.

Watch for

Blaming her attention. The three named mechanisms are the wrong exercise for unsupervised work, a drill she saw no value in, and fatigue taking away her reset. Start with those.

Skipped unmonitored work read as laziness. It is a signal about the program. Read it as one.

Say when you swap a lift for the unsupervised version

I'm giving you the RDL for this block. It's the one I know you'll nail without me standing there.
