When to use it

The problem you notice. You audit her push day and nothing in it gets her arm overhead. Or she cannot organise herself under a free press, and the bar path changes rep to rep. Or she kicks heavy dumbbells into position and is spent before the first rep. The overhead press answers all of them, on the right rung.

Reach for it when

# Nothing in her push day gets her overhead

sub: Five horizontal presses and no vertical one

Confirm it: Read the session. The worked example of the fault is "barbell bench, then dumbbell press, then flat machine press, then pec deck, then a dumbbell lateral raise". Five horizontal presses.

What it gets her: The shoulder blade turning upward under load (upward rotation), once per pressing day. The rule is plain: "I want to get into deeper shoulder extension and I want to get overhead on every pressing day". Shoulder extension is the arm travelling behind the body. A bench session needs an overhead press or a pulldown later in the same session. The bench pins the shoulder blade, and this restores upward rotation. Not optional. Do not put heavy lat rowing immediately before the pressing.

# She cannot yet organise herself under a free press

sub: The path changes rep to rep, or the lower back supplies the last of the range

Confirm it: Watch two sets. If the bar or the dumbbells travel a different line each rep, she does not own the position yet.

What it gets her: The overhead range trained anyway, on a machine. "most people do not have the proper stability to do an effective overhead press". The machine supplies what she has not built yet, and the pattern stays in her program.

# She trains alone and you want her at true failure

sub: A press she can fail safely

Confirm it: Nobody is there to take the bar.

What it gets her: Pins set to touch the bar at her true bottom range. She dumps the bar onto them rather than being pinned under it.

# Her side or rear delts are the lagging head, and the press is always first

sub: Order by what is lagging

Confirm it: Her program puts the press first every time, and the raises come after it, tired.

What it gets her: The lagging head trained fresh, on the highest neural drive. Raises first, press last.

# She kicks heavy dumbbells into position

sub: Spent before rep one

Confirm it: Watch the setup, not the set. The kick-up is an athletic feat of its own, done before the set that was supposed to be the stimulus.

What it gets her: A working set that starts fresh. A seated barbell press from the rack, or a Swiss bar (Swiss bar).

Taking the machine for stimulus is not the same as reading it as evidence

Seated, Smith and machine pressing each take away something she would otherwise have to control. Take the machine for the stimulus it gives. Do not read a clean machine press as proof she can press a free weight overhead.

Setup. Rib cage stacked over the pelvis, hands relatively close, elbow under the hand before the first press. Setup has the full list.

Terms on this page

upward rotation: The shoulder blade turning so its outer corner rises and the socket tilts upward. It is what lets the arm travel overhead.

Swiss bar: A multi-grip bar with parallel neutral handles set inside a rectangular frame.
