Box squat

The box does different jobs in different places, and its height decides which.

Six jobs for a box

# A depth regression

sub: A box at the depth she actually has

Do this: Set the box at the depth she has. Lower the box before you add load. Counterbalance box squat, then goblet box squat, then no box.

# A tension drill for the lifter whose joints move past normal range (hypermobile)

sub: Hover, do not sit

Do this: She holds tension in the muscle above the box before she is allowed to dive-bomb into her joints and ligaments.

# The step before a pause squat

sub: Remove the bounce, then restore it under control

Do this: Box squat, then pause squat, in that order. The box removes her reliance on the bounce out of the bottom of the squat (the hole). The pause squat restores it under control.

# The cranky knee

sub: The goblet box squat

Do this: Range restricted, knee still loaded, control of her centre of mass still trained.

# The pain-control variation

sub: For the week she feels good and wants load back

Do this: A five-second lowering, two-count pause front squat to a box. The exercise caps the load rather than her intent.

# The max-effort variation in the conjugate plate, for a competitive powerlifter

sub: Heavy every week without frying the tested movement

Do this: Box squat worked to a 3RM in week 1 and a 1RM in week 2 of the variety phase, then rotated out.

The rule that governs the conjugate use. Never work up to a max (max effort) on the same exercise more than two weeks in a row before week 9. The exercise rotates every one to two weeks, so she never stalls. That plate is for an advanced lifter who stalls on a linear program, and it needs the kit: bands, chains and boards.

When not to

A high box squat for a lifter who struggles above parallel. For a lifter who competes without supportive gear (raw lifter) it does not touch the actual cause. That is the pelvic and bracing problem (bracing) at the bottom. For a competitive powerlifter, the strength system rejects range-cut lifts (partials) used as fixes. The reasoning is the same: they solve a problem they are not causing. The conjugate box squat above is a different job, a heavy rotating variation rather than a fix.

Terms on this page

hypermobile: Her joints move past the normal range. She drops into deep positions easily and has to work harder to control them.

the hole: The bottom of the squat, where she turns around and drives up.

bracing: Filling the trunk with air and tensing the whole midsection so the spine stays rigid under load.

3RM: The heaviest weight she can lift three times with good form.

1RM: The heaviest weight she can lift once with good form.

max effort: Working up to the heaviest weight she can lift that day on the chosen lift, for a single or a triple.

raw lifter: A powerlifter who competes without a squat suit or a bench shirt.

partials: A lift done through part of its range rather than all of it, cut short on purpose by a box, a board, blocks or pins.
