Variations

The three holds are not the same brief. The plank hold and the RKC plank share a position and differ in what she is asked for. The side plank has three jobs of its own.

The three holds

# Plank hold

sub: The front plank, held for time

What changes: It stabilises against spinal extension under fatigue. The trunk is held at a single length and the demand rises with time under tension.

# RKC plank

sub: Same position, maximal tension instead of duration

What changes: The RKC plank is a plank with far more emphasis on the tension. The brief is to squeeze as hard as she can, not to hold as long as she can.

Who it is for: A lifter who loses trunk rigidity off the floor in a pull. That loss is the usual cause of a failed pull or lockout, rather than a weak muscle. The ab wheel does the same rigid-torso job.

Coach it: With the pull in mind. Breathe big and brace hard and maintain that position through the movement. Give it only after bracing has been taught, never instead of teaching it.

Duration: None. A plank's time standard does not transfer, because the point is tension, not time.

# Side plank hold

sub: Three jobs, so not simply a plank on its side

What changes: Side-to-side mobility with the midsection stable against shearing forces. Compressing the rib cage from the sides as preparation for overhead work. And the entry point to resisting the rib cage tipping sideways.

None of them is progressed by load. Progress by duration and by leverage. Volume and Effort and intensity govern how much and how hard.
