Setup

Set her up in this order

Stand tall, feet hip width. Shoulder blades set back and down into a stable position before the first rep. Comfortable rather than maximally pulled back. They stay there all set.

Elbow set level with the torso line and locked there.

Run the external-rotation screen before you choose between two arms and one (external rotation). Watch for has it. Where it fails, curling with both arms is contraindicated and she goes single-arm. That is a permanent decision, not a temporary one.

For a standard palm-up dumbbell curl, work single-arm and rotate the torso slightly outward. Palm-up is the standard curl grip (supinated grip). The forearm then does not travel diagonally, and the palm can face straight up.

Brace with the opposite hand on a post or the machine frame. That resists the sideways lean of a single dumbbell and locks the torso angle.

The alternating curl gives up steps 4 and 5. The fix for a diagonal wrist path is to work single-arm, turn the torso slightly outward and brace the free hand. An alternating curl holds a dumbbell in each hand. There is no free hand to brace with, and no reason for the torso to turn. She is running the two-arm setup and taking one arm at a time.

So alternating buys rest between reps for each arm, and forfeits both setup corrections. Choose it where the wrist path is already clean. Where it is not, the answer is single-arm. Alternating does not change that answer.

Watch for

An alternating curl written as the answer to a failed screen. It is loaded on both sides at once. The screen's answer is single-arm, and the single-arm setup is one dumbbell, torso turned, free hand braced.

Terms on this page

external rotation: Turning a limb outward in its socket: the upper arm at the shoulder, so the palm turns to face forward or up, or the thigh at the hip, so the knee points more to the side. On the screen it is what she does with the arms held straight out in front.

supinated grip: Palms facing up or toward her. On a curl it is the standard grip, and it puts the work on the biceps.
