Elbow: assessment starts outside the gym

Look at the positions she is in most of the day before you look at the lift. For the elbow this is the whole first step.

The named mechanism. A desk and a mouse produce claw hands: wrists bent, palms down, all day. Then a movement demands full elbow and wrist extension overhead. The tissue was never bridged from one position to the other. Ask her what she does between leaving the desk and loading the bar.

Her report already tells you which positions provoke it. Which lift hurts, and which version of it, is the finding. You do not need to re-provoke it, and you never put hands on the joint to test it.

Named anti-pattern: security through obscurity

Going global and esoteric before looking at the joint that hurts. Her elbow hurts. Look at the elbow. Then look one joint above it and one below.

Say it like this

Before we talk about the gym, walk me through a normal day. Where are your hands most of the time? That's the position your elbow has been trained in.
