Knee: where the general rules do the work

The knee has no red flag list of its own. The general triggers and the two numbers govern it, as they govern every region except the low back.

A knee after surgery. We hold no return protocol for it. Her surgeon and physio set the plan, and you program inside their clearance. Returning to training covers how you load a cleared client.

The clinical sorting sequence for a knee is a clinician's, not a coach's. You will not find it here. What you have is the sort, the load-matching test, the foot check and the first levers. That is enough to build a program that does not provoke her while the clinician does the rest.
