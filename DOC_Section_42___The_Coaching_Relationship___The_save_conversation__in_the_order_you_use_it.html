The save conversation, in order

A client with one foot out the door gets four of this topic's rules, in this order. Nothing here is new. The order is the whole assembly.

The save conversation

Ask, do not defend. Open with the question about the goal, not a case for staying. "Are you happy with where you're at" answers more than any pitch you could make.

Read what she actually brings. If the disengagement has shown up as missed or thin check-ins, that is not something to chase. It is information about the relationship, arriving on schedule.

Say what you have noticed, out loud. Name the pattern plainly. Ask what is going on. Once, properly, rather than three times softly.

Decide whether it should be saved. The test is your own intention, not the outcome. A save attempted because losing her stings is a different act from a save attempted because the fit is still there. Only the second is coaching.

Why the order matters. Defending first makes her defend back. Chasing first teaches her the check-in was yours to carry. Deciding first, before she has spoken, decides on an unknown. Ask, read, say, decide.

Watch for

A pitch. The moment you are selling, you have stopped coaching. If the fit is there, the question shows it. If it is not, no pitch fixes that.
