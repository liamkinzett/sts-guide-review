Glutes · E-GLU-STEPUP#Where tension peaks

Where it's hardest

Across the range

title: Where it's hardest

note: Off the floor, in the midrange of hip extension (hip extension)

from: Foot on the box, standing on the floor

to: Standing tall on the box

mark: 20

label: Hardest

The step-up is hardest at the transition off the floor, and the external load peaks in the midrange of hip extension. The glute is at its longest (lengthened) at the start. The load is heaviest a little way in, once she is committed to the working leg and the trailing foot leaves the floor. Where the load peaks is what decides exercise selection and where a pause goes. See Tempo and pauses.

Terms on this page

hip extension: Opening the hip so the thigh travels behind the body line. On a step-up the glute does it to lift her onto the box.

lengthened: The point in the rep where the working muscle is at its longest while it carries the load. It describes the muscle's length, not whether she feels a stretch.
