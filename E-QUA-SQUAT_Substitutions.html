Swaps

The five-rung teaching ladder is the swap structure for this movement. It doubles as the route back to load. A complete beginner starts at the first rung. She builds at one rung, over time and load, before she moves to the next.

The ladder, most knee-dominant first

Counterbalance squat. A light plate held at shoulder height at arm's length. Stance narrower than her low-bar stance. Ribs tucked, tailbone underneath, knees travelling forward over the toes, torso fully upright. Deliberately the most knee-dominant point on the swing between knees, hips and back. STS uses it as a teaching rung, not as an exercise it writes into a program.

Goblet squat. The load sits closer to her base, so it can be heavier. Elbows under wrists, stance slightly wider. Ribs tucked. Start the rep with the knees.

Barbell front squat. Front rack or crossed arms, stance slightly wider again. Ribs tucked, elbows driven up. Start by bending the knees. Sternum high out of the hole.

High bar barbell back squat. Bar on top of the traps, elbows under the wrist position. Unrack with the legs. Ribs down, core tight. Bend the knees to start rather than sitting back from the hips first.

Low bar barbell back squat. Narrower grip. Do not let poor shoulder mobility push her hands out to the collars. Squeeze the shoulder blades to build the shelf between the rear delts and the top of the shoulder blade. Ribs down, pelvis underneath. Knees and hips start together. Lats tight, chin back, elbows forward.

Where the ladder ends up. At low bar the torso sits in the position of a trap bar deadlift. Keep progressing along that line and the squat turns into a deadlift. That is why low bar is the last rung, not a place to start.

When the barbell squat is not available

# Equipment missing

sub: The rack is busy or the bar she needs is not there

Swap to: SSB squat; Barbell front squat; Goblet squat

Why: In that order, where the rack has a safety squat bar. Read the note on the SSB below before you use it.

# No barbell option works

sub: Nothing with a bar is available or tolerable

Swap to: Hack squat; Pendulum squat; Smith machine squat

Why: The machine-squat family carries the quad stimulus. There is a whole scale of machines below the squat and the deadlift. See the Machine squat page.

# A joint says no

sub: A cranky knee, or a lower back that is the limiter

Swap to: Goblet squat; Hack squat; Pendulum squat; Smith machine squat

Why: A cranky knee: a goblet squat to a box. It restricts range while still loading the knee and still training control of her balance point. STS does not write the box version as its own exercise, so program it as a goblet squat with the range limit. A lower back that is the limiter: a hack, pendulum or Smith machine squat. She can move there entirely without losing quad stimulus. Pain is Pain rules and when to refer.

# Home

sub: No gym access

Why: STS coaches clients who train in equipped gyms, so there is no home swap to give.

The safety squat bar

The SSB squat is an upper-back and erector movement, not a quad one. A lifter with strong erectors tucks the rib cage down and holds the lower back neutral. The load then lands on the erectors of the upper back. That is what the bar is for, and it is a very good exercise for building upper-back strength.

The handles sit in front of her, and with gravity they put her in a position she can hold without much body awareness. The bar encourages the spine to stabilise rather than demanding it first.

Pick it by the test, not by default. The test is whether her upper back is a weak point. Where it is not, leave the bar on the rack. Its second job is insurance: it trains the rounded-over position, so a bad rep on a straight bar meets a position she has already trained.

Shoulder mobility that will not tolerate low bar is not a reason to reach for it. Try two things first. The setup's own accommodation: hands as close together as her shoulder mobility allows, never forced out to the collars. Or a step back to high bar on the ladder, which asks less of her shoulders for the same reason. Reach for the SSB only where her upper back is the actual target.

One fault is specific to this bar

She pushes up on the handles to start the rep. That removes the load her upper back was meant to resist. She arrives at the bottom with the erectors unloaded, which is the opposite of the reason the bar was chosen.
