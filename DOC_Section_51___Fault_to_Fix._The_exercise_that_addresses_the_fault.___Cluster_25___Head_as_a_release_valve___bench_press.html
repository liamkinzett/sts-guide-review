Head as a release valve on the bench: the touch point

The head lift is downstream of touching too low. Raise the touch point and set her eyes under the bar at the unrack. It is not a triceps problem.

Head as a release valve on the bench

# Head lifts or pokes forward as the bar reaches the chest

sub: Setup

What you see: Her head comes off the bench as the bar arrives, and she tries to hammer the triceps at lockout.

Why it happens: She is dumping the bar too low. The head lifts to release tension the shoulder blades should be holding.

Do this: Touch the bar down a little higher. Set the eyes under the bar at the unrack and keep the head down for longer.

Not a triceps problem

Do not prescribe triceps work for a head that lifts. The fault is where the bar lands. Fix the touch point.
