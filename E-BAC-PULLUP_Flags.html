Watch for

  • The chin-up's shorter bottom range is not a fault. The palms-facing grip stops the shoulder blades turning upward, so the chin-up cannot fully unlock the shoulder at the bottom. Do not force a dead hang there. Both ranges are correct for their own movement.
  • The pull-up is a skill that sits on three tiers of prerequisites. Treat it that way rather than as a lift to be attempted and failed.
  • A separate gate applies. She needs enough relative strength to bodyweight to learn the skill under meaningful load.
  • A failing pull-up may be a programming gap elsewhere. One client's pull-up fixed itself once her rowing and hinging loads were high enough. Check the rest of the week before rebuilding the pull-up. See Building a session.
  • Straps are refused on this movement. They remove the grip demand the pull-up carries. Build the grip instead.
  • Who to modify for. Wrist or elbow discomfort under a palms-away grip goes neutral. Front-of-shoulder pain regresses the assistance rather than the range. Pain itself is Pain rules and when to refer. Anything about her being a woman is The female body.
    • The three tiers under a pull-up

    Foundation. Trunk and shoulder-blade control drills. The 90-90 curl-up, the scapula dip, the scapula push-up and the serratus wall rollout. Scapula is the anatomical name for the shoulder blade (scapula), so the two scapula drills move only the blades. STS doesn't program these as exercises; use them to teach the positions.

    Middle. The hanging scap raise, which is STS's form of the scapula pull-up and scales with the heels on a bench. Then an ab rollout. Then a supported dumbbell row loaded in the opposite hand, which teaches the diagonal sling from one lat to the opposite glute (posterior oblique sling). Then a neutral grip lat pulldown, legs against the thigh pad. She leans back slightly so the arms travel in the plane of the shoulder blade (scaption). The rollout and the supported row are teaching rungs rather than programmed exercises.

    Top. Band assistance at the bottom of the rep and negatives from a box. Then the hanging knee raise, where the feet are lifted by extending at the shoulder rather than by flexing the spine.

    Terms on this page

    posterior oblique sling: The diagonal line of muscle and connective tissue running from one lat, across the lower back, to the opposite glute. A row loaded in the hand opposite the front foot trains it.

    scaption: Raising the arm in the plane of the shoulder blade, between straight out to the side and straight in front. On a pull-up the elbows follow that curve rather than a flat line.

    scapula: The shoulder blade. The flat bone on the back of the rib cage that the arm hangs from.
