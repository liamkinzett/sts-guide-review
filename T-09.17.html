Effort and intensity · T-09.17

Tell her she is not training hard enough off the footage

The verdict that she is not training hard enough is never an opinion. It is delivered off her own footage, shown to her, with two ways out.

The sequence that must survive

Show her the footage. Her last two reps moving at the same speed as her first two. The footage is objective and it is shared before the verdict lands.

Name the verdict. The footage shows she is not close to failure.

Give her the two ways out. Heavier, or more. She leaves holding a lever rather than a judgement.

Why it works. An opinion about her effort invites a defence of her effort. A picture of her own bar speed does not. It is the same verdict problem The coaching relationship describes, wearing gym clothes.

Effort talk only works against a felt anchor. Before you can tell her a set was not hard, she has to know what hard feels like. Calibrate the anchor first. The STS version of that move is the leg press as-many-reps-as-possible set (AMRAP): a machine, a question attached, never a compound. Once she has felt twenty reps at a weight, her set of eight to ten at that weight is plainly too light.

Say it like this

You just got 20 reps with that weight. So your working set of 8 to 10 at that weight isn't close. Put the weight up.

Where the shortfall is priority rather than effort, the words are different. The coaching relationship carries the openers for a client who is not making the time.

Register. Key the words to the client. The message does not move. What must survive any softening is the sequence: the footage shown, the verdict named, the two ways out.

Watch for

Delivering the verdict without the footage. A client told she is not training hard enough on your say-so has been handed something to argue with. A client shown her own last two reps moving at the same speed as her first two has not.

Terms on this page

AMRAP: As many reps as possible. She keeps going until she cannot complete another clean rep, so it is a set to failure by definition.
