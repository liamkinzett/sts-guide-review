Pain rules and when to refer · T-35.7

Two numbers that escalate a case: how bad, how long

Two numbers send a case to a clinician: seven out of ten reported consistently, or pain still consistent between three days and one week. These are referral thresholds. They are not the training-through ceilings.

Red flags

title: Escalate to a referral when either is true

Intensity: seven out of ten or higher, reported consistently

Persistence: still consistent between three days (72 hours) and one week

Consistently is the operative word. One seven in the moment she tweaks something is a reading, not a pattern. The clock runs in both directions, so a single day tells you little.

Pain out of ten: which number does what

Ceiling for impact work, diagnosed and cleared client | 3 | Hopping, jumping, running

Ceiling for resistance training, diagnosed and cleared client | 5 | During or immediately after

Referral threshold, any client | 7 | Reported consistently

Two numbers, two homes, two decisions

Do not let seven leak into the training-through decision. Later in this topic, under Loading a cleared client, you will meet two other numbers: five and three. Those are ceilings for a client with a diagnosis and clearance.

A client cleared to train stops at five. A client reporting seven is being referred. That is a different question with a different number.
