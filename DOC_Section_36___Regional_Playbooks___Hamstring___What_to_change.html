Hamstring: what to change first, by presentation

Where the hamstring presentation is one we already know, the first lever is written down. Sort every movement into helps, no effect and hurts, and build from what does not hurt.

First lever by presentation

# Pain at the top of the hamstring, near the sit bone

sub: Refer. Tendon pain at an attachment is a compression problem

Do this: Suspected tendon pain where compression at the attachment is the aggravator (insertional tendinopathy) is a referral, not a loading plan.

# Pain in the belly of the muscle, mid-thigh

sub: More likely muscular

Do this: The sort. Expect the hinge pattern in the provocative bucket.

# It only appears on deep hip flexion

sub: Reduce range before load

Do this: The lever order. Keep the load and the pattern, shorten the range to what is painless.

# It appeared after a volume jump

sub: The intervention is the volume, not the exercise

Do this: Take the history. Put the volume back where it was.

# Cramping in the hamstring during curls

sub: A fault you find by asking, not by watching

Do this: Ask after the set. Hamstrings carries it as an ask-about observation.

Terms on this page

insertional tendinopathy: A tendon problem where the tendon attaches to bone. It is made worse when that attachment is compressed, such as at the bottom of a deep stretch.
