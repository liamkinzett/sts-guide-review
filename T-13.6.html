Choosing exercises · T-13.6

Her execution decides which muscle an exercise trains

The plan says a rear-foot-elevated split squat is glute work. It is glute work if she hinges at the hip and descends into the bottom. Performed upright, the same exercise loads her quads.

The name in the program is a claim about what will happen. Her execution decides whether it does.

Why this matters more than it sounds. Volume has you total her working sets per muscle (hard set) before you assign the program. That arithmetic assumes every exercise lands where the plan put it. If half her glute volume is being performed as quad work, the audit says fifteen sets and her glutes received eight.

So the check is the footage. You already watch her video for mobility and for effort. This is the third thing to look for. It is the one that silently invalidates the volume plan.

Watch for

A client whose glutes are not responding on a program that looks correct on paper. Before you add sets, watch her perform the ones she has.

Terms on this page

hard set: A working set taken within three reps of failure.
