Common faults

Two common faults

# The grip escapes the demand

sub: Fingertip grip on a hanging raise. The grip ends the set before the abs do

Why it happens: Her hands are the weakest link in the chain.

Do this: Straps. Or the captain's chair.

# Hip-flexor dominance

sub: You ask about this. You do not watch for it

Why it happens: The pelvis never tucks, so the hip flexors lift the legs and the abs never shorten. She reports a pinch in the hip crease rather than work in the lower abs.

Do this: Reduce the range. Change the version.
