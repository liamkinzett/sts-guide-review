Reading her training data · T-39.7

When her report and the data disagree, get a third reading

She says the week was great. Every number says otherwise. You do not pick a side. You get a third reading.

A third reading is any observation that neither of you is producing from memory. Two of them are already built into how STS coaches.

Where her report and the data disagreeWhat STS does
She logs a set as near-failure and it was notShe rests sixty seconds and repeats the set. The repeat is the third reading
A client hides fatigue and cannot report it honestlyYou stop relying on her report for that variable, and decide her easier weeks yourself from what you can see

Neither resolves the disagreement by deciding who is right. One produces a new observation. The other stops relying on the report at all, for that client, for that variable. Both refuse the choice you feel invited to make.

Why picking a side is the error. Deciding the data is right makes her a liar. An accusation invites a defence, and you lose the information you were after. Deciding her report is right discards the only independent evidence you have. Either way you have turned a measurement problem into a relationship problem.

What a third reading looks like. The (repeat-set test). The video, where you watch the speed of the last reps. The logbook trend across weeks rather than her account of one. The data is read before the conversation, and this is the same instinct applied when the two collide.

Then sort what you found. A genuine mismatch between report and data is a signal, and it obeys the change rule: one thing, the smallest that could work. A mismatch is not by itself a reason to change the program. It is a reason to stop trusting one input until you have a second.

Where it turns out not to be a data problem at all. A client whose report is consistently sunnier than her numbers may be managing your opinion of her rather than misreading her week. Motivation and adherence owns the hidden constraint and the words you use. The data pull, and its warning against running it as a gotcha, governs here too.

Terms on this page

repeat-set test: She rests sixty seconds and repeats a set she called one rep short, at the same weight. If she comfortably does, the set was not as hard as she logged.
