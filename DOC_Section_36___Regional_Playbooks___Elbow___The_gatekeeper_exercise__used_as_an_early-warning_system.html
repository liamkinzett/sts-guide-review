The single arm overhead triceps extension as an early warning

Keep the single arm overhead triceps extension in her program as an alarm, not as a strength lift. If pain starts there, you know what is coming down the track.

Why it works as an alarm. It puts the shoulder in a structurally unstable position and tests the elbow at full overhead range. Training only with the arm at 90 degrees means you are missing 90 degrees of potential information.

The load is not the point. Nobody cares whether her overhead extension goes from 45 to 60 pounds. It screens for what will cost the bench press in six or eight weeks. Take it out of the program and the alarm sounds much later.

The version we program. The single arm overhead cable tricep extension, which carries the same overhead position.

Watch for

Dropping the movement because it is the first thing to hurt. That is the alarm doing its job. Treat pain appearing overhead first as the early warning it is, not as a reason to remove the movement.
