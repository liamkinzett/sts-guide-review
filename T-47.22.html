What she will ask you · T-47.22

"My influencer says the opposite"

Where two trusted sources conflict, or her influencer contradicts what you coach, answer the specific mechanism being claimed. Never ask her to pick an authority to trust.

When two sources you trust conflict. Stop picking a side and go looking for the principle underneath. Maybe both of them are wrong. Find the principle so you can understand it rather than emulate someone.

Why one paper or one influencer does not flip the practice. The best available findings, coaching experience and what she values are three pillars, and they are not equal ones. New information refines where a rule applies and where it does not. It is not good or bad. It is appropriate or not, for her.

The conversation with her. Take the influencer's specific claim seriously and answer it on mechanism, not on credentials. "Should I change it up" is the worked example. Muscle confusion is answered by explaining that skill in an exercise is what lets it be pushed. It is not answered by saying who is more qualified.

Say it like this

Let's not argue about who's right. What's the actual reason they're saying that works? Let's test that.

Answering a claim from elsewhere

Ask what mechanism is being claimed. Not who said it. What they say happens in the body.

Test the mechanism against what you know. Does it hold? For whom? At what dose?

Give her the answer to that. She leaves understanding a mechanism, not having chosen a side.

Why it works. Asking her to choose between you and her influencer makes you one more voice. Answering the mechanism makes you the one who explained it. Only the second survives the next video she watches.

Watch for

"They're not qualified." True or not, it is an appeal to authority, and it is the exact move you are trying not to make.
