Scheduling cardio around lifting for the same muscles

Cardio done a few hours before lifting costs the lift, in the muscles that did the cardio. The cost is over inside a day. Separate cardio and lifting for the same muscle group by a day. Failing that, put the cardio beside a session for something else.

Where does her cardio go?

? Does the cardio use the same muscles as the lift?

no: Put them wherever she likes. The chest does not pay for a run.

? Is there a full day between them?

yes: Fine. The muscles have recovered.

= Move one of them. Same-day cardio for the same muscles costs the lift.

What was measured. Trained men did four sets of leg press and bench press at about 75% of their max. They lifted at set times after a cardio bout on the legs.

Lifting after leg cardio, by the gap

# 4 hours later

sub: Leg press volume down. Bench unchanged

What it means: The legs that did the cardio cannot yet do the same lifting work. The chest never did the cardio and is untouched.

# 8 hours later

sub: Leg press volume still down. Bench unchanged

What it means: Same-day separation is not enough for the muscles that did the work.

# 24 hours later

sub: Leg press fully recovered. Bench unchanged

What it means: A day is the gap. Cardio on Monday morning does not cost the squat on Tuesday.

So this is a scheduling decision, not a fitness one. A client who runs in the morning and squats at lunch is not undertrained. She is mis-scheduled.

If they must share a session. Lift first only when maximal lower-body strength is the goal. Otherwise the order is hers. Separating them beats sequencing them: different days, or several hours apart. Steps and cardio holds that rule.

Aerobic fitness helps between sets. A fitter trainee clears acid and refills their fast fuel (phosphocreatine) faster. Denser mitochondria, more capillaries and more oxidative enzymes do that work. The page on rest covers what the rest itself restores.

Watch for

Treating the hours as exact. The shape is firm: same-day costs, next-day clear. The exact hours came from trained men doing one kind of cardio. Read them as a guide.
