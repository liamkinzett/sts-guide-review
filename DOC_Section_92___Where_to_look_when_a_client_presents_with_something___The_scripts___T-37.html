Returning to training: what to say

Words for her on returning to training, by situation.

A script is only right inside its rule's conditions. Read the rule in Returning to training before you use the words.

Pain-free is not the same as ready. The absence of a symptom is not a qualification.

Say it like this

Good, that's the first thing sorted. Now we build back up to it properly, because feeling fine and being ready to load it are two different things.

Returning after illness is not the same decision as returning after a layoff. She does not pick up where she left off and you are not the one who decides she is well.

Say it like this

You've been unwell, so we're not testing anything this week. Ask your GP before we go back to normal loading.

You are not refusing to train her. You are refusing to be the person who decided she was ready.
