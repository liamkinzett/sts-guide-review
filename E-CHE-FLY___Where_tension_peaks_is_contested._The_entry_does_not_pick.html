Where the pause goes

Load the stretch. Take the squeeze if the machine gives it to you free. That is the priority, and it comes from Choosing exercises: the mid and stretched range every week, the squeezed end optional.

You will hear two places named for the pause, and they sound like they disagree.

Compared

columns: The deep stretch | Peak contraction, hands together

The case: Lower under control to a deep stretch, pause briefly, sweep back | With cables the tension is continuous. It can be made to peak at the squeezed end

What it is really about: Where you deliberately load | What a cable makes available

They answer different questions. The second is about what a cable makes available. Continuous resistance means the peak can be placed at the squeezed end. That is the argument for cables over dumbbells. The first is about where you deliberately load. Nothing stops a single rep doing both. Lower to a deep stretch, then sweep to a squeeze the cable is still resisting.

So the question is which end gets the priority, and the answer is the stretch. Take the squeeze when the machine gives it to you free. On a pec deck the stretch is where the machine is hardest anyway. On a cable fly the squeeze comes free and the stretch is what you have to protect.

The one thing every version agrees on: the lowering half is controlled. How long any pause lasts is Tempo and pauses' count. No pause length is set on this page.
