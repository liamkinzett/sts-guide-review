The lower session, slot by slot

A strength-system lower session runs six slots in a fixed order: main lift, second pattern, unilateral, two isolation slots, then trunk. Every accessory gets two sets, each taken within one to two reps of failure.

The lower session in order

Main. Squat, one to two top sets (top set).

Second pattern. A hinge. RDL or stiff-leg deadlift.

Unilateral. Lunge or split squat (unilateral).

Isolation. Leg extension.

Isolation. Hamstring curl.

Trunk. Core work, or a carry. The suitcase carry is the system's stated favourite.

Rest between sets comes from Rest and intensity techniques, as for any session.

Why it transfers. The shape is squat, hinge, unilateral, isolation, trunk. It is a category ladder, not a list of exercises. The programming echoes a lot of what bodybuilders do.

The trunk slot. The suitcase carry is the system's favourite there, and the Loaded carry page in Core teaches you to coach one. STS does not yet program the loaded carry, so fill the trunk slot with core work, the slot's other option. Where a client already carries, coach it from that page.

Terms on this page

top set: The heaviest set of the exercise that day. Back-off sets follow it at a lighter load.

unilateral: One limb doing the work, as in a split squat or a single-arm row. A lunge or a split squat is unilateral; a back squat is not.
