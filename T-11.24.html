Spend a fifth day on the exposure that is short

Adding a day is not automatically adding work. Run the frequency arithmetic first. If a muscle's weekly band already needs three exposures (exposures) and her four-day week only gives it two, the fifth day spends itself there. It spreads existing volume across an extra session rather than adding sets.

Check

Run the arithmetic. Is any muscle's weekly band asking for more exposures than her four days give it?

yes: The fifth day is an exposure fix. Spread that muscle's existing sets across it. Do not add sets.

no: The fifth day is a genuine opening. Spend it on the goal bias, or run it as a specialisation day.

Where every muscle's band is already served at the current frequency, the fifth day is a genuine opening. Spend it the way the week is already biased, toward the lower-body and glute priority most of our clients carry. Or run it as a specialisation day, moving volume from a muscle held at maintenance to the one that needs it.

Watch for

Filling the day with whatever felt thin rather than running the arithmetic first. A fifth day is either an exposure fix or a specialisation slot. It is rarely both on the same client at the same time.

Terms on this page

exposures: The number of separate sessions in a week in which a muscle is trained.
