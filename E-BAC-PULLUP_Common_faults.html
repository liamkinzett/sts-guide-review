Common faults

Four common faults

# Grip that escapes the demand

sub: Fingertip or thumb-over grip. Also straps

Why it happens: A full grip is hard work, and straps or a loose grip take that work away. The pull-up carries a grip demand on purpose.

Do this: Full grip. Knuckles above the bar, thumb wrapped. No straps.

# Elbow off its line

sub: Grip too wide on a chin-up

Why it happens: A palms-facing grip set wide forces the elbow off the line it needs to travel along.

Do this: Narrow it. Shoulder width or narrower on a chin-up.

# Position degrading across the set

sub: Technique breaks down under fatigue

Why it happens: The trunk and shoulder-blade position is the hardest part to hold, and it goes first when she tires.

Do this: End the set. When form breaks, the set is over.

# Body English

sub: Swing. The feet travel behind the body at the bottom

Why it happens: Feet behind the body unload the core and give up the spinal position the shoulder blade needs. It is a bottom-position failure, not a pulling one.

Do this: The gymnast pull-up cues, in order. Feet never behind the body, feet actively in front, then contract the quads.
