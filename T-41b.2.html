Inheriting a client · T-41b.2

Change these immediately: anything that breaks a rule with no deviation

Some things do not wait for a month of observation. Everything else does.

Change now

A compound programmed below 1 RIR. Compounds stop at 1 (RIR), with no deviation. A tested single at the end of a strength phase is the one thing this does not fire on.

Loaded forward flexion or explosive spinal twisting in known vertebral osteoporosis. No deviation. Mobility and stretching owns the contraindication.

Any exercise STS does not program. The STS exercise list decides what may be prescribed.

Anything she has reported pain on that has not been triaged. Pain rules and when to refer owns her before anything else does.

A weight written in kilos rather than a selection method. Effort and intensity writes a method, never a number.

Everything else waits.

Why it works. The distinction is between a program you would have written differently and a program that breaks a rule. Only the second is urgent. Treating the first as urgent is how a handover damages a client relationship.

A worked sort. Her inherited program has a barbell row taken to failure and a split you would never have used. The hip thrust has its load written as a number of kilos. The row and the kilos change now. The split waits, and it may never change at all.

Terms on this page

RIR: Reps in reserve. How many more reps she could have done when the set stopped. Every STS set is prescribed in it.
