Building a session · T-12.10

Trisets and giant sets are a fat-loss tool

Three or more exercises run back to back (giant set) generate whole-body, cardiovascular fatigue. It is out of all proportion to the stimulus they deliver to any one muscle. In a fat-loss phase that is the point. In a building block they are banned.

Why they fit fat loss. Metabolic output, the energy the session burns, is what you are buying. The job in a fat-loss phase is retention rather than growth. Assessing a new client sets out the paths.

The one exception in a building block. A genuinely advanced client with the localised recovery capacity to absorb that density. Almost nobody on a normal roster meets that description. A coach who thinks their client does should re-read Volume's rule on programming the least that works.

Watch for

Giant sets used to make a session feel hard. They will. That is not the same as making it productive. The cost lands on the next two sessions.

Terms on this page

giant set: Three or more exercises performed back to back with no rest between them. Three is a triset.
