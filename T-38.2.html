A missed session is not a session to make up

A missed session is not a session to make up. Zoom out to the month rather than the week.

Why it works. Making up a session compresses recovery to pay a debt that does not exist. The week's job was a stimulus, not a total. The stimulus is still delivered by what she did do.

A rotation makes this cheaper. Use A and B days that alternate. A three-day week reads A, B, A, and the following week starts on B. The pattern absorbs a missed session without anyone deciding anything. Whatever she missed comes round again on its own.

Deviate where the missed session carried something the week genuinely needs and the week still has room. That is rarer than it feels.

Making it up with a second session in the same day is the same error twice. Two sessions in a day are an athlete's tool, run only as a deliberate short overreach with the recovery already booked. Nothing licenses one for a general client to repay a missed day. Split design has the detail.

Watch for

A client apologising for a missed session, and a coach agreeing with the premise. The first principle of this library puts her life ahead of the schedule.

Exercise used to punish a missed session. Extra work as penance for a missed day, an eaten meal or a bad weigh-in is a compensation behaviour, not a programming choice. Body image and the referral line owns it.

Say it like this

You didn't fall behind. The week's job was done by the sessions you did, and the one you missed comes round again on its own.
