Diagnosing from video · T-33.2

Ask first, then look, then reconcile the two

Get her account before you form yours. Then watch the footage. Then put the two together. The reconciliation is the diagnostic step.

What you ask. Where does she feel it? Where does it stop? What does it feel like at the bottom? Then, and only then, press play.

The interesting case is when they disagree. Her report tells you where the limitation is expressed. The video tells you where it comes from. Those are frequently different places. She may feel it in one place because the fault is somewhere else, and neither source of information finds that on its own.

Why the order matters. Once you have a hypothesis from the footage, her answer stops being evidence and starts being confirmation. You will ask a leading question without noticing you did.

In this order

Ask. Where do you feel it, where does it stop, what is it like at the bottom.

Look. Watch the whole set, first rep to last.

Reconcile. Put her account against the footage. Where they disagree is where the answer usually is.

Say before you press play

Before I watch it, tell me where you feel that one.
