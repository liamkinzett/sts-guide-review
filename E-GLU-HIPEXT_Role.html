Glutes · E-GLU-HIPEXT#Role

What it's for

The 45 degree hip extension is STS's default loaded glute movement for the shortened position (shortened position). When the glute needs to be worked short and under load, this is where we start.

Why it pairs with the hinge rather than competing with it. The RDL loads the glutes at their longest (lengthened position), hips back and torso forward. This movement loads them at their shortest, locked out in a straight line. The two cover opposite ends of the same muscle's range.

A glute program built around the hinge and paired with this movement trains the whole muscle. One without the other leaves half the range untouched.

Terms on this page

shortened position: The muscle is working while it is bunched short. For the glute that is full hip extension, torso in a straight line with the legs.

lengthened position: The point in the rep where the working muscle is at its longest while it carries the load. It describes the muscle's length, not whether she feels a stretch.
