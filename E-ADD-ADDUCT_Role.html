Calves and adductors · E-ADD-ADDUCT#Role

What it's for

Hip adduction, bringing the leg in toward the midline (hip adduction), loads the inner-thigh muscles (adductors) directly. The adductors are one of three hip extensors STS wants you tracking, alongside the glutes and the hamstrings. Most lower-body technique arguments are really about which of the three a position is favouring.

They get loaded by accident all over the program. A sumo or wide stance pre-shortens the glutes. The hip work shifts toward the quads and adductors. A hack squat with the knees drifting inward becomes an adductor exercise rather than a quad one. A wide, toes-out leg press biases them. A low-bar squat runs out of depth where the adductors run out of stretch. Direct adduction work is how they get loaded on purpose instead of by accident.

She does not switch the adductors on by choice under a squat. They contract anyway. Direct work is how they get trained as prime movers rather than as stabilisers.

Compared

columns: Machine hip adduction | Copenhagen hold

What it is: Seated isolation, the pad holds her (externally stabilised), tension never leaves | Side-lying bodyweight hold, a partner holds the top leg

Use it for: The loadable version. The pause is written for it | No machine, or a left-against-right test

Needs: The machine | A partner

A second use for the side-lying position. A side-lying adductor squeeze is also run as a pre-squat check. Can she hold a pelvic position that lets her run force through the system? That is priming, not training, and Warming up owns it.

Placement. Isolation is allowed here. It is not the main course. It must never displace loadable compound work. Building a session owns the order.

Terms on this page

adductors: The muscles of the inner thigh that pull the leg in toward the midline. They also help extend the hip.

externally stabilised: The machine, bench or support holds her position so she does not have to.

hip adduction: Bringing the thigh in toward the midline of the body. The inner-thigh muscles do it. The adductor machine loads it.
