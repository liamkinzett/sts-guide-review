Chest · E-CHE-FLY#Substitutions

Swaps

Pec deck and cable pec fly both fill the fly slot. Whichever the floor has will serve, and there is no third rung below them. They load opposite ends, so when you swap one for the other you have changed which end she trains. Where it's hardest has the two ends.

When the fly is not available

# Equipment missing

sub: The pec deck or the cable station is taken

Swap to: Pec deck; Cable pec fly

Why: One for the other and back again. Know which end you have moved to.

# The fly itself is provocative

sub: Pain, not a missing machine

Why: The fly is more often the destination of an injury swap than its origin. It is the named warm-up before a provocative press, and a decline arm path is comfortable at the shoulder. Where the fly itself is provocative, tighten the active range before dropping the movement. Then Region by region, shoulder.

# Home

sub: No gym access

Why: STS doesn't coach home training, so there is no home swap to give.

Not a swap: the dumbbell fly. STS rules it out rather than tiering it. No tension at the top. A poor resistance profile through the arc. Extreme shear at the shoulder.
