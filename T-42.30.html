The coaching relationship · T-42.30

A complaint about the package is usually a boundary being tested

A complaint that you are underdelivering on the package is usually a boundary being tested, not a service failure being reported. Treat it as the first before you treat it as the second. This is a judgement call.

What this topic owns and what it does not. The package itself, what it includes, what it costs, what she is entitled to, is a contract matter and sits outside coaching doctrine. What this page owns is the conversation when terms she already agreed to get relitigated as a personal complaint.

The shape of the conversation is the one you already use for a boundary. Restate what was actually agreed. Say plainly that the line is yours to hold. Expect the same result as any re-asserted boundary: she takes ownership of having pushed on it. Do it plainly, not apologetically, the same way you redirect a message to the right platform.

Where it stops being this conversation. Where the true answer is that the package genuinely does not cover what she now needs, this is no longer a boundary conversation. It is a contract change. Take it to the head coach before you promise anything beyond what was sold.

Watch for

Adding to the package to end the conversation. You have committed STS to something you do not own. Restate the terms first.
