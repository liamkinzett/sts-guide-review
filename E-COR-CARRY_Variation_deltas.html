Core · E-COR-CARRY#Variation deltas

Variations

Pick the version by what you want her trunk to resist. The two carries load the trunk from different directions.

Compared

columns: Suitcase carry | Front-loaded carry

Load: In one hand, at her side | Held at the front

The trunk resists: Being pulled sideways toward the load (lateral flexion) | Being folded forward, a flexion moment

Where it appears: The strength system's trunk slot, and the cue on Cues | The pain ladder on What it's for, with a gentle flexion moment to resist

That is the whole difference, read off where the load sits. There is no other rule for choosing between them.

Remember where the carry stands. STS does not yet write a carry into a client's program. This page is for coaching one when it appears in the strength system's lower session or in a client's existing training.

Terms on this page

lateral flexion: Bending the trunk sideways, ear toward hip.
