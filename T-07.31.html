The phases · T-07.31

Periodisation buys strength and structure, not more muscle

Organising training into blocks does not grow more muscle than steady progressive overload. That organising is periodisation (periodisation). It buys maximal strength, and it buys you a structure.

The condition is the whole rule. Periodised and non-periodised programs grow the same muscle when total weekly volume, load and effort are matched. Muscle growth is governed by the volume she accumulates and the tension her muscles work under, not by how the calendar is cut up.

What periodisation does buy. Maximal strength develops better with it. Strength and skill owns strength as its own goal.

This does not license abandoning the phase system. The phases exist because a structure makes volume and effort easier to hold. That is exactly the condition above.

Watch for

A client who drifts on volume or effort without a structure has not run that comparison. She is not getting the same growth as a structured block. She is getting less, because the volume and effort were never matched.

What it licenses you to say to her

The blocks are there to keep your work consistent. The calendar itself does not grow muscle. The work does.

Terms on this page

periodisation: Organising training into planned blocks, each with its own job, rather than running one program without change.
