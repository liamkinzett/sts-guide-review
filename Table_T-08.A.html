The weekly bands, and where each one is decided

These are the bands her weekly hard sets sit inside. Each is decided in the topic named beside it. This page gathers them so you only ever open one place.

How to read it. Find her training age and her phase. Her band is the one that fits both. An intermediate in a hypertrophy block sits at 15 to 20 on the muscles she wants to grow. The same client in a cut drops to 10 to 15.

A deload has no figure of its own. She is cut to one or two working sets per movement. Her load and her reps in reserve (RIR) do not move. A deload is a reduction in sets, not a new number to look up. That is why nothing in this table changes for one.
