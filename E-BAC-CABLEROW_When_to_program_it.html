When to use it

The problem you notice. Her free-weight row ends when her low back gives out, not when her back does. Or she is compensating through the arm on a chest-supported row and you cannot get her shoulder blade to move. The seated cable row is the row she can drive closest to true failure, because her legs act as an anchor.

Declare the bias first. Every row below is either a lat row or an upper-back row. Choosing without declaring is the reason backs fail to grow. A row run without a declared bias is not a neutral row, it is an accidental one.

Reach for it when

# Her free-weight row ends when her low back gives out, and output is what the slot is for

sub: The set stops with her standing up out of the hinge

Confirm it: Watch the last reps of her barbell or dumbbell row. If the hinge fails before the back does, the low back is the limiter.

What it gets her: A row she can drive closer to true failure. The legs act as an anchor, which is why this is a send-it slot rather than a teaching one.

# You have declared an upper-back bias and want the rhomboids specifically

sub: The mid-back between the shoulder blades

Confirm it: The bias is upper back, and the traps are taking the standard-height row.

What it gets her: Rhomboids biased further than a standard-height row reaches. Raise the seat and pull low to high with the elbows drawn back on an upward angle.

# She is advanced, underdeveloped in the mid-back, and still compensating through the arm on a chest-supported row

sub: The pad is hiding the fault rather than fixing it

Confirm it: On video her arm bends and straightens while the shoulder blade barely moves. She has trained long enough for the habit to be set.

What it gets her: Shoulder-blade movement she cannot avoid. On the bench-supported single-arm version the arm is carried more overhead, and that makes the blade move. It is the chest-supported row's next version.

# Her shoulder will not tolerate overhead work and you have already scaled the pulldown

sub: The rung below the pulldown

Confirm it: The scaled single-arm pulldown still provokes the shoulder.

What it gets her: The pattern kept, one rung down. The single arm cable high row sits below the pulldown and above arm isolation.

The bench-supported version is a teaching tool first

For several weeks it is a poor stimulus per rep, until the variables stop going wrong. Do not count it as a loadable row while it is being learned. She needs another row that carries the load in the meantime.

Novices are not the target. It is for advanced lifters whose compensations are set.

Setup. Sit tall, hinged from the hips, feet braced. Choose the handle before the load: two separate handles, never the close-grip V-bar. Setup has the full list.
