When she presents with… · DOC:Section 92 · Where to look when a client presents with something

Where to look, by what she says

Find the line that sounds like what she said. Open that topic and read its What to do procedure before you act.

She does not say a topic name. She says *my knee hurts* or *the numbers stopped moving*. This table is keyed the way she talks.

Watch for

A heading is not an answer. Acting on the routing without opening the procedure skips the four steps that keep her inside her scope.

The procedure decides, not this row. Each What to do block names its own rules in the order it uses them. That is the only order that means anything.
