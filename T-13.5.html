Choosing exercises · T-13.5

Movements her own trunk has to hold are earned, not assigned

Priming runs on movements where the machine or a support holds her position (externally stabilised). Coming out of priming, she earns the more demanding movements by showing she can hold position under load.

Simple free weights are always available. A dumbbell curl, a lateral raise, a dumbbell Romanian deadlift. Low complexity. The load limits itself, because she cannot pick up more than she can control.

What is earned is the complex, heavily loadable work. The work her own trunk has to hold (internally stabilised). Barbell squats, barbell hinges, standing pressing. These reward stability and punish its absence.

The test. Take a set near its limit and ask what ended it. If the muscle could not produce another rep, she is ready. If her trunk gave way, her balance went or the pattern fell apart, the limiter is not the muscle. A client who cannot yet hold position under load does not get a movement that requires it. The answer is not the same movement lighter. Split design uses the same test to decide when she moves to the next split.

Watch for

Assigning a barbell version because she has been training a while. Time served is not the criterion.

Terms on this page

externally stabilised: The machine, bench or support holds her position so she does not have to.

internally stabilised: Her own trunk and bracing hold the position under load.
