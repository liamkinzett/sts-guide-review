What the intake form asks about her cycle

A new client's intake form asks about her fuelling and her menstrual regularity. It does not ask what phase of her cycle she is in.

Why the question is narrower than it sounds. STS does not program from cycle phase at all. Nothing measured shows her strength moving by phase, and a calendar cannot map onto her cycle anyway. A form question that collects her phase or her last-period date would be collecting data you have no use for, so it is left off.

What the form asks instead. Menstrual regularity, whether she bleeds, and her contraception status are part of the under-fuelling screen every client gets. That is a different question from cycle phase. Missed or irregular periods are one of the clearest signs a client is not eating enough for what she trains. That applies regardless of her cycle phase.

Why the form also asks about contraception. A bleed on the pill is a withdrawal bleed, not a period, as the next section covers. It does not tell you her own cycle is working. Without knowing her contraception status, you cannot tell which kind of bleed her answer describes. A regular bleed off the pill can reassure you. The same bleed on the pill cannot.

The form itself lives in Assessing a new client. It is screening for a safety issue, not scheduling her training around a phase.

How to ask it. The way you would ask about any sensitive health item: briefly, and without follow-up questions that turn it into an interrogation. The warning against turning symptom tracking into a survey applies here too.

Say it like this

I ask this of every client, not because I'm going to plan around your cycle. I don't, it doesn't hold up. I ask because how regular your periods are tells me something about whether you're fuelling enough, and that's something I do want to know.
