The coaching relationship · T-42.40

A client who cannot train still has a coaching relationship

A client who cannot train for weeks still has a coaching relationship running. Keep the channel and the check-in going even though the content has nothing to report.

Why. Nothing about one platform or the check-in's own channel is conditional on there being training data to discuss. The structure exists for the relationship, not only for the log. Letting it lapse teaches her the same lesson as answering on the wrong platform: that the structure was never really load-bearing.

What the check-in holds instead.

Different content, same schedule

# What she is doing instead

sub: Rehab, walking, nothing

Ask: What the weeks actually look like. Where the time she trained has gone.

# How the layoff is actually going

sub: Six weeks is a long time to be quiet

Ask: Sleep, mood, how she feels about the return. The things the form asks every other week.

# When the return conversation should start

sub: Before the clearance, not after it

Ask: What clearance she is waiting on and from whom. Returning to training owns the return itself.

What this page does not own. The training side of the return is Returning to training. This page owns making sure the relationship does not go quiet by default because its usual subject has paused.

Watch for

"Check in when you're back." That is six quiet weeks, and quiet has two meanings. Keep the day.
