The coaching relationship · T-42.13

Plan the trip before she takes it. Ask three questions

Plan the trip before she takes it. Ask three questions. This is the usual choice, and the reason to plan rather than absorb is below.

Ask, in this order

# How long is she away

sub: Read against where she sits in the phase

Why: A week in an early block and a week in the last block of a phase are different losses. The phases owns where she is.

# What will she be doing

sub: Beach, work, family

Why: It decides how much of the week is recoverable and what training, if any, fits.

# Who is she with

sub: Named alongside the other two

Why: Company decides food, sleep and whether a session is even possible.

The trip is stress, not a training gap. The week becomes a deload (deload) by circumstance. Get the body moving, and treat the week as the lighter one.

Why plan it rather than absorb it. Unplanned, you are always working from a deficit afterwards, because nobody accounted for the week. Planned, it is just a pothole in the roadmap and the same plan continues.

In transit, coach only what she controls. The worked example is choosing the flight that lands her sleep on the destination's clock, rather than fighting the clock afterwards.

What this page does not cover. Tracking food on holiday belongs to the Nutrition Manual. Training around life owns a holiday week she did not plan as a deload. Motivation and adherence owns pre-framing the return so the trip does not start a spiral. Her check-in during the trip has its own page: the deadline flexes by a day, once.

Watch for

Hearing about the trip in the check-in after it. Ask at intake when her next trip is, and put the three questions in the diary a fortnight before.

Terms on this page

deload: A planned easier week that lets fatigue clear while the muscle and the skill stay.
