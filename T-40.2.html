Writing program instructions · T-40.2

Training content only, written to her alone

Program notes carry training. Not food, not steps, not cardio prescription, not lifestyle commentary. And they are written to her, one to one, never as though to a group.

This is a standing STS instruction on client-facing program notes, and it has two halves.

The content half. A program note carries training content only. Food, steps and cardio each have their own place in her coaching. A program note is not that place, however useful the reminder feels.

The voice half. The note is written to her. It is never copied from another client's program, and never phrased as though it were addressed to a group. Even where every client on your roster needs the same reminder, each one gets it written to her.

Compared

columns: Group-voiced | Written to her

Sounds like: "Everyone, keep the lowering controlled this block" | "Your lowering sped up on the last two reps last week. Slow it down, that's the one thing"

Where it came from: Pasted from the last client's program | Written from what you saw in her footage

What she reads: A notice | A coach who watched her

Why it works. A note that could have been written to anyone reads as though it was. The moment she notices that, every other note loses its weight.

Watch for

A note that has survived three clients because it was good the first time. It was good the first time.

Food, steps or cardio creeping into a program note. They are not training content and they do not belong there.
