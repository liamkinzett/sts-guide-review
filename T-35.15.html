Pain rules and when to refer · T-35.15

Challenge a stale ban. Never lift a medical restriction

You may challenge a stale ban. You never lift a medical restriction. Everything on the previous page licenses the challenge. Nothing licenses the override.

Compared

columns: You may | You may not

The question: Ask which of two things it is. A structural limitation, or an old instruction | Tell her the restriction no longer applies

The test: Test-load the movement and read what happens, through the bucket method and command before demand | Describe a clinician's reasoning as outdated to her

The rebuild: Rebuild towards it through the ladder from a hold to the full movement (Returning to training) | Treat a resolved issue as self-evidently resolved

The referral: Send her back to a CURRENT clinician where a genuine clinical question remains | Rely on the years-old note, in either direction

The last row is the whole rule. A stale restriction is not answered by a stale note. It is answered by somebody looking at her now. Under your scope, that person is not you.
