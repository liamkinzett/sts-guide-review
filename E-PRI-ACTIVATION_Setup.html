Setup

Set her up in this order

Landmine: half-kneeling by default (half-kneeling) (landmine). Standing or split stance where the bar alone is too heavy. Shortening the lever is the first adjustment, not a lighter implement. A cable is the preferred implement wherever one is available.

Leg under the base of support. Toes, knees and hips in line. What you are after is the pelvis and the rib cage sitting square to each other. Check the pelvis before you accept that line. The back knee is the canary. A lifter can pass the line by rotating the pelvis to buy the range.

Serratus press: face down, forearms flat as in a push-up position. STS doesn't program it. The setup is here so you recognise it.

Scaps set before load in every drill. Depression before retraction (depression) (retraction). No shrug toward the ears.

Terms on this page

half-kneeling: One knee down, the other foot forward, with the hips square and the torso tall.

landmine: A barbell with one end anchored to the floor or in a sleeve, so the free end moves in an arc.

depression: The shoulder blade pulled down, away from the ear. The opposite of a shrug.

retraction: The shoulder blade sliding back toward the spine, so the two blades pinch together.
