Choosing exercises · T-13.9

There is no rotation percentage. The reasons decide

You will find rotation targets published as percentages of a program. We do not use one. The omission is deliberate.

Rotate the exercises that have a reason and keep the ones that do not. The three reasons are loadability, joint strain and variety. If that comes to two exercises in a block, it comes to two. If she is moving from a build into a cut, the loadability of everything has to fall. Then it may come to most of the program, and that is also correct.

Why a percentage would be worse. It becomes a quota. A coach at 30% who was aiming for 50% goes looking for things to change. That means rotating movements that were working, which pays the relearning cost for nothing. The target would generate exactly the behaviour the previous rule forbids.

Do not set a number of weeks an exercise stays, either. Judge it by whether a reason has appeared, not by the calendar.

Watch for

A phase change where nothing rotates at all. That is possible and it is rare. If a build has become a cut and not one exercise needed its loadability altered, check that the phase actually changed.
