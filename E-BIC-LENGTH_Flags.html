Biceps · E-BIC-LENGTH#Flags

Watch for

  • The preacher curl is never the first movement of a session. It is heaviest close to the fully lengthened stretch, which puts the biceps in a precarious position for a tear under high load. The stretch-loaded bottom is a tear risk on cold tissue. It goes second or third at the earliest. This is a hard sequencing rule, not a preference. Building a session owns the order.
  • The rule stands whatever the implement. The tear risk is argued from the stretch-loaded bottom, not from where the load peaks. It does not hang on whether she is using a cable, a bar or a dumbbell.
  • High biceps tear risk at full lockout on the preacher, for the same reason. Never lock out.
  • Extreme shoulder extension loads the shoulder joint in shear where the position is not stabilised (shoulder extension). That applies to the behind-the-back and face-away cable versions.
  • Do not raise the pulley high for the lengthened cable curl. It over-challenges the elbow tendon at full stretch and then drops tension to zero at the top. Maximum lengthened tension is bought at the elbow's expense. Keep the pulley at or below a right angle to the hand.
  • A cable that does not line up through the elbow joint is the named mechanism for medial elbow pain here. It is the same mechanism as on the standing curl. Both alignment checks gate the load. They are not optional.
  • Accumulated forearm flexion fatigue and the wrist-extension counter-measure live on Standing curl's Watch for. They apply here.
  • Pregnancy, the return after a baby and under-fuelling are The female body's. This page adds no modification of its own for them.
