Returning to training · T-37.3

Command before demand: reproducibility is the qualification

Establish that a tissue can hold up to command first. The qualification to advance is reproducibility of the stimulus rep to rep, never one successful repetition.

Every tissue has a demand placed on it and a command it can answer with. Command is what she decides: the position, the speed, the load. Demand is what the world does to the tissue once it is out of her control.

The moment she leaves the ground, control passes to the demand side. You no longer decide what the tissue meets. That is why the ladder ends where it does, rather than beginning there.

Reproducibility is the test. If she produces the same rep, at the same tempo and in the same position, rep after rep, the tissue has met that command. It can be asked for more. One good rep tells you nothing about the next one.

Watch for

The reverse-band jump. It is the named worst offender, because nobody can predict how she will meet the ground. Keep it out of a return.

Chaos as a proxy for progress. Variability is not the same as readiness. An exercise that produces a different rep every time cannot tell you whether she is improving.

Asymmetry under unpredictability. A squat that is thirty percent on one side and seventy on the other is multiplied by chaos, not trained away by it.

Say it like this

Every rep has to look like the last one before we go faster. Same speed, same position, every time. That's what tells us you're ready for the next step.
