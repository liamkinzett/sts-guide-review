Position degrading across the set on the deadlift: the deficit pull

A lifter who rounds through the lower back on the pull, with strong abs and weak erectors, gets the deficit deadlift. Light enough that technique does not break. Then rigid-torso core work, after bracing has been taught, not instead of it.

Position degrading across the set, on the deadlift

# She loses back tightness and rounds through the lower back

sub: Accessory: the most specific of three

What you see: Strong abs and obliques, weak erectors. The trunk rounds as the set goes on.

Why it happens: The muscles that stabilise the trunk in this exact position are the weak link.

Do this: Three options work the lacking area. A back extension isolates the erectors. A good morning builds a rigid torso through hip flexion, which is more specific. A deficit deadlift, light enough to prevent the technical breakdown, builds the rigid torso through an extended range of the exact movement where it breaks. It is the most specific and so the best pick, especially with a competition close. Deadlift variations carries who gets one.

Exercises: 45 degree hip extension; Good morning

# Loss of trunk rigidity off the floor

sub: Accessory: rigid-torso core work

Why it happens: This is the stated root cause of a lagging lockout. Not a muscular weak point at the top; position lost at the bottom.

Do this: After she has learned to brace, and only if she is still losing position, train the lacking area and be specific. Exercises where the torso stays rigid: RKC plank, GHD sit up, ab wheel. Breathe big, brace hard and hold that position through the movement. Core has the pages.

# Time in the start position

sub: Accessory: the deficit height

Do this: A deficit of 1 to 2 inches and no higher. The purpose is to hold the starting position longer, not more range for its own sake. Higher changes the mechanics and defeats the purpose. Performed at a weight where technique holds, it carries over to pulls from the floor.

Watch for

A deficit pull for a deadlift that hurts off the floor. That is the wrong lever. Pull from blocks and progress down instead. The pain page in this topic and Pain rules and when to refer own it.
