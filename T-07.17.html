The phases · T-07.17

Deloads are reactive, never scheduled in advance

A deload happens when one of three triggers fires. It is never written into the calendar in advance. A deload (deload) is a planned easier week, and at STS only the sets come down.

The three triggers

# Consecutive weeks of regressing performance or volume

sub: Her numbers are going backwards, not just flat

What you see: Load or reps falling across more than one week, with technique holding.

# Life or illness forcing a pull-back

sub: The week is not going to happen as written

What you see: Illness, travel, work or family load that makes the full week impossible.

A holiday: An unplanned week off is information. If it lands where a deload was due, take it as the deload. If it does not, it is a missed week. Training around life has the rule.

Repeated illness: Getting sick for the third time in one block is a recovery signal, not bad luck. Run the stall list in Volume before you call it coincidence. Pain rules and when to refer covers it.

# The week after a test day

sub: The end of a strength phase

What you see: A genuine maximum has just been expressed. The week after it is deliberately easy.

Does she deload this week?

? Have her load or reps fallen across more than one week, with technique holding?

yes: Deload. Her numbers are regressing, not just flat.

? Has life or illness made the full week impossible?

yes: Deload. The week is not going to happen as written.

? Did she express a genuine maximum on a test day last week?

yes: Deload. The week after a test is deliberately easy.

= No trigger, no deload. She stays in the block.

No trigger, no deload. A client who is progressing cleanly in week four of a block stays in the block.

When a reference program schedules a deload

The programs in Peaking for a meet carry a deload at a fixed week, with the load dropped. Run the plate as written and record that you did. A planned deload inside a sourced twelve-week structure is not what this rule was written against.

Never import the plate's rhythm into a block that is not a plate. This rule stops a coach deloading a general hypertrophy client on the calendar instead of on her performance.

Why STS deloads on signals, not the calendar. Her own signals are more reliable than a calendar, because the calendar cannot see whether she is tired.

Which way the cost runs. A full week off costs a trained lifter strength. If a scheduled deload were free, we could run them regardless. It is not free. That argues for deloading when she needs it rather than on a schedule. It also argues for reducing her work rather than removing it, which is exactly what the next page does.

Do not schedule a deload to make her muscle "responsive" again. That idea (resensitisation) is not a reason to deload on the calendar. Deload on her signals.

Terms on this page

deload: A planned easier week that lets fatigue clear while the muscle and the skill stay. At STS only the number of sets comes down.

resensitisation: The idea that a period of lighter training makes muscle respond strongly to hard training again.
