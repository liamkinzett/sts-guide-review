Setup

Set her up in this order

Sit at the very edge of the bench before lying back. Otherwise the head meets the bar on the unrack.

Crown of the head hanging off the end of the bench, eyes well forward of the bar. This buys hip extension and lets the feet tuck back, which is what establishes the arch.

Feet slid back onto the balls of the feet, then heels pressed down for the whole set. They need not touch the floor. Constant pressure is the point.

Slide down until eye level sits just behind the bar, so the bar clears the hooks. For a novice, eyes directly under the bar is the safer landmark.

Shoulder blades pulled back and down before the unrack (retracted and depressed). They do not move again.

Rack height set so the hand-off is short. If she has to reach, the shoulders rise and the lock is gone before rep one. It cannot be recovered under load.

Breath in and held before the unrack. The brace is set before the bar moves.

Where the bar touches. The touch point is set from her own range, not from a landmark on her chest. Touching too low is what lifts the head off the bench: raise the touch point and keep her eyes under the bar at the unrack. If full contact hurts, stop half an inch short.

Terms on this page

retracted and depressed: The shoulder blades pulled back toward each other and down toward the back pockets. On the bench this position is set once before the unrack and held for the whole set.
