The coaching relationship · T-42.17

Not a training question and nothing here covers it? Escalate

When it is not a training question and nothing in the Library covers it, you escalate. You do not improvise. A coach with no instruction does the natural thing, which is to answer alone, in a message she cannot take back.

Why this rule exists. Questions about money, employment, another person's privacy or your own conduct will arrive. Every one of them. The alternative to this rule is a coach inventing STS policy on the spot.

The test has two parts, and both must be true before it fires.

Check

Is this something other than a training question? Would a better coach with the same information answer it from training judgement? If the answer turns on money, on employment, on another person's privacy or on your own conduct, it is not a training question.

Does the Library hold nothing on it? Search it first. Where the Library says plainly that it does not cover the question, that is this rule firing, not an exemption from it.

yes: Stop. Take it to the head coach before you act. Not after.

no: It is a training question, or the answer is here. Coach it.

Why before, not after. A conduct question is usually decided by the first reply. There is rarely a second chance to have handled it differently.

What this rule does not do. It does not name who you escalate to beyond the head coach, and it does not name a timeframe. Pain rules and when to refer owns anything medical, and it forbids you to diagnose. Where that applies, it wins over this page.

Watch for

Answering because silence feels rude. A short holding reply is fine. "Let me come back to you on that" costs nothing. A policy invented in the moment costs a great deal.
