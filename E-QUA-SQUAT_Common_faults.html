Common faults

Eight common faults

# Rib flare

sub: Excessive arch through the spine, pelvis tipped forward (anterior pelvic tilt)

Why it happens: The ribs were never brought down before rep one, so the brace becomes an arch.

Do this: Ribs to hips before the rep. Check she is not the opposite case first. Rib flare has an opposite: the sternum stuck down. Cueing "chest up" at a client who has that version makes it worse.

# Butt wink (butt wink)

sub: The pelvis tucks under at depth

Why it happens: She has run out of hip range for that stance and bar position, or lost her brace, or was cued too upright.

Do this: Run the page What to do about a butt wink. You are finding out what her wink responds to, not judging it against a rule.

# Knee travelling inside the foot (knee valgus)

sub: The knees cave

Why it happens: The standard is knees pointing the same way as the toes. Some inward travel under a heavy squat is normal. Past a small band, the hip has stopped steering the knee.

Do this: Cue "spread the floor" and watch whether it changes. Judge it against the standard, not against a knee pointing straight ahead.

# Momentum at the turnaround

sub: Dive-bombing the descent

Why it happens: She drops to depth to bounce out of it, or fatigue has taken the control away.

Do this: Control the lowering half (eccentric).

# Chasing range she does not own

sub: The go-lighter-and-squat-deeper trap

Why it happens: She has been told depth is the goal, so she drops the load to reach it.

Do this: Depth is what she owns under load. Do not buy depth by dropping load.

# Hips shoot up

sub: She has to throw the hips back to start the rep. The hips open before the knees bend

Why it happens: This is a setup fault, not a cueing one. The stance, the brace or the start position is wrong, so the only way to move is to throw the hips.

Do this: Fix the setup and the start position. Do not spend a cue on it.

# Bar in the wrong place on the back

sub: Chasing a lower bar position for a heavier lift. Bar resting on the bone at the base of the neck

Why it happens: She has copied a low-bar position without the shelf to hold it.

Do this: Reset the bar on the trap shelf.

# Bar off the midline

sub: The bar drifts forward, shoulders reaching forward over it

Why it happens: The lats are off, so nothing holds the bar to her. The same fault appears on the Romanian deadlift.

Do this: Reset the midfoot position and re-tighten the lat brace before touching the cue list.

Terms on this page

anterior pelvic tilt: The pelvis tipped forward, so the lower back arches more.

butt wink: At the bottom of the squat her pelvis tucks under and her lower back rounds. It usually shows up when she runs out of hip range or loses her brace.

knee valgus: Her knees fall inwards, inside the line of her toes, as she squats or stands up.

eccentric: The lowering half of a rep, where the muscle lengthens under load.
