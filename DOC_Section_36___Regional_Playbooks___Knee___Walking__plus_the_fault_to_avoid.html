Walking, and the limp she must not practise

Walking is unbelievably understated in knee rehabilitation. The first day is spent deliberately moving, to prevent the learned experience of pain and the loss of range.

The fault: practising a limp. With knee pain the default is to strike the ground with the knee fully straight rather than soft. Every step rehearses it.

The fix: have the good knee teach the bad knee how to walk. She walks as slowly as she needs to in order to copy the good side exactly.

Say it like this

Walk as slowly as you need to. I want the sore knee doing exactly what the good knee does, soft at the knee when your foot lands. Speed comes later. Copying comes first.
