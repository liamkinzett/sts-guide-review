The coaching relationship · T-42.3

Get her agreement to hard conversations at intake

Get her agreement to be told uncomfortable things at intake, before you need it. This is the single most useful thing in this topic and it costs one question.

At intake

It's my job to make sure that you accomplish whatever you want to accomplish. That means at some point we may have to have uncomfortable conversations. They will be about behaviours or actions that are directly damaging your ability to reach your goal. Are you okay with having those conversations?

The consequence of a no is stated as plainly as the yes. If it is a no, okay, no sweat. She can find someone else. A client who will not agree to hear hard things has told you the coaching cannot work.

Why it works. You have made an agreement that you are allowed to have these conversations. People do not like to break agreements they have made. Every harder conversation in this topic, a regain, a goal that looks borrowed, data that disagrees with her report, is licensed by this one question.

The same day-one timing runs through the topic. The conduct floor goes down at the start. The response window is set at the start. Set at the start, each is a policy. Set in month four, each is an accusation.

Watch for

Skipping it because she is enthusiastic at intake. The agreement is for the month she is not.
