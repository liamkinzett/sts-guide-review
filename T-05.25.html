The female body · T-05.25

Body image moments a coach will hear

Three body-image moments recur in coaching, and none has a script. You build the sentence from the mechanism and the redirect below.

What she says, and the move

# "I liked myself better when I was underweight"

sub: The smaller body became her identity

Why it happens: Other people reinforced the small body until it became who she was. Stepping out of it feels like losing herself, and she is afraid to.

Do this: Separate the thought from her. Do not argue with the thought. Then redirect to strength and fuelling rather than to the mirror. Become performance driven, focus on strength, fuel your body, and you get far more out of yourself.

# Baggy clothes and an apology for how she looks, every check-in video

sub: Concealing and pre-emptively criticising her body is avoidance

Why it happens: It sits on the same axis as the referral signals: social or situational avoidance, and a script rehearsed in advance.

Do this: Notice it. Do not confront it as an accusation. Treat repetition, not one video, as the trigger to ask a plain question rather than to diagnose one. The under-fuelling page's behavioural row already asks you to watch for this: body image distortion and a drive for thinness.

# Food language darkening: earned, deserve, punish

sub: The line is drawn at fear and rigidity, not at the food

Why it happens: Wanting change becomes a problem when there are emotions around every food choice. The referral-grade signs are avoidance, panic around specific foods, and withholding food to make up for something eaten earlier.

Do this: Model the language rather than correct hers. Fun foods, not good or bad foods. Where post-diet hunger is the driver, de-shame it physiologically. Prolonged restriction changes appetite regulation, and hunger hormones can stay elevated for up to a year after a diet. Offer that as "it's not on you, it's not your fault".

Why this is a judgement call. No single sentence fits all three women. What you get instead is the mechanism and the redirect. The mechanism explains why the sentence needs to land the way it does. The redirect supplies where to land it. Body image and the referral line has more on the conversation itself.

Verdict

Avoidance, panic around foods and compensatory restriction are referred once they cross from language into behaviour.

This page governs the conversation before that point. Pain rules and when to refer holds the routing.

Watch for

Treating the second row's read as diagnostic. It is a read-across from the referral signals, not a named pattern in its own right. A coach who is wrong about it has lost nothing by asking a plain question.
