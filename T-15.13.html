Tempo and pauses · T-15.13

No held stretch pauses for beginners, fewer in a deep deficit

Beginners do not get held stretch pauses. In a deep deficit they are scaled back, coming off the heaviest and most stretch-loaded movements and staying on lighter externally stabilised work.

Beginners do not get them. Loaded stretched positions are out for novices. They lack the tendon and ligament strength and the movement control to hold them. Standard controlled full range work is their safety net. The pause is added once the pattern is owned. That is the same trigger Her first program uses when it puts execution ahead of everything else after week one.

In a deep deficit they are scaled back rather than removed. Training at long muscle lengths and at short ones preserve tissue equally when she is eating below maintenance (deficit). But training at long lengths costs significantly more metabolic stress, damage and fatigue. In a compromised recovery environment that raises injury risk without buying anything back. Pull the held pause off the heaviest and most stretch-loaded movements. Leave it on lighter work where the machine holds her position (externally stabilised).

Why the two triggers differ. Rest and intensity techniques removes intensifiers, techniques like a drop set that add work to the end of a set, in any fat-loss phase. This page fires only in a deep deficit. Both are about what a depleted client can absorb rather than about what works. An intensifier is pure added fatigue, so removing it costs nothing. A held stretch pause is buying execution quality, so pulling it early costs something real. The cheaper thing goes first.

What it looks like. Twelve weeks into a hard cut, her Romanian deadlift loses its stretch pause and goes to `3010`. Her seated hamstring curl keeps its `3110`. The machine holds her, the load is lighter, and the pause is still buying clean execution.

Terms on this page

deficit: Eating fewer calories than she burns, so that she loses body fat.

externally stabilised: The machine, bench or support holds her position so she does not have to.
