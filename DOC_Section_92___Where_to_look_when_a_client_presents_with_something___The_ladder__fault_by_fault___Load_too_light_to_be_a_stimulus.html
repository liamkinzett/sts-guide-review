Load too light to be a stimulus

The movement is being done as a warm-up when it is programmed as work.

Load it.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Trunk flexion

sub: Too light, performed as a warm-up when it is programmed as work

Do this: Load it

# Hip extension (45 degree and GHD)

sub: The movement is performed as a warm-up when it is programmed as work

Do this: Load it

There is no set cue written for this fault. Say the fix in plain words, one thing this set.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Trunk flexion

Say: Curl your spine.

Why: Try this one first. It is the fault and the fix in three words.

When: Trunk flexion

Say: Breathe out all of your air as you slowly flex your spine while contracting your abdominals.

When: Trunk flexion

Say: Almost peeling the rib cage up, or pulling the rib cage apart, or lifting it up like a car hood from the pelvis. Getting a lot of stretch through my rectus abdominis.

Why: GHD sit up only.

When: Trunk flexion

Say: Breathe big and brace hard through the stomach like you're about to block a punch.

Why: For bracing on the GHD.

When: Hip extension (45 degree and GHD)

Say: When I get to this neutral position, that's when I'm done.

Why: The stopping rule, said as a cue. Try this one first.

When: Hip extension (45 degree and GHD)

Say: Squeeze your glutes hard in the fully shortened position.
