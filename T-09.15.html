AMRAP is a measurement, never on a compound

An as-many-reps-as-possible set is a measurement tool, used on a machine with a question attached. Never on a compound.

That set is an AMRAP (AMRAP). It is not a training method and it is not a way to finish a session.

When it earns a place. Finding out where a client actually is on an isolation movement, usually because her reported effort and her logbook disagree. Confirming a rep range is right after an equipment change. Both are diagnostic uses with a question attached.

Why it teaches her. A leg press AMRAP is how a client learns what her own reps in reserve means. She cannot judge a subjective target until she has felt the actual limit under control. A machine, a diagnostic question, never a compound.

Why never on a compound. An AMRAP is a set taken to failure by definition. That puts it directly against the compound floor. There is no version of it that respects the floor.

Watch for

The AMRAP that appears in the last slot of a session because the session felt short. That is not a measurement. It is fatigue with a name on it, and it is charged to the next session.

Terms on this page

AMRAP: As many reps as possible. She keeps going until she cannot complete another clean rep, so it is a set to failure by definition.
