Grip that escapes the demand

Her hands take the easy way out: fingertips, heel of the palm, thumb over.

Whole hand, full grip.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Pull-up

sub: Fingertip or thumb-over grip. Also using straps, which removes the grip demand the movement carries

Do this: Full grip

# Machine and cable chest press

sub: Hands working like little typewriters

Do this: Fix the grip

# Push-up

sub: Pressing through the heel of the palm

Do this: Whole hand

# Leg raise

sub: Fingertip grip on a hanging raise. Grip ends the set before the abs do

Do this: Straps. The captain's chair

# Romanian deadlift

sub: Pronated dumbbell hold, shoulders float. Presents as upper-back achiness

Do this: Neutral grip. Straps when grip limits the set

Cues written for this fault.

Cues

When: Pull-up · external cue

Say: knuckles above the bar

Why: Fixes the fingertip or thumb-over grip as a checkable position rather than as an instruction to try harder.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Pull-up

Say: pulling your shoulder blades into your pockets

Why: Sets lat tension in the hang before anything moves. It goes first because most of the named faults are failures of the bottom position rather than of the pull.

When: Pull-up

Say: driving your elbows down toward your pockets

Why: Initiates the pull from the lat rather than from the arm.

When: Pull-up

Say: gymnast pull-up

Why: The whole-body standard in one image. Use it when the fault is swing rather than position.

When: Pull-up

Say: the feet never come behind the body

Why: The rule underneath the gymnast image. Reach for it when that image does not land.

When: Pull-up

Say: feet actively in front of your body

Why: As against feet merely dangling.

When: Pull-up

Say: Contract your quads

Why: Contracting it creates hip flexion and holds the pelvic position the other three cues describe from outside.
