Pain rules and when to refer · T-35.12

Do not diet her through a recovery phase

In a recovery phase, she does not diet. It is not the goal. The reflex to cut calories when training output drops is backwards.

Why. Her resting energy use (basal metabolic rate) rises while she is injured or after surgery. The margin varies with whether the injury is bone or soft tissue. Treat that as a direction rather than a number. It means eating the same amount may already be maintenance. Cutting from there is a deficit she is trying to heal in.

This is the rule the intake question exists for. The intake asks whether she is in a deficit. A client who gets injured and immediately cuts food has moved two variables in the wrong direction at once. Then she blames the slow recovery on the injury.

The general form: nutrition follows the training goal, not the reverse. In a deficit you train to preserve.

When to deviate. A competition date is a real constraint and this rule does not outrank it. Where a client is in a prep and injured, say plainly which one is being compromised. Training around life owns that call.

Watch for

The long rehabilitation judged by whether flare-ups have stopped. In a year-long case they never stopped. The interval between them got shorter. That was the progress.

Terms on this page

basal metabolic rate: The energy her body burns at rest just to run itself. It rises while she heals, so eating the same amount may already be maintenance.
