Pelvis escaping to the back leg

The front leg unloads into the back leg, so a front-leg movement quietly stops being one.

Keep it a front-leg movement: kickstand contact only, or narrow the stance.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Lunge

sub: The front leg unloads into the reaching leg

Do this: Keep it a front-leg movement

# Romanian deadlift

sub: B-stance: back heel drops, weight shifts back. It has quietly become bilateral

Do this: Kickstand contact only

# Split squat

sub: The pelvis pushes away toward the back leg. The frontal plane (frontal plane) is never accessed

Do this: Narrow the stance. Contralateral load

There is no set cue written for this fault. Say the fix in plain words, one thing this set.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Lunge

Say: Keep three points of contact on your foot

Why: The tripod, held through the whole stride. Try this one first.

When: Lunge

Say: Be mindful of your walking path. Maintain a straight line as much as possible, avoiding lateral deviation of your strides.

Why: Walking version only.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Forward shin angle.

Why: The master cue for anyone who lives in their heels.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Fall forward before you push back.

Why: If the centre of mass is already back, there is nowhere left to travel.

When: Romanian deadlift

Say: Keep the main thing the main thing. It is a front leg exercise.

  • Lunge. Keep the centre of mass balanced over the base of support. Use it as a felt reference rather than as a shape. Where she is chasing balance the load or the variation is wrong rather than the cue.
