What she will ask you · T-47.3

"My physio says my glutes aren't firing"

It is an access problem. It was never an activation problem. If she is alive, her muscles are activated. Muscles are on.

The replacement word. Where someone says she is lacking activation, what she is lacking is access. Her glutes are not failing to switch on. They are not able to produce force well in the position she is in, because she does not have variability there.

Why this is a coaching answer, not just a correction. Access and variability are trainable, through position and range. "Activation" points at nothing you can program. Access points at a position she cannot yet get into or produce force from, and that is a session.

Compared

columns: "Not firing" | "No access"

What it claims: The muscle is switched off | The muscle cannot produce force well here

What you can do about it: Nothing programmable | Train the position and the range

Where it goes: A glute activation drill before every session | The glute pages, and Fault to fix for the position she loses

On the wider idea that imbalances cause injury. Predicting injuries is next to impossible. Success does leave clues, but the clue is what she can and cannot do, not a label on a muscle.

Say it like this

Your glutes are firing, they're alive. What's happening is you can't get into the position where they can push hard yet. That's something we train.

Watch for

A glute activation circuit as the answer. It agrees with her premise. Train the position instead.
