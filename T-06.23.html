Assessing a new client · T-06.23

An unrealistic timeline is tested and named before you build

A goal that is unrealistic is one of the three exceptions to building exactly what she asked for. Test the timeline, then say the result plainly before you build.

How you test it. A short runway to a first competition is the usual case. The phases shows you how to project a cut. You work out how much needs to come off, and how many weeks that takes at a safe rate. Work back from her date to where she actually is now. Then say what you found, rather than building toward a date you already have reason to doubt.

Say it like this

I've worked the timeline back from where you actually are right now. That date doesn't get you there safely. I'd rather tell you that today than build toward it and have both of us find out later. Here's what the real number looks like, and here's what we can do with the time you do have.

This page covers the conversation only. How a competition prep itself is run, bodybuilding peak week included, is a different subject.
