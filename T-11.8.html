Split design · T-11.8

Weight the week toward lower body, glutes first

Her week is weighted toward lower body work, and glutes are prioritised inside it. That is where most of our clients' goals sit. The split reflects the goal rather than a template's idea of balance.

What it looks like. More lower-body exposures (exposures) than upper. Glutes trained fresh, at the front of a lower day or on a day where nothing has already fatigued them. Upper body work is still there. It is not where most of the week's recovery is spent.

Where this does not apply. A strength pathway wants equal exposure across the foundational patterns. There the patterns themselves are the thing being built, so balance is correct for a reason that has nothing to do with aesthetics. Assessing a new client explains the three paths.

Why it works. Recovery is finite and every hard set spends some of it. Spending it on the muscles she asked you to change is the whole point of having a goal.

Terms on this page

exposures: The number of separate sessions in a week in which a muscle is trained.
