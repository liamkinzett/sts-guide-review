What the scale hides after menopause

After menopause, fat mass typically rises and lean mass falls, often at the same time. Her weight can sit completely still while her body changes underneath.

Why both move at once. The same falling oestrogen that costs her fast-twitch fibre, covered on the last page, also favours storing fat. It makes holding onto muscle harder too. One trend pushes her weight up. The other pushes it down. They can cancel on the scale while both are real underneath it.

Her body mass index (BMI) sits still with it. Neither number can see the change.

A communication rule as much as a physiological one. Her weight may not have moved in a year. Meanwhile she may be losing the thing training exists to protect. The number she is watching will never tell her.

Why it works. Her goal comes first. A goal expressed as a scale number is one this physiology makes unmeasurable. The measurement has to change before the goal does. Strength on the bar and how her clothes fit are the measures that still work.

Watch for

Congratulating stable weight. In this population it is not information.

Say it like this

The scale is going to be the least useful number you have from here. Strength on the bar and how your clothes fit will tell you far more.

Terms on this page

BMI: Body mass index. Her weight divided by her height squared. It says nothing about how much of that weight is muscle or fat.
