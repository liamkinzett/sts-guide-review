Undeclared scapular bias

She is rowing one bias while you prescribed another, or the rear delt work has become a retraction.

Declare which you are training before you judge the rep.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Seated cable row

sub: Shoulders held stiff on an upper-back-biased row. Hard pinching at the finish on a lat-biased one.

Do this: Declare the bias

# Free-weight row

sub: Rowing the wrong bias for the prescription

Do this: Declare it

# Rear delt

sub: Retraction recruited instead of the rear delt

Do this: Declare what you are training

There is no set cue written for this fault. Say the fix in plain words, one thing this set.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Seated cable row

Say: use your legs as an anchor

Why: The setup cue that makes everything after it possible. It is also the reason this row can be driven closer to failure than a free-weight one.

When: Seated cable row

Say: top of the head to the ceiling

Why: A deliberate replacement for "chest up"

When: Seated cable row

Say: let your shoulders flow freely

Why: Upper-back bias only. Expanded as hang loose at the bottom and pinch at the top. Do not give this cue on a lat-biased row.

When: Seated cable row

Say: drive down

Why: Lat bias only. Biases shoulder extension plus depression rather than pulling back. It is the direct fix for pulling back too far or for letting the shoulder roll forward.

When: Free-weight row

Say: chest over your knees

Why: Sets the parallel torso the whole exercise depends on. It is checkable from the side in a single frame.

When: Free-weight row

Say: toes on the ground like grip in the ground

Why: The direct fix for the heel fault. It produces whole-foot pressure rather than a conscious weight shift.
