She never failed. Fix the word before you fix the feeling

She never failed. Fix the word in your own vocabulary before you fix the feeling in hers.

Never

You managed to complete eight reps of our six to eight range.

Never tell a client she failed at eight reps in a six to eight set. She completed eight reps of the six to eight, or six to ten, range. The rewiring happens in your own vocabulary first.

The word for a bad week is trajectory.

A bad week

Just because we're going the wrong direction a little bit doesn't mean we're changing directions.

The bound that makes it true

You're not going to lose progress because you had a bad session or a bad week of sessions.

Where the rule stops. It cannot be used to excuse a real problem. Weeks of bad sessions is a different conversation. So is a month. Adherence as a signal owns what to do when the pattern is the finding, and it changes the program. One week does not.

Why it works. "Failed" ends the sentence. "Trajectory" has a direction, and directions can be corrected. She takes the word you use and uses it on herself.

Watch for

Using trajectory language to wave away a month. A month is a pattern. Read it as one.
