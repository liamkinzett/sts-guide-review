The conjugate phases

A conjugate peak narrows the funnel: variety, then specific, then the tested lift itself. She goes heavy every week without ever frying herself on one movement.

The three conjugate phases

# Weeks 1 to 4, variety

sub: Working up to a top single or triple (max effort) on variations rather than the tested lifts

What she is doing: She gets the benefit of going heavy every week without wearing herself out on the same movement. The exercise rotates every one to two weeks so she never stalls. Speed work is light and explosive, training her ability to produce force fast.

# Weeks 5 to 8, specific

sub: The variations move closer to the real thing

What she is doing: Chains on the squat, close-grip bench, block pulls. Speed work percentages climb. You are narrowing the funnel.

# Weeks 9 to 12, realisation

sub: Max effort becomes the tested lift itself

What she is doing: Speed work gets cut back sharply because it is no longer the priority. Recovery is. Week 11 is a full opener rehearsal (opener) and week 12 is a taper into the test.

Terms on this page

max effort: Working up to the heaviest weight she can lift that day on the chosen lift, for a single or a triple.

opener: Her first attempt on the platform. Light enough to be certain, heavy enough to set up the attempts that follow.
