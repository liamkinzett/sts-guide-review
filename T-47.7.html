What she will ask you · T-47.7

"Will lifting make me bulky?"

The bulky fear arrives first. Answer it at intake, before it costs you the session. It is the first objection most women bring, and it is handled by education before training starts.

What she is afraid of. That lifting, and higher volumes especially, will put on lots of muscle mass she does not want. You explain and educate her through it, at the start.

What is fixed here is the order, not the argument. It comes first. It is handled before the first session. A coach who waits for it to surface in week four has already lost sessions to it, in half-effort sets and skipped lifts.

Do not reach for a hormone number. The female body owns female physiology and is where you go for the reasons. A wrong number in your mouth on this topic is worse than no number. Give her the position and point her to what you can stand behind.

Where it connects. "High reps tone, low reps bulk" is the same fear with a rep count attached, and its own page inverts it. The thick-waist fear has its own page too. Where the question is about how she feels about her body rather than what bodies do, Body image and the referral line takes over.

At intake

Before we start, I want to talk about the getting-bulky worry, because nearly everyone has it. I'd rather answer it now than have it sitting in the back of your mind during every set.

Watch for

A confident hormone ratio. If you cannot source the figure, do not say it. Point to The female body.
