Warming up · T-16.10

A top single potentiates, on the tested lifts only

This is for the strength path only: a client training for squat, bench and deadlift numbers. Finish her ramp with one clean single at roughly 90% of the day's working top. Then go to the working sets. The hypertrophy path, a client training for muscle, never uses one.

Key figure

value: 90%

unit: of the day's top set, one clean single

note: Strength path only. Of today's top set, never of her one rep max.

Ninety percent of the day's top set. Not ninety percent of her one rep max. Her one rep max (1RM) is the most she can lift once. Those are very different weights. The difference decides whether this is a warm-up or a near-maximal single in front of every working set. If her top set today is 100kg, the potentiator is 90kg. A lifter whose top set is 300kg takes a comfortable 270kg single first.

It is the last step of the ramp, not an addition to it. The mechanism is the same neural rehearsal that makes a specific warm-up worth doing above 80%. One heavy, clean rep wakes the nervous system up so the working sets move better. Because it is a single, it costs almost nothing.

Hypertrophy path does not use this. There is nothing to potentiate for. A single at 90% on a day built around sets of ten is a heavy single wearing a warm-up's clothes.

Watch for

A grinding top single. It is a potentiator. It has to be clean and fast. If she has to fight it, the working weight for the day is already wrong.

Terms on this page

potentiator: A single heavy, clean rep done before the working sets so the working sets move better. It wakes the nervous system up without tiring her.

ramp: The run of feeder sets that climbs toward the first working set.

1RM: The heaviest weight she can lift once with good form.
