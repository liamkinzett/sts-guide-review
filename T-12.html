What goes where inside one workout

Ordering the session

  • Priority goes where she is freshestT-12.1
  • With nothing competing for the front, compounds go firstT-12.2
  • When the joint is the limit, an isolation can go firstT-12.3
  • When she cannot hold position, stabiliser work goes firstT-12.4
  • Heavy, low-rep work before light, high-rep workT-12.5

How many exercises, and which

  • Exercise count is calculated, not looked upT-12.6
  • Cover the muscle with compounds, then one or two isolationsT-12.7
  • Regional growth justifies coverage, not session bloatT-12.16

Pressing and pulling in one session

  • A session that benches must restore upward rotationT-12.19
  • Do not put heavy lat rowing immediately before pressingT-12.20

Supersets, giant sets and the warm-up

  • A same-muscle superset pairs two different resistance profilesT-12.9
  • Trisets and giant sets are a fat-loss toolT-12.10
  • Every session opens with a warm-upT-12.11
