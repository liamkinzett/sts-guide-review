Quads · E-QUA-SQUAT#Execution

How to do it

Lower under control. Never dive-bomb. A dropped descent whips the bar and unsettles the spine. She starts the rep by pushing outward through the feet. That turns the thigh bones out and opens the hips, which gives the pelvis room to sink between the knees while it stays neutral. The knees point the same way as the toes for the whole rep. She looks straight ahead throughout.

On the knees. Caving in (knee valgus) and forcing the knees out are both power leaks. That is why "knees out" is not a cue STS gives as a direct instruction. Cueing explains the objections.

Depth is as deep as she can go while holding a neutral pelvis, and therefore a neutral spine. For some clients that is genuinely full depth. It is a position she owns, not a target you set. Low bar sits naturally shallower. The hips travel further back, the inner thighs reach the end of their stretch and the pelvis runs out of rotation. Forcing past that point produces a butt wink (butt wink).

Stand up with intent, the way she stands out of a chair. Uncontrolled speed on the way up breaks the position down. Tempo and pauses owns the count on both halves of the rep.

Terms on this page

knee valgus: Her knees fall inwards, inside the line of her toes, as she squats or stands up.

butt wink: At the bottom of the squat her pelvis tucks under and her lower back rounds. It usually shows up when she runs out of hip range or loses her brace.
