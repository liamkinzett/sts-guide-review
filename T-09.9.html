Effort and intensity · T-09.9

The per-phase effort numbers live in The phases

This topic owns the compound floor and the rep ranges. Every number that changes with her phase is looked up in The phases.

What you needWhere it lives
Reps in reserve across a hypertrophy blockThe phases, the hypertrophy block
The reps-in-reserve floor in a deficitThe phases, the deficit
Reps in reserve in a holding phaseThe phases, the holding phase
Reps in reserve through a deloadThe phases, the deload. It holds at the block's number rather than easing off
Percentages in a strength blockThe phases, the strength block. Percentages take over only there

Why we do it this way. One number, one home. It is the same discipline Volume uses. The moment two pages state the same figure they start to drift apart, and a coach quotes the stale one.

There is no table that converts reps in reserve to a percentage. Priming establishes the weights a strength block is built from. A written percentage is checked by how the sets move, on video and in the logbook, not by arithmetic.

Watch for

Quoting a deload as "easier". Through a deload her reps in reserve holds at the block's number. The sets come down. The effort does not.
