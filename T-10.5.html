Progression · T-10.5

She earns the right to load up

Hitting the top of the rep band makes her eligible for more weight. It does not make her entitled to it. The current working weight has to be moving with quality first.

If the reps that got her to the top of the band were rushed, shortened or ugly, the answer is not more weight. It is the same weight performed properly. Effort and intensity already ends a set where quality goes.

Why it works. Without this rule the ladder becomes a ratchet. "Top of the band, add load" runs every week regardless of how the reps looked. The logbook climbs while execution rots underneath it. Her first program warns about the same failure in a beginner's first month. Here it arrives later and is harder to reverse, because by then the numbers look like a track record.

Watch for

A client who reaches the top of the band suspiciously fast on one movement. Range of motion is the usual culprit, and it is the hardest thing to see without video. She is not progressing faster on that lift. She is doing less of it.

Say it like this

You've earned the jump on those. Before we make it, I want to see the current weight looking the same on rep eight as it does on rep one. That's the standard. Once it's there the weight goes up.
