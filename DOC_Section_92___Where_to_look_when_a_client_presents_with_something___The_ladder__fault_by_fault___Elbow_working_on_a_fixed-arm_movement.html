Elbow working on a fixed-arm movement

Her elbow bends and straightens on a movement where it should stay fixed.

Fix the elbow angle.

Where it shows, and the fix

# Straight-arm pulldown

sub: Dynamically bending and straightening the elbows

Do this: Fix the elbow angle

# Lateral raise

sub: Bending the elbows mid-rep

Do this: Fix the elbow angle

# Triceps pushdown

sub: Turning a straight-arm pulldown into a pushdown

Do this: Fix the elbow angle

There is no set cue written for this fault. Say the fix in plain words, one thing this set.

Other cues on those exercises. Not about this fault. Use them when the same set needs them.

Cues

When: Straight-arm pulldown

Say: sweep in a long arc

Why: States the whole movement as a shape. The shape is what separates this from a pressdown.

When: Straight-arm pulldown

Say: step back for tension

Why: The first fix for the most common setup fault and it is checkable from across the room.

When: Straight-arm pulldown

Say: maintain long, soft arms

Why: Targets the elbow-bending fault that converts the exercise into a triceps movement.

When: Straight-arm pulldown

Say: Contract your glutes the whole time to keep your back aligned in a neutral spine.

Why: Dumbbell version only, where the bridge is what stops the lumbar arching under an overhead load.

When: Lateral raise

Say: Lead with the elbows.

Why: The elbow sets the path and the hand follows it. This is the Execution standard "The elbow leads. The hand does not outrun it"

When: Lateral raise

Say: Reach wide, not up.

Why: The initiation cue.
