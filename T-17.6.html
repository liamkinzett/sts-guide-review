Steps and cardio · T-17.6

Prescribe the number of sessions, not just a weekly total

Cardio is prescribed as a weekly total with a stated number of sessions. No session is under fifteen minutes. The total lets her divide it around her life. The session count keeps the dose what you meant.

Why it works. A weekly total alone has a hole in it. Sixty minutes divided as she sees fit can become five twelve-minute sessions. That is a different dose from two thirty minute ones. Session length and weekly frequency each carry their own cost. A total constrains only one of them.

A worked example. Write "60 minutes of cycling a week as 2 sessions of 30, on the days you don't train legs". Not "60 minutes of cardio a week".

This rule is about the number being decided, not about it being low. Three sessions is the baseline and six can be correct. What is never correct is not knowing which she is doing. A coach reading her weekly total cannot tell.

Watch for

The client following your prescription exactly and progressing worse than one who ignored it. Count her sessions before you conclude anything about her.
