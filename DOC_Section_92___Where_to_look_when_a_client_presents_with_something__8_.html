Red flags

title: Pain ceilings, for diagnosed and cleared clients ONLY

Resistance training: pain does not exceed 5 out of 10

These ceilings apply only to a client a clinician has diagnosed and cleared. Undiagnosed pain has no ceiling to train under; it is routed, not dosed.

Verdict

Seven out of ten or higher, reported consistently: she is referred.

Terms on this page

eccentric: The lowering half of a rep, where the muscle lengthens under load.

concentric: The lifting half of a rep, where the muscle shortens under load.

scapular: To do with the shoulder blade, the scapula.

abduction: A limb travelling out and away from the midline of the body: the arm in a lateral raise, or the thigh moving out to the side.
