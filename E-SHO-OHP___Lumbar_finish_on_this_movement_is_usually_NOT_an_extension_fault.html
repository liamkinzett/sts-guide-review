Shoulders · E-SHO-OHP#🔴 Lumbar finish on this movement is usually NOT an extension fault

A lumbar finish here is usually a shoulder fault

When her lower back arches to finish an overhead press, the fault is usually at the shoulder, not the trunk. The shoulder blade will not turn upward far enough to get the arm overhead (upward rotation). The lower back supplies the rest.

Screening it as a bracing problem sends you to the wrong joint. You will cue the brace harder, she will hold it, and the arch will come back the moment the shoulder runs out of range again. This is the clearest example in the whole Library of a fault appearing where it is not caused.

What to do instead. Check how far her arm travels overhead with the rib cage held still (shoulder flexion). If the range is short, that is the problem to work on, and the press moves down a rung until she owns the position. See Swaps.

Say when you see it

Your back is arching because your shoulder has run out of room, not because you're not bracing. We'll give the shoulder more room first.

Terms on this page

upward rotation: The shoulder blade turning so its outer corner rises and the socket tilts upward. It is what lets the arm travel overhead.

shoulder flexion: The arm travelling forward and up in front of the body, all the way to overhead.
