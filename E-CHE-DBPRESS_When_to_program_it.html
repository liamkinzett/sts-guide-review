When to use it

The problem you notice. The barbell hurts her shoulder and dumbbells do not. Or her only pressing is a locked-down barbell bench and nothing in the week lets the shoulder blade move. Or she has a nagging shoulder, or a flat chest that will not develop. The dumbbell bench press answers each of these inside the same pattern.

Reach for it when

# The barbell hurts her shoulder and dumbbells do not

sub: Same pattern, same session, different equipment

Confirm it: Ask her to press a barbell and then a pair of dumbbells at a comparable load. The barbell hurts, the dumbbells feel fine.

What it gets her: Pressing kept in the program instead of cut out of it. If a barbell bench press hurts but dumbbells feel fine, you do not have to switch the pattern altogether. Change the equipment and keep the press.

# Her main pressing is a locked-down barbell bench

sub: Nothing in the week lets the shoulder blade move

Confirm it: Read the program. Every press pins the blades.

What it gets her: The shoulder-blade freedom the bench takes away, given back inside the same pattern. The neutral-grip dumbbell press is the named accessory for that job.

# She has nagging shoulder pain, or a flat and less-developed chest

sub: Bottoming out hands the load to the joint

Confirm it: Watch the bottom of her rep. The dumbbells touch the chest and the shoulders roll forward.

What it gets her: Tension held on the pec rather than handed to the joint. Stop the descent half an inch off the chest instead of bottoming out.

# She cannot yet press overhead

sub: You need the rung before overhead

Confirm it: An overhead press provokes the shoulder or the position is not there yet.

What it gets her: A shoulder walked up to overhead rather than thrown at it. Start with a cable press in a more neutral plane. Then maybe move to a 30 degree incline dumbbell press. From the 30 degree incline, maybe move to the 45. Eventually she is getting into something overhead.

The bench angle is not itself a reason

Choose the angle for the client and record it numerically. Which angle suits which client is set from her structure, and What it's for explains how. No angle is a reason to pick this exercise on its own.

Setup. Angle chosen and recorded, ribs stacked, blades set down, head on the bench. Setup has the list.
