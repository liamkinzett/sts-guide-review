Base too wide or too long, sumo: shin vertical

Narrow it until her shin is perpendicular to the ground with the knee directly above the foot. That is the test, not a measurement. Shins on the bar, not an inch away.

Base too wide or too long on the sumo pull

# Sumo stance too wide for the knee to sit over the foot

sub: Setup

What you see: The knee cannot sit over the foot, or the stance is wider than her hips can open.

Do this: Bring the feet in until the shin (tibia) is perpendicular to the ground and the knee sits directly above the foot. Open the hips to get there.

# Shins set an inch off the bar

sub: Setup

Why it happens: She has carried the conventional gap across.

Do this: Shins on the bar. In sumo the knees track outwards rather than forwards, so the shin never pushes the bar away from her centre of mass.

Exercises: Sumo deadlift

Terms on this page

tibia: The shin bone. Vertical in the sumo setup, so the knee sits over the foot.
