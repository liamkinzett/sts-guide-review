Practising a lift · T-48.3

Practise fresh, because fatigue changes what she is practising

Skill work goes early in the session, while she is fresh. Fatigue takes her out of the state where practice works.

Practice under fatigue is practice of something else. A tired lifter cannot run the movement at the level it needs to be learned. She still gets the reps in, but the reps she is grooving are the tired version of the lift. Do enough of them and the tired version is what she learns.

This is about her nervous system, not her muscles. Building a session covers where each exercise sits in the order, and Warming up covers getting her ready. What this rule adds is the reason skill work leads: her brain has to be fresh to practise, not just her legs.

What it looks like in the program. The lift she is learning is the first working movement of the day, after the warm-up. Heavy accessory work and anything that leaves her gasping comes after it, never before.

Watch for

Skill work parked at the end because "it's light so it can go anywhere". Light is not the same as fresh. A light lift practised tired is still the tired version.

Reading a late-session breakdown as a skill problem. If the pattern is clean early and falls apart late, she knows the lift and cannot express it tired. That is fatigue, and it needs less, not more.
