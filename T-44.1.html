Motivation and adherence · T-44.1

Your job is building her self-awareness, not supplying motivation

Your job is building her self-awareness. It is not supplying her motivation. Creating an environment that fosters self-awareness faster is the number one goal of a coach.

Your programming is not what sets you apart. You can give her a template push, pull, legs split and a template meal plan and it will work. You are the one who has to get her to show up every single day.

Read those two together. The plan is a commodity. What she can see about herself is not. Building that is the work a template cannot do.

What building self-awareness looks like in practice. She notices that her worst weeks follow her worst sleep, because you asked about sleep every time it happened. She notices that she skips the same session every time, because you named the pattern instead of counting the misses. She learns to see the thing before you point at it. That is the product.

Why it works. Motivation you supply runs out when you stop supplying it. Awareness she builds is hers, and it keeps working after the coaching ends. The coaching relationship holds the same standard: judge the coaching by what she can do without you.

Watch for

Reaching for a pep talk. Encouragement feels like coaching and does nothing structural. Ask what she noticed instead.
